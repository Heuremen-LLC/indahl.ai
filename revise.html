Division: HERMES

How far does a first estimate move?

The Bureau of Labor Statistics states that the 90-percent confidence interval for the monthly change in total nonfarm employment is about plus or minus 122,000. This page counts how often, over the 120 most recent qualifying months, the first estimate was later moved by more than that: by the third release, and by the most recent value after the annual benchmark. The values are the first, second, third and most recent releases kept by the Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia's Real-Time Data Set.

The plan was committed before any revision was counted. The stated interval describes sampling error, and a later release also holds late reports and non-sampling error, so this is a count of how often the first estimate moved by more than the stated margin, not a test of the agency's method. The stated figure is today's and is applied to every month. There is no price, rate or forecast on this page.
