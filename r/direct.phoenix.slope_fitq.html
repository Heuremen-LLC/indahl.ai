Where it is printed

QIA bill QIA-2026-001 (PDF)

Two runs on the same pairs, fixed before each job. Beat beside a superposed neighbour: median 12.9 kHz, slope on stated 0.19 [0.01, 0.38]. Shift, neighbour 0 then 1: 0.49 [0.45, 0.53] of stated, slope -0.06 [-0.63, 0.32] (0.19 [0.04, 0.37] without qubit 41’s two slipped fits)

Paper, A Readout Is Not an Idle (PDF)
