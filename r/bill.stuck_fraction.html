What it is: Measured qubits stuck in 1 (median stuck fraction f) 0.4 to 1.1 % of shots across processors.

Where it is printed

QIA bill QIA-2026-001 (PDF)

Only shots where every measured neighbour was verified in its |0⟩ cluster on every readout (97 to 99 % of shots); a neighbour left in a level that reads as 0 is excluded by an X-flip check on marrakesh, to the extent the flip catches it; neighbours excited and misread as 0 (0.4 to 1.1 % of shots stuck in |1⟩) are not excluded as the whole cost
