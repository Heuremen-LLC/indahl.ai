The line the number is read from:

slope 0.19 [-0.0001, 0.37]; rho 0.39 [0.03, 0.67]; median 12.9 [11.7, 14.25]. The lower slope bound touches 0. Within-A fixed-effects slope 0.17 [-0.15, 0.57]; within-A concordance 10 of 17. Restricting to pairs with >=30 live taus: slope 0.095 (n=31); >=40: 0.097 (n=27). So "above 0" is not robust; "far below 1" is (upper bounds 0.37-0.57 in every variant).
