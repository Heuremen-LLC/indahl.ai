What it is: Every desk listed with a kit on indahl.ai/reproduce has a reproduce kit (reproduce/<desk>/ in the public repository) that recomputes its headline number and outcome label offline from the kit's own contents. ledger/ship.sh step 3 runs reproduce_kits.py gate, which reruns every kit in a clean folder and refuses the ship unless each prints its expected headline, that headline equals the one the live feed served when the kit was made, the desk's current live ledger value row covers it (same label, not a material change under live_headlines.py), and the live feed has not moved past it. Desks without a kit are listed on the page with the reason and what can be checked.
