What it is: LAUNCH headline: the number comes from https://indahl.ai/launch/live.json, field ["flown_watched"]. Refresh cadence 0.75 h, read stale after 1.125 h. A new value row is written only when the outcome flips. The plan states no rule and no width for the count of watched launches that have flown.
