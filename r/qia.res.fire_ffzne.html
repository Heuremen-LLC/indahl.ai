The number

“~6%” average deviation from ideal after FF-ZNE, for up to 50 qubit EfficientSU2 circuits on a 133 qubit IBM Quantum device (their abstract). Their released notebook names ibm_torino

Our run of their method on ibm_fez, 50 qubit circuit with r = 1: 0.922 (standard error 0.002), 7.8% from ideal (raw best layout 13.4%). Three different triples of the same five layouts give 0.922, 0.896 and 0.916, so the choice of layouts moves the answer by 0.026 (defined after the data). Across five layouts the IBM record score does not predict the delivered value linearly (chi-squared 152 on 3 degrees of freedom)

Our run on ibm_fez, 25 Oct 2026 (2 jobs)
