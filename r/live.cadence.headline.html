What it is: CADENCE headline: the number comes from https://indahl.ai/cadence/live.json, field ["most_overdue", 0, "age_days"]. Refresh cadence 0.25 h, read stale after 0.375 h. A new value row is written only under the plan's stated rule, read on the fields listed in the row. The headline is the series read furthest past its stated interval. It is BELOW by construction under the plan's rule (PLAN.md line 14, BELOW when the age is more than twice the stated interval) and has no width of its own. A value row is written when another series takes the top place. Its age grows by a day each day without a new row.
