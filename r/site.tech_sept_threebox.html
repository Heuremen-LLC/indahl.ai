The line the number is read from:

<p>24 September 2026, IBM ibm_marrakesh, replicated on ibm_kingston and ibm_fez. These runs have no equal-idle arm on untouched qubits and are not used for the readout claims of the paper. Two-qubit (spatial) and one-qubit sequential (temporal) correlations against a rotation angle, and a three-box weak measurement (third-box weak value &minus;0.506 &plusmn; 0.057). The temporal and spatial curves follow the same cos&thinsp;&theta;, as standard quantum mechanics predicts.</p>
