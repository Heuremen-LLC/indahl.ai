Do the published results stay what they say?

MLCommons publishes percentile scores for AI systems on its AILuminate Safety benchmark, with a Detailed Results report for each system. It states that the reference set is fixed so that adding systems does not shift the scores of those already tested. We read the results page and every report, compare the page with its own reports, and keep every read so that any later change to a published score shows up.

The page is checked against the reports: a disagreement is ABOVE when the lower end of the 95 percent interval is above 5 percent and CONSISTENT when the upper end is below 15 percent. Published scores kept: a system is changed when any of its 13 percentiles or its release month differs from the first value we stored. MLCommons states that the overall score weights all 12 categories equally.
