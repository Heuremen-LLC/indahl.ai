What CENSUS is

CENSUS sets what the U.S. Census Bureau states about the uncertainty of its first estimates beside what the record shows later. CENSUS is the name of this division of Heuremen LLC. It is not the U.S. Census Bureau and the Bureau does not endorse it.

The first desk reads the 90 percent interval Census states for each first print of new home sales, housing starts, housing completions and advance retail sales against the revised values that followed. Its page is CENSUS Revisions, and the newest reading is in /census/live.json.

The second desk sets the 2020 Census count of each state beside the Census Bureau's own Post-Enumeration Survey. Its page is CENSUS Coverage. It was run once and does not refresh.

The third desk sets the state counts of marriages and divorces reported to the CDC beside the Census Bureau's American Community Survey estimates. Its page is CENSUS Fidelity. It was run once and does not refresh.

The fourth desk sets each state's July 1 population estimate as an earlier Vintage gave it beside the later Vintages and the 2020 Census count. Its page is CENSUS Vintages. It was run once and does not refresh.

Sources: U.S. Census Bureau, Economic Indicators Time Series API and explanatory notes. Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, ALFRED archival series. This product uses the Census Bureau Data API but is not endorsed or certified by the Census Bureau.
