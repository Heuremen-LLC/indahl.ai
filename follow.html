Follow

Every way to follow indahl.ai

Every way to follow indahl.ai. None of them needs an account.

Email

The sign-up form at the foot of every page sends the weekly newsletter every Tuesday, news of new desks and new data, and an email when an outcome changes on the desks you pick. To pick desks, use the email updates page. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.

Notifications on this device

Your browser or phone can show a short notification when an outcome changes: the desk, the value before and now, and a link. Press the button to turn it on for every desk, or turn it on for one desk from that desk’s page. Your browser asks for permission first. On an iPhone or iPad, add indahl.ai to your Home Screen first, then open it from there.

Calendar

One calendar holds the weekly newsletter and the release dates the sources of our desks publish, each event with a link to the source’s own schedule page. Only dates the source states are listed.

  • Subscribe in your calendar app · calendar.ics

One desk at a time:

  • CONE: calendar.ics
  • FEDSEP: calendar.ics
  • SEASONAL: calendar.ics
  • STEO: calendar.ics

Feeds

Feeds work in any feed reader. Each one announces new entries through the public WebSub hub at pubsubhubbub.appspot.com, so a reader that supports it gets them without asking again and again.

  • New studies, desks and bench rounds: feed.xml (Atom) · feed.json (JSON Feed)
  • Corrections: corrections.xml
  • The weekly newsletter: newsletter/feed.xml
  • Site updates: changelog.xml
  • Every desk and organisation feed in one file, for one import into a feed reader: feeds.opml

Webhooks

A webhook sends a signed POST to your server, or a message to a Slack, Discord or Microsoft Teams channel, when an outcome changes. Register a webhook.

API and MCP

Every live measurement is readable as JSON at /api, and by agents through the read-only MCP server at https://indahl.ai/mcp.

What each of these keeps about you is on the privacy notice.
