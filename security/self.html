Our Own Records

The SECRECORDS checks, run on our domains

We run the checks of our SECRECORDS desk on our own domains, with the same code and the same outcome labels the desk prints for other domains. The desk reads two claims. A Strict-Transport-Security header that states preload is held against the preload list requirements (a max-age of at least 31,536,000 seconds and includeSubDomains). An SPF record is held against the limit of 10 DNS lookups. AGREE means the record can do what it states. DISAGREE means it cannot. NOT_COMPARABLE means there is no statement to read or the read failed.
