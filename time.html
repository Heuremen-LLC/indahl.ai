TIME · Stated Against Delivered

Why this number has no kit, and what can be checked.

Division: ATLAS

Public Time Servers, Stated Against Delivered

Each server below is asked ordinary time queries, and the table says when one did not answer. Every reply states its own error bound, a root dispersion plus half a root delay. We ask each server eight times per round from a clock disciplined by a GPS pulse, keep the fastest reply, and compare the measured offset with the server's own stated bound. The internet path adds an uncertainty of half its round trip, so a bound smaller than that cannot be confirmed or refused, and the outcome says cannot tell. Rules were committed before the first query.

Consistent: the offset and path uncertainty fit inside the stated bound. Above: the server is further from true time than it states. Cannot tell, with its reason beside it: too wide to resolve when the path uncertainty reaches both a perfect clock and the stated bound, straddles edge when the offset and path uncertainty overlap the bound, no interval formable when the reading cannot be computed. The path uncertainty is half the round trip of the kept reply, a worst-case bound and not a probability interval. The outcome rule is the plan with its addendum of 12 November 2026. A server that returns nothing is a failed read, listed apart and outside every count of outcomes. No ranking.
