Public Time Servers, Stated Against Delivered

Each server below answers ordinary time queries. Every reply states its own error bound, a root dispersion plus half a root delay. We ask each server eight times per round from a clock disciplined by a GPS pulse, keep the fastest reply, and compare the measured offset with the server's own stated bound. The internet path adds an uncertainty of half its round trip, so a bound smaller than that cannot be confirmed or refused, and the outcome says cannot tell.
