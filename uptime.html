Status Pages Against Independent Probes

Each vendor below publishes a status page that says whether its systems are operational. Every round, three of our probe seats, two on a home network and one on a cloud network, make one plain request to a public endpoint the vendor serves. A request fails if it times out, cannot connect or returns a server error. A disagreement is a round where the page said operational and at least two seats failed. Two control targets with no status page set the floor for chance failures. Rules were committed before the first round.
