RANK · US News Education Rankings Against the Public Record

Division: OY

US News education rankings, set against the public record

US News & World Report publishes rankings of schools and colleges. For each list it states the indicators it uses and the weight of each. This desk records the top of each list in the current edition, with the list and the stated method linked. It then sets each figure US News prints for a school against the same figure in the public record, such as a state report card, the federal school and college data, or the school’s own published report, and checks whether the order follows from the stated method where the method can be worked again from public inputs.

Every row below reads check in progress. The checks are added list by list as each is done. Each figure will read match, mismatch or cannot tell, with the year used on each side. The rules were fixed and committed before any figure was compared.

The lists were read on 2026-11-10. A rank is printed as US News prints it. A tie is marked, and a tie at the tenth place is kept whole, so a list can hold more than ten schools. Only the rank, the stated method and the figures to be checked are shown here. The full lists are on the US News pages linked below.

What comes next

For high schools, each printed figure is set against the state report card for the same school and year and against the federal Common Core of Data. Schools in the list that hold a National Blue Ribbon award get the award year, its category and its stated criterion, checked against the state assessment data for the years the award cites. For colleges, the stated inputs are set against IPEDS, the College Scorecard and each school’s own Common Data Set. For graduate schools, they are set against the published ABA disclosures, AAMC figures where public, and each school’s own reports. Where a stated input is a survey score, purchased data or a model US News fits, the order cannot be worked again from public inputs, and the page will say which input is missing. Public, documented cases where a school’s submitted data was later corrected and its rank changed will be listed from primary sources only.

Rankings, list names and method text belong to US News & World Report and are quoted here only as far as each check needs, with links. Named here and want to answer a row? Each row has an answer link. US News can answer here: Respond.
