Division: OY

US News education rankings, set against the public record

US NewsFollow & World Report publishes rankings of schools and colleges. For each list it states the indicators it uses and the weight of each. This desk records the top of each list in the current edition, with the list and the stated method linked. It then sets each figure US News prints for a school against the same figure in the public record, such as a state report card, the federal school and college data, or the school’s own published report, and checks whether the order follows from the stated method where the method can be worked again from public inputs.

Each figure reads match, mismatch or cannot tell, with the year used on each side. Match means the public figure, rounded to the precision US News prints, is the same. Cannot tell is given with its reason, such as a year that is not printed, a figure the state suppresses, or a figure not found. The rules were fixed and committed before any figure was compared. Every list on this page is checked: high schools, National Universities, National Liberal Arts Colleges, the law, business, engineering, education and medical school lists, and the New Jersey lists. On the graduate school lists, figures US News shows only to paying subscribers were not read and read cannot tell (paid tier, not read).

Figures checked so far: High schools 57 match, 0 mismatch, 18 cannot tell, National Universities 8 match, 0 mismatch, 69 cannot tell, National Liberal Arts Colleges 6 match, 0 mismatch, 64 cannot tell, Law schools 44 match, 0 mismatch, 11 cannot tell, Business schools 24 match, 0 mismatch, 56 cannot tell, Engineering schools 1 match, 0 mismatch, 49 cannot tell, Education schools 0 match, 0 mismatch, 72 cannot tell, Medical schools (Tier 1) 1 match, 1 mismatch, 94 cannot tell, New Jersey high schools 70 match, 0 mismatch, 10 cannot tell, New Jersey elementary and middle schools 0 match, 0 mismatch, 48 cannot tell, New Jersey colleges 4 match, 1 mismatch, 79 cannot tell.

The lists were read on 2026-11-10. A rank is printed as US News prints it. A tie is marked, and a tie at the tenth place is kept whole, so a list can hold more than ten schools. Only the rank, the stated method and the figures to be checked are shown here. The full lists are on the US News pages linked below.
