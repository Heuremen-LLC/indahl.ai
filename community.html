Community

Heuremen LLC is a member of MLCommons.

Below are the communities around the data we index, grouped by the division that reads them. They are listed for reference and are not endorsements. Each group publishes and decides its own work.

QIA: quantum hardware

  • Qiskit. The open-source software IBM processorsFollow are programmed with, and the community around the circuits QIA runs.
  • arXiv quant-ph. Where most of the quantum studies QIA indexes are posted.
  • Zenodo. Where our data and code releases are archived, each with its own DOI.

IRIS: language models

  • MLCommons. Publishes the AILuminate safety results and the MLPerf results our desks read.
  • Hugging Face. Hosts the model weights, licenses and file sizes the WEIGHTS, HFLICENSE and HFSIZE desks read.
  • OpenRouter. Publishes the host and endpoint data behind the HOSTS desk.
  • arXiv cs.CL. Where most of the language-model studies IRIS indexes are posted.
  • PyPI. The package index the PKGINSTALL and PKGLICENSE desks read.

HERMES: public claims

  • Measurement Lab. Open internet measurement data and the community around it.
  • ClinicalTrials.gov. The registry the TRIALS desk reads.
  • Bureau of Transportation Statistics. The flight data behind the AIRLINE desk.
  • EPA Air Quality System and PurpleAir. The two air-quality networks the AIRQ desk compares.

ATLAS: infrastructure

  • OpenStreetMap. The mapped data centers on the ATLAS map.
  • Epoch AI. Research and data on AI data centers.
  • U.S. Energy Information Administration. The electricity, plant and carbon figures on the ATLAS pages.
  • NTP Pool. The public time servers behind the TIME desk.

Our own

  • Heuremen-LLC on GitHub. Code, plans and data for every desk.
  • indahl on Hugging Face. The datasets behind the QIA, IRIS, OY, ATLAS and MODEL pages, the run data and the YO model.
