Public DNS Filters, Checked Against Domain Alerts

Free public resolvers advertise blocking of malware and phishing domains. Two public lists of bad domains, the Phishing.Database active domain list and the CERT Polska domain list for blocking, are read about every 17 minutes. Each domain that is new since the last read is asked about at first sight, then after 1 hour, 6 hours and 24 hours, through three resolvers that state no filtering (Cloudflare 1.1.1.1, Quad9 9.9.9.10 and Google 8.8.8.8) and through each filter. A filter blocks a domain when it refuses while the unfiltered resolvers answer. Only DNS questions are asked. No listed host is contacted and nothing is downloaded. The rules were committed before the first question.
