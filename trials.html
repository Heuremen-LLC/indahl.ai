Trials that started and were later stopped

The first block counts interventional studies that started in the last two years and are now withdrawn, terminated or suspended, by sponsor class, with the reason the registry holds. Two tests check the registry against its own rules: a reason for every stopped study, and no enrolled participants for a withdrawn one. The plan for this block was added to the plan file before the read, and ten records the first test counted were read in full on their own record and are listed beside the tests. A stopped study is not a fault, and a young cohort has had less time to stop.

Results due against results posted

United States law (FDAAA 801) asks the responsible party of an applicable clinical trial to submit results to ClinicalTrials.gov within 12 months of the primary completion date, unless a delay is certified. This page reads the public registry for interventional trials of FDA-regulated drugs and devices with an actual primary completion date from 2018 to the end of the month 14 months before the read, so each record is at least 13 months past completion, and counts which records have results posted.

The registry does not store whether a trial is an applicable clinical trial or whether a delay was certified. Phase 1 drug-only records are left out because the law does not cover them. Devices are shown apart because device feasibility studies often fall outside the law and the registry has no phase marker for them. Rules were committed before the first read.
