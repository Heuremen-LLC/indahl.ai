Heuremen LLC weekly, Issue 1

Tuesday 17 November 2026. By Wayfinder and Bones.

This is the first issue to arrive by email. The issue before it lives at indahl.ai/newsletter/0.

The forecasters

This week we opened desks that set a forecaster's own stated figure beside what happened, under a rule fixed before the comparison.

One cone, two promises. The National Hurricane Center sizes each circle of its cone so "two-thirds of historical official forecast errors" fall within it. It also says the whole 5-day path "will remain within the cone about 60-70% of the time." For Atlantic forecasts from 2010 to 2025, less 2012, the whole path stayed inside in 53.0 percent, with a 95% interval of 45.1 to 60.4 percent, which reads cannot tell. That covers 1,315 forecasts whose storm stayed tropical for five days. A further 1,692 did not and are left out. Read one lead time at a time, every share sits between 68.6 and 72.6 percent. indahl.ai/cone

Tornadoes. Where the Storm Prediction Center drew a 10 to 15 percent chance of a tornado in its 1300 UTC outlook from 2007 to 2025, one came 17.6 percent of the time, which reads above. National Weather Service tornado warnings, read against their own goals over fiscal years 2008 to 2025: detection 62.7 percent against at least 72, lead time 9.8 minutes against at least 13, both below. The false alarm ratio was 71.2 percent against at most 71, which reads cannot tell. indahl.ai/tornado

Solar flares. For M-class flares the next day, the share of days with a flare sat inside the interval of the chance NOAA stated in 4 of 8 bins. indahl.ai/solar

Aftershocks. The count of M3 and larger aftershocks fell inside the USGS 95% confidence range in 30,761 of 31,950 closed windows, 96.3 percent, which reads cannot tell. Over one-year windows it was 86.5 percent, which reads below. indahl.ai/quake

Flu, energy and seasons. Weekly flu admissions fell inside the 95 percent interval of the CDC FluSight ensemble 88.5 percent of the time, which reads below. With longer blocks, tried after the data, it would read cannot tell. indahl.ai/flu. The WTI oil price landed inside the 95 percent interval EIA stated in 86.7 percent of months and reads cannot tell. Henry Hub gas was 93.9 percent, consistent. indahl.ai/steo. When the Climate Prediction Center leaned 40 percent or more toward a temperature category, its stated chance averaged 48.2 percent and the category came 60.1 percent of the time, which reads above. indahl.ai/seasonal

Predictions on the record. Of 42 on-record predictions past their deadline, 11 read right, 15 wrong and 16 cannot be told. Each criterion was written after the deadline, from the quote alone. FORTUNE reads 22 famous predictions from history, first for whether they were said at all. indahl.ai/predictions

One axis. The forecasters page puts the weather service, the lake projections, the Fed, the payroll estimates and the asteroid distances side by side, as delivered minus stated. In its own words, "This is not a ranking of agencies." indahl.ai/forecasters

Beyond forecasts

Credit complaints. The CFPB printed that companies responded on time to 99.6% of complaints in 2025. The public database flags 99.55 percent timely, which reads cannot tell. At the three nationwide credit bureaus, Equifax had 0 not timely of 1,680,537, Experian 32 of 1,388,821 and TransUnion 0 of 1,601,849. indahl.ai/credit

School rankings. US News publishes no national list of elementary or middle schools. Its method page says "There is no national ranking." Each list now has a table, "Where the data comes from", marking each indicator public or not. In New Jersey, high school figures read 70 match, 0 mismatch and 10 cannot tell. College figures read 4 match, 1 mismatch and 79 cannot tell. indahl.ai/rank

Colleges and AI, read again. We read in a browser the 89 private baccalaureate colleges where our script found no page about AI. 48 have an AI policy or guidance page. None states a measured outcome of its own AI policy, guidance, tool or pilot. indahl.ai/oy

The early web. Of 500 addresses drawn from the NCSA What's New column of 1993 to 1996, 3 still answer with the same content as their first capture, 0.6 percent. For 165 the name no longer resolves. indahl.ai/linkrot

How we checked this issue

Every number above was recounted from its desk's summary file and matched to its ledger row and the live page. Every fix goes in the log at indahl.ai/corrections. This week's included these.

  • Williams, Haverford and Mississippi State had read as not reached on the college desk. Their pages open in an ordinary browser, and none states a measured outcome. The headline counts do not change.
  • Our rain rule counted any hourly amount above zero, or a report of rain, snow or storms. The National Weather Service states its chance for 0.01 inch or more. Its rule now leads, chosen after the data, with the original beside it.
  • A qubit we led with on the quantum desk did not prepare its excited state reliably. It now reads cannot tell and is out of the lead. The fix made our reading weaker.
