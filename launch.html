Stated launch time against the time it flew

Every upcoming orbital launch carries a stated target time that keeps being revised. The schedule source keeps only the current time, so we read it about every half hour, store each change, and score a launch after it flies. For each launch we take the time that was stated 1, 7 and 30 days before it flew and measure how far the flown time was from it.

Our first snapshot was taken on 28 October 2026 and earlier history is not claimed. Rows appear here as launches that we were already watching fly. Rules were committed before the first snapshot.
