Division: IRIS

Does the size in a model's name match the weights?

Many model names state a size, such as 8B or 135M. For the most downloaded models on Hugging Face this page compares that size with the parameter count Hugging Face computes from the weight files. Names with no size, with several sizes, or with a mixture-of-experts pattern, and repositories that are quantized or packed, are not comparable and are counted apart.

The plan, with its dated addenda and notes, was committed before the first model was read. The band that counts as agreement was fixed before the first read. A row outside it is read in full and the reason written beside it. Rows not yet read in full are counted above the tables as awaiting a read in full. The page does not say that the name is wrong. Most rows are a name that states one part of a model or the model a draft checkpoint pairs with.

Named here and want to answer a row? Respond

Agree: the weights hold between 0.70 and 1.35 times the stated size. Disagree: outside that band. Above: the lower end of the 95 percent interval of the disagreement share is above 0.10. Consistent: the upper end is below 0.05. Otherwise, and with fewer than 30 comparable models, cannot tell. The list of models where Hugging Face's own total contradicts its own breakdown was defined after the first read and carries no outcome. Source: the Hugging Face API.
