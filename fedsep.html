Division: HERMES

Do the Fed's stated ranges describe what happened?

Each Summary of Economic Projections carries a table of average historical projection error ranges and says there is about a 70 percent probability that outcomes for real GDP, unemployment, consumer prices and the federal funds rate fall within those ranges of the projection. This page takes the September projections from 2015 to 2025, the median for the current year and the next year, and counts how often the outcome landed outside the stated range. A range that described the errors would leave about 30 percent outside.

The plan was committed before any outcome was compared, with one dated addendum and two notes from the full read. The intervals are made over years, because the four variables of one year share the same economy. The ranges describe past forecast errors by various forecasters, and one year outside is no contradiction. There is no rate, price or forecast of ours on this page.
