Correction or Removal

Ask for a correction or a removal

Anyone can use this form: a person or organisation named on one of our pages, an author, or anyone who finds an error. It needs no agreement, no sign-in and no work email. Nothing you send here is posted.

We read every request in full and answer by email. A correction is made at the source and the page says what changed. For a removal of a name or a page, we may write back to confirm that the request comes from the person or organisation named.

What this form keeps and for how long is in the privacy notice. To add a public response to a page instead, use the Respond link at the foot of that page.
