Heuremen LLC publishes its first research and data

For release: 26 October 2026

Heuremen LLC, a research company, published a paper and its data on heuremen.org today, and data tables on indahl.ai.

A Readout Is Not an Idle measures what reading out some qubits does to the qubits left untouched, on IBM 156-qubit processors, with every run, calibration record and analysis released.

Recount Matrix: Learning Research lists what United States colleges and universities have published about measuring their own policies on AI in teaching and learning, with a source for every row. As of 25 October 2026, of 148 institutions listed:

  • 0 had published a measured learning outcome of their own AI policy.
  • 3 had measured their own policy or an institution-wide tool.
  • 8 cited a chatbot as the source in their own AI documents.

Any institution may add a PDF to its row from the table.
