Corrections

Every catch and every fix, in the open

This log lists every referee catch and every fix we made to a reading on indahl.ai or heuremen.org, newest first. Each row says what was published or staged, what was wrong, what it is now, why, and who caught it. Staged means the reading was checked before it went public. Fixes that made our own numbers weaker are listed the same way as the others.

Our referee is a reviewer who reads the data and the page before or after publication. Our cold read is a read-only pass of the live site. Our own check is a test we run on our own work. An outside reader is anyone outside Heuremen LLC, named only when they ask to be. The issued bill QIA-2026-001 is listed here when anything about it changes.

Rows are never removed. To report an error, use the correction or removal form. Each row links to its page and to the plan, commit or receipt that records the fix. Where the working file that records a catch is not in the public repository, its path is printed.
