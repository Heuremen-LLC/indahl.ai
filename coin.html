Ravencoin, Stated Against Delivered

Block records from a public explorer, read every few minutes. Each statement is quoted from the Ravencoin whitepaper and held against what the chain recorded. The hash of the latest block settles character by character as more of the recent chain is read.

Consistent: the record fits the statement. Above and below: the record is higher or lower than stated. Cannot tell: the statement gives no threshold or the block record cannot show it. Payout addresses and coinbase tags are shown as the chain records them and say nothing about who is behind them. No ranking.

Trends: Pool Shares and Batch Payouts

Who signs the blocks, hour by hour, and who pays the miners. Blocks and transactions are read from a public explorer. Each pool address or coinbase tag has its share of blocks tested in six hour buckets against its own earlier share, and every move of three or more standard deviations is counted against the number chance alone would give. Batch payouts are transactions with twenty or more outputs spent from a watched address. The plan was committed before any series was computed.
