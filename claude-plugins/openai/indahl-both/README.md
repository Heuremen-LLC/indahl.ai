# indahl-both

Hold two competing explanations open instead of collapsing to one too early: two evidence ledgers, four states (A, B, BOTH, NEITHER), and the test that would separate them. Nothing is sent outside your session.

Heuremen LLC, https://indahl.ai. Source: https://github.com/Heuremen-LLC/indahl.ai/tree/main/claude-plugins/src/both

## Services this plugin contacts

None. It runs inside your session.
