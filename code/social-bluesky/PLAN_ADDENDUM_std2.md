# BLUESKY: plan addendum for Faults and Guards 2.0, 12 November 2026

This addendum is written before any reading under the new outcome rule. PLAN.md and its addenda are not edited. The stream, the counting readings R1 to R7, the stated figures, the signature check and the planted-world gate are unchanged. Only the outcome rule is moved onto the kit's read, and the way a cannot tell is named and the counts beside the page are printed. Outcomes recorded earlier stand under the rules they named.

## The rule
- Each stated figure has a band from the rounding of the printed figure, as the plan fixes it. The delivered side is the lowest to highest count or share across the plan's counting readings. That range is a rule spread and the page says it is not a sampling interval, so no level, no draw and no seed apply.
- A stated figure read as a plain count or share is read with read(band), edges closed. The range inside the band is CONSISTENT, wholly above is ABOVE, wholly below is BELOW, and a range that crosses an edge is CANNOT_TELL with reason STRADDLES_EDGE. The result is the same as the plan's earlier reading.
- A takedown or suspension count is a one-sided floor, because those actions can be taken outside the stream, and the plan reads only a stream count above the stated figure. It is read with read(bound), which gives above, below or cannot tell. A count above the bound reads ABOVE. Any other result is CANNOT_TELL with reason NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE, because the stream count is a floor and the planned quantity, the stated figure compared with the full count, cannot be formed.
- The plan names no size that matters, so the test of both alternatives is not applied.

## How the desk's own causes of CANNOT_TELL map
- A figure not in the stream, a stream that served no row of a value in the year, and a failed planted-world check: NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE. The stream that served no row is a read that succeeded and found no figure, so it is CANNOT_TELL and not an outcome called Nothing returned.
- A range that crosses an edge of the stated band: STRADDLES_EDGE.
- The status NOTHING_RETURNED is kept for a failed read of a source, and the desk has none when its gates pass.

## Counts beside the headline
The section prints the REQ-13 line for its rows: the NOTHING_RETURNED count, then the CANNOT_TELL share split by reason. The section has no headline number of its own.

## What flips
No outcome changes. The one BELOW row and the CANNOT_TELL rows keep their outcomes and carry a reason.
