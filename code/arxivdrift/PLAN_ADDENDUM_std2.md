# Addendum for Faults and Guards 2.0 to the ARXIVDRIFT plan, 12 Nov 2026

Written before any new reading under the 2.0 reporting rule. PLAN.md is not edited. This file is a new addendum.

## What changes
Only what is printed beside the headline share. Data, sources, the arXiv reads, the classes, the known-good gate and the Wilson 95 percent interval of the share of studies with any change are unchanged.

## Outcomes
The desk is descriptive and tests no claim. It assigns no ABOVE, BELOW, CONSISTENT or CANNOT_TELL outcome to any study or to the share. The classes UNCHANGED, REVISED_SAME, REVISED_TEXT, REVISED_NUMBERS and WITHDRAWN stay descriptive classes. There is no size that matters, no band, no tolerance and no edge convention. The interval method stays a Wilson interval at 95 percent for the share of studies with any change.

## Failed reads (REQ-13)
NOTHING_RETURNED is the status of a failed read of the arXiv API for one study. It is never a class of a study and never an outcome. Studies whose last read returned nothing are listed apart in the summary as failed reads. They are outside the numerator and the denominator of every share. Studies not yet compared (PENDING, because the read version's own abstract has not been fetched yet) are also outside, and are listed with their count.

## Beside the headline share
The page prints one line from the kit: the count of NOTHING_RETURNED and the CANNOT_TELL share split by reason. Because the desk assigns no outcome the CANNOT_TELL share is 0 of the studies read and the split reads none. The line is printed so that the absence is stated and not implied.

## Earlier recorded classes
Every class recorded before 12 Nov 2026 stands under the rules the plan named then.
