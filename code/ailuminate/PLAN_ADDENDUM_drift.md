## What the stored data shows, 12 Nov 2026
All 170 stored page reads and all stored report reads carry one label set: Safety 1.2.1, Problem Set 2.0, evaluator v1.4. The results page lists no Safety 1.0 or 1.1 results and no archive link. Nothing version-related has changed in our record, so tests G and I below return NOTHING_RETURNED today. Tests F, H and J run today. The reader (ailuminate_tick.py) keeps only reports it can parse as 12 percentile rows, so a report in a different format (grades, bands) would land in its unread list and drift_tick.py reports that list as a change in coverage.

## Statements tested
S1. "MLCommons will not change what a score means without changing the label" and "Existing results are not re-scored" (release notes). Tested by F and H.
S2. "Results you already hold are untouched and keep the version labels they were produced with." Tested by I.
S3. "Only results labeled ... compare directly" and "Scores from new runs are not comparable with earlier scores." Not tested as true or false. G measures how much scores moved across labels and says nothing about whether they should have.
S4. The stated judge. The label line, and the sentences quoted in J, are kept and compared with their first stored value.

## Rulers named first
R1. The label tuple of a report: benchmark version, problem set, evaluator version, read from the "Technical labels" line of the report (not the page).
R2. The 13 stored numbers of a system (overall and the 12 category percentiles) and its release month, as ailuminate_tick.py stores them in data/reads.jsonl.
R3. A label is "the same" only when all three parts match exactly.
R4. For G, symmetry: if a label change moved no direction in particular, about half of the scores that moved went up. The ruler is the share up with its Wilson 95 percent interval, over pairs that moved.
R5. For F, H and I the ruler is the share of systems with a stated event, with a Wilson 95 percent interval, as in Test B of PLAN.md.

## Tests (fixed now)
F. Labels kept. For every system read at least twice, CHANGED when the label tuple of any read differs from the label tuple of its first read, KEPT otherwise. A report with no readable label line is counted as unreadable and left out. Headline: the share CHANGED among systems read at least twice, Wilson 95 percent. ABOVE if the lower end is above 0.05. CONSISTENT if the upper end is below 0.15 (27 systems need 0 changed). Otherwise CANNOT_TELL. BELOW is not used for a share that cannot go under zero. The same table lists, per label tuple, the first and last read it was seen in, and how many systems carry it in the latest read. This is where an evaluator change shows first, because the evaluator version is part of the tuple.

G. Item-by-item drift across labels. For every system that has been read under two or more label tuples, take its first stored values under its first label and its latest stored values under its latest label. For each of the 13 numbers, count UP, DOWN or SAME. Counts are kept per system, per category, and in total. They are never netted into a mean or a sum of differences. Headline: among pairs that moved (UP plus DOWN), the share UP with Wilson 95 percent. With fewer than 30 moved pairs the outcome is CANNOT_TELL. ABOVE if the lower end is above 0.5, meaning more went up than down. BELOW if the upper end is below 0.5. CONSISTENT if the interval lies inside 0.35 to 0.65. Otherwise CANNOT_TELL. Pairs from one system are not independent, so the interval is a ruler and not a significance claim, and the per-system counts are printed beside it. Until a second label tuple exists for any system this returns NOTHING_RETURNED.

H. Scores kept under an unchanged label. The unit is a system and a label tuple, with at least two reads under that tuple, CHANGED_SAME_LABEL when any of its 13 numbers or its release month differs from its first value under that tuple, KEPT otherwise. Headline: the share CHANGED_SAME_LABEL, Wilson 95 percent, with the F rule (ABOVE above 0.05, CONSISTENT below 0.15, otherwise CANNOT_TELL). This is Test B cut by label, so a score that moved under a changed label is not counted here. Each changed row lists both values and the dates of both reads.

I. Earlier-label results stay published. For every label tuple seen in a stored read and different from the latest label tuple, STILL_PUBLISHED when the latest read still contains at least one report carrying it, WITHDRAWN when no report in the latest read carries it. Also per system: DROPPED when a system read before is absent from the latest read, whatever its label. Headline: the share WITHDRAWN among earlier-label tuples with the F rule, which needs a second label to exist, so it returns NOTHING_RETURNED today. A system that is re-scored under a new label and has its old report replaced is counted as WITHDRAWN for the old label. That is a statement of what stays on the page, not a claim about intent.

J. Judge statements kept, no outcome. The following sentences are taken exactly from each stored results page and compared with their first stored value: the Technical labels line, the release-notes sentence (new components, evaluator), the "Only results labeled" sentence, the reference-set sentence, and the "MLCommons began running" sentence. CHANGED with both versions and the dates of both reads, or KEPT. A sentence not found is "not found" and counts as CHANGED if the first read had it. The fetched release-notes quotes above are not tested, since we do not store those files.

## Rules that stay
Plain sentences. No ranking and no list ordered by score. Every statement quoted with URL and read date. The tests fire on their own on the next tick after a stored read carries a second label tuple. Nothing is changed in ailuminate_tick.py.

## Known-good, run before every real read
A planted history of three reads with two label tuples and four systems must return exact planted counts. System S1 is read under the old tuple, then twice under the new one, and moves UP in 5 of its 13 numbers, DOWN in 3 and SAME in 5. S2 has the same two tuples and does not move. S3 stays under the old tuple and changes its overall at the last read. S4 is read once and is absent afterwards. Expected: F is 2 of 3 changed (S1 and S2), G is 5 up, 3 down and 18 same with 8 moved pairs, so CANNOT_TELL, H is 1 of 3 units changed (S3, all 13 numbers listed by name), I lists the old tuple as STILL_PUBLISHED because S3 still carries it and lists S4 as DROPPED. The same history with S3 removed from the last read must list the old tuple as WITHDRAWN. A planted null history of 27 systems read twice under one tuple must return F and H CONSISTENT and G and I NOTHING_RETURNED. Planted histories of 40 systems must return G ABOVE when all 13 numbers go up, BELOW when all go down and CONSISTENT when half go each way. The label parser must read the planted Technical labels line and return None for a text without it. A planted change in one sentence must show as CHANGED in J and the other sentences as KEPT.

## Not claimed
That any system is safe or unsafe. Any ranking of systems. That a change in a published score, label or evaluator is an error. MLCommons states that results are not comparable across labels and that the run is ongoing. A change under a new label is the expected behavior of the benchmark. A change under an unchanged label is a reading against the statement in S1, listed with both values, and still not a claim of error.
