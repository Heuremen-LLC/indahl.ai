## KNOWN-GOOD (ours, ours/known_good.py, before the readings)
From the inspect-ai logs (closed logs only, status success, picked by their task arguments), with our own code: Table 2 on the four
anchors present in both zai-honest-epochs2 (Gate 0) and s2-main (complete 22 to 7, unreachable 16 to 0, incomplete 0 to 28, budget 2 to

## Readings (defined after reading the paper; four outcomes; 95% intervals)
B1 The grammar shift per anchor: strong claims (complete plus unreachable) per anchor under each grammar (10 games each), with an exact
   Fisher test per anchor and the pooled Newcombe interval, noting that games share four anchors and the change is confounded with time
   (the paper's own caption).
B2 The disclosure contrast by instance: false verdicts per instance in each arm on the six solvable grid instances, how many instances
   carry the 18, an exact sign test with the instance as the unit, and the exact upper bound for 0 of 58.
B3 Meter against lantern in Gate 2, with the instance as the cluster: strong-claim rate difference (meter minus none-lantern) with a
   percentile bootstrap over the 12 instances (10,000, seed 42), beside the per-instance sign count and its exact test. The paper itself
   says binomial intervals on 60-game cells are optimistic.
B4 Record items. (a) Fidelity flags in Gate 2: games with at least one flag against flags in total, per cell, beside the printed
   "35/60 games flagged vs heroic 29/60". (b) Budget-bearing narrations in hetero_values.jsonl: rows against unique keys, beside 5,181.
   (c) Gate 2 eval headers: revision, dirty flag and generate config (temperature) per cell, beside Section 3.5 ("All runs ... temperature
   0.7") and the Gate 2 ratification text (55789d2, 081586f, 05acada).
The paper's 3,636 valued narrations rest on an inventory that is not released, so no reading is made of it.
