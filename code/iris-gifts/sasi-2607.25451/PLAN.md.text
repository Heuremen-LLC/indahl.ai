## Readings (defined after reading the paper; four outcomes; 95% intervals)
S1 Intervals the paper does not print: a Wilson interval for every extraction rate at n 500, and for each quantized configuration a
   Newcombe interval for the difference from its reference (unpaired, since per-sequence outputs are not released, so wider than a paired
   interval would be).
S2 The unreported larger runs: the 410M FP16 and NF4 files for seeds 1 and 2 (their n as stored), their extraction rates with Wilson
   intervals and m (NF4 over FP16 for each seed), beside the seed-0 values behind the "nearly three-quarters of the memorization" sentence.
S3 Two sentences against Table 1: "over seventy percent of memorized sequences" (1B NF4 extracts 0.596 of the sample and m is 0.718 against
   the FP16 reference) and "selectivity about seven" (s on Pile and on WikiText for 1B NF4).
S4 The reference against the definition: the paper defines the sampled sequences as ones "the full-precision model reproduces greedily",
   and the reference extraction rates are 0.756, 0.834 and 0.830. Read whether any released file states why.
L1 Live reference arm (their items through the same public weights on our setup). Setup record before the first run: EleutherAI
   pythia-160m-deduped at 582159a2dfe3, pythia-410m-deduped at c4fc8d586d62 and pythia-1b-deduped at 7199d8fc61a6 (sha256 of every weight
   file recorded), transformers and torch versions, Mac CPU in FP32 (the Apple GPU is held by the lab queue), their three .npy samples,
   their generate call (first 32 ids as prompt, greedy, 32 new tokens, one beam, all-ones attention mask, pad id 0, batch 32), exact match
   of all 32 ids, mean correct prefix, token accuracy, and the per-sequence outcome. Two repeats each, all runs before any reading.
   Readings: our FP32 exact-match rate per model beside theirs (FP32 for 160M and 410M, FP16 for 1B, so the 1B comparison crosses
   precision and is labelled), our repeat floor (sequences whose outcome differs between repeats), and which sequences fail at full
   precision. Their GPU, CUDA, torch and transformers versions are not recorded, so a gap is a setup carrier, never a cause.
