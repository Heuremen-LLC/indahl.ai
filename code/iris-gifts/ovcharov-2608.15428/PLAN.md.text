## The lines it answers
Llama 3.1 8B through Amazon Bedrock answers A on 92% of blind items (Table 4: A 0.92, B 0.00, C 0.07, D 0.01, blind 0.292, habit 0.287,
excess +0.005, n 7,886 of 8,128), and in the full condition returns no parseable letter on 47.5% of calls, "and a larger token budget does
not help". His paper cannot say whether the A habit and the unparsed replies belong to the weights or to the serving path. Two machines of
our own, the same open weights, his prompts and his parser, can.

## Runs (our runner, his prompt strings and his parser copied as text from his code/scenario_a.py and code/small_models.py)
- Model files: Ollama library llama3.1:8b and ministral-3:8b (Q4_K_M), the same file digest on both machines, recorded before the first run.
  Bedrock serves its own weights format, so every reading below is about these weights on our two setups, and says so.
- Request: /api/chat, one user message holding his prompt text, temperature 0, seed 1, repeat_penalty 1.0 (set, not left to the engine),
  num_ctx 8192; num_predict 4096 for the blind arm (his scenario_a) and 2048 for the full arm (his small_models).
- Arm B: blind, all 8,128 gate-accepted items, his BLIND prompt, options in stored order.
- Arm F: full condition, his 600-item sample (400 accepted, 200 rejected, the ids in his small_models.jsonl), his FULL prompt.
- Machines: Armature (RTX 3080 10 GB, one parallel slot) and the Mac (our seat 127.0.0.1:11500, one job at a time, after the queued S1L
  rounds). Two repeats per machine. Order: Armature B r1, B r2, F r1, F r2 for llama3.1:8b, then ministral-3:8b; the Mac the same.
  Server version, parallel slots and model digest saved beside every run.
- Every reply is saved with its raw text, parsed letter and token counts.

## Readings (four outcomes; intervals are 95% Wilson for rates, exact binomial for flip counts)
R1 repeat floor per machine: items whose parsed letter differs between the two repeats, with the unparsed counted as its own class.
R2 machine floor: items whose parsed letter differs between machines (repeat 1 against repeat 1), against R1.
R3 the habit: A/B/C/D shares, blind accuracy, habit and excess on each machine, against his Bedrock row. CONSISTENT where his printed
   value sits inside our interval, ABOVE or BELOW otherwise, CANNOT_TELL where our two machines disagree with each other beyond R1.
R4 parse: unparsed share in arm B and arm F on each machine against his 242 of 8,128 (blind) and 198 of 400 (full, accepted).
R5 ministral-3:8b, the same readings, against his row (A 0.02, B 0.25, C 0.45, D 0.29, blind 0.239).
Before any rate is read, 10 replies per arm per machine are read in full against the parser.

## What this gift will not say
Anything about Bedrock's serving internals. Anything about other models. A cause for the habit: we separate weights from serving, no more.

## Addendum 1 (30 Oct 2026, 19:50 UTC by the commit clock, e72619f; before any run; only a smoke test, written to scratch, has touched a model; item 5)
1. What his release holds. His prediction files keep the parsed letter only, no reply text. His "unparsed" holds two outcomes: a call that
   failed after retries (reply None) and a reply with no letter. Ours keeps the reply text and keeps errors as their own outcome. R4 compares
   our no-letter count plus our errors against his unparsed count and says the bins differ in what they hold.
2. R6, added now: item by item, his Bedrock letter against ours on the same items, counted in each direction (same letter, different letter,
   his unparsed and ours parsed, ours unparsed and his parsed), against our R1 and R2 floors. A Bedrock-to-ours flip count inside our own
   floors is CONSISTENT with the same behaviour; above them, the serving path moves the letter.
3. Carriers named: Bedrock applies its own chat template and serving format; Ollama applies the template shipped in the model file (its hash
   is saved beside each run). Llama 3.1 8B Q4_K_M digest 46e0c10c039e... is the same file on Armature and the Mac seat (read 30 Oct
   19:45Z). Armature: Ollama 0.34.3, OLLAMA_NUM_PARALLEL=1, MAX_LOADED_MODELS=2, KEEP_ALIVE 30m, RTX 3080. Mac seat: Ollama 0.34.2,
   NUM_PARALLEL=1, MAX_LOADED_MODELS=1 (seats/serve.sh). The server versions differ and R2 names that.
4. Order: Armature runs between Faults/Guards rounds r2 and r3 (rounds PLAN Addendum 2). The Mac runs after the queued S1L rounds, from the
   lab queue, the same order.
5. Smoke test: 3 blind items (general-0001 to 0003) and 2 full items (civil-0840, criminal-2734) through the runner on Armature,
   19:46:13Z to 19:50:28Z, saved to scratch, never part of any reading. (Corrected 19:50 UTC, daca899: the first line of this addendum said 2 blind items.)
