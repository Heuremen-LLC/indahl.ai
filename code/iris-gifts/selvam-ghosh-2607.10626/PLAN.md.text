## KNOWN-GOOD (ours, ours/known_good.py, before the readings)
From the nine data/runs/*judge-*-on-*/06_judge.jsonl files with our own code: per answer (record, generator) the judges' contains_factual_error
flags and the answer's gold_has_induced_error; on the 275 records that pass all five validation gates, the paper's paired same-model contrast
(matching judge's flag minus the mean of the other judges' flags on the same answer, averaged over answers): recall on answers that adopted the
induced claim (printed -0.5 pp) and flagging on answers that avoided it (printed -4.3 pp), and the answer counts.

## Readings (defined after reading the paper; four outcomes; 95% intervals by a record-clustered bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 42)
E1 The perturber as judge. Each record's induced contradiction was written by one of the three models (generators.perturb.provider). The same
   paired contrast with the perturber's judge in place of the generator's: the perturber-judge's flag minus the mean of the other judges' flags
   on the same answer, on adopted and on avoided answers.
E2 E1 restricted to answers whose generator is not the perturber, so the perturber's judge is never the matching judge there, beside the
   paper's same-model contrast restricted to answers whose generator is not the perturber.
The paper's own interval scheme is not stated (the scout matched it only with an unstated seed rule), so our intervals are ours, said so.

## Addendum 1 (31 Oct 2026, written after E1-E2 were computed and before E2b; E2b is defined after the data)
On answers whose generator is not the perturber, three distinct judges see each answer: the generator's (M), the perturber's (P) and the third
(O). E2's P minus the mean of M and O moves with M, and M flags less on avoided answers, so E2 cannot separate a perturber effect from the
paper's same-model effect. E2b compares the two judges that are not the generator's: P minus O on the same answer, on adopted and on avoided
answers, record-clustered bootstrap as above.

## Addendum 2 (31 Oct 2026, after the referee pass; E3 and E4 are defined after the data)
Wording from the referee: judges never see the perturbed passages, so the perturber's judge is "the judge from the model that wrote the
contradiction", not a judge seeing its own perturbation, and E2b is worded as "do not differ detectably" with its bound. New readings:
E3 the validating model against the perturbing model for every record (generators.validate.provider), and the records failing any gate by
perturber.
E4 the paper's matching-judge contrast on answers whose generator is also the perturber, beside the same contrast on the other answers.
