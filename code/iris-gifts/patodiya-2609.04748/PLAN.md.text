## Known-good before any reading (L0)
- Our rendered prompt sha256 equals her prompt_sha256 for all 200 items (checked by the driver before the first request; it refuses otherwise).
- Telemetry purity per request, as in her check: cached_tokens 0 on cold passes; on warm passes cached_tokens equal to the prompt length less

## Readings (each per format and per session; four outcomes; nothing folded)
L1 Path determinism on Metal: items with cold token ids equal to cold2, and warm equal to warm2 (of 200).
L2 Path divergence on Metal: items whose cold and warm completions differ, counted only where both paths agree with themselves, Wilson 95%;
   against her CUDA row at the same format (Q8_0 44.5 [37.8, 51.4], Q4_K_M 41.5 [34.9, 48.4], Q3_K_M 48.0 [41.2, 54.9]) by the difference
   with a Newcombe 95% interval. Both alternatives tested: zero divergence, and her rate.
L3 Session floor: items whose cold completion differs between our two sessions, and the same for warm.
L4 Metal against CUDA: items whose cold token ids equal hers exactly, and the same for warm (a carrier reading: kernels and hardware differ).
L5 Correctness: cold and warm accuracy, flips in each direction, exact McNemar on the discordant pairs.
L6 The divergence by kind as she reports it: of the divergent items, the share whose extracted answer is unchanged (formatting-only by her
   rule in scout/rebuild_bridge.py) and the first divergent token position.
