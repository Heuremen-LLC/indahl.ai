## Readings (defined after reading the paper and code; four outcomes; 95% intervals)
L1 The distance feature on labelled items. build_features computes each item's cosine distance to the nearest labelled item with the
   labelled set including the item itself, and fit_dcm fits on labelled items only. Check with their embeddings_minilm.npz and our own
   reveal draw in their scheme (labels revealed per item from {0, 1, 2, 5, 10}): how many labelled items have nearest distance 0, the spread
   of the standardised column inside the fit set, and its spread over zero-label items. If the column is constant inside the fit set, it
   moves with the intercept there and its coefficient is not set by the data, while it scales trust on zero-label items. Reported as
   identified / not identified / cannot tell. The size of its effect on Table 2 is not measured here (their pipeline is not run), and the
   reading says so. The 10-nearest density column is read the same way (self included for labelled items only).
L2 Five labels against the judge, the paper's "worth about five labels" in the same units. Two rulers side by side.
   (a) The original five-annotator labels (old_labels in the mirror) as a distribution, Manhattan against the full 100-label distribution,
       beside the judge's error on the same items, paired difference per item with an item bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seed 42). These
       items were chosen by ChaosNLI for disagreement among those same five annotators, so this panel is gated on its own labels: never
       unanimous by construction. Items without exactly five valid old labels are counted by reason and left out of both arms.
   (b) Five labels drawn without replacement from one 50-label half, scored against the other half, beside the judge and the old five
       scored against that same half, over 200 random half-splits (seed 0), per-item means, item bootstrap for the paired differences.
   Outcome per ruler: the judge ABOVE, BELOW or CONSISTENT with five labels, or CANNOT_TELL.
L3 Appendix C direction. The printed errors are self-update 0.258, conjugate 0.262 with the familiarity channel and 0.307 without
   (dice/RESULTS.md line 174 holds the same three). Lower error is better (Table 5 caption). Read whether "loses to" matches the printed
   order. Per-die values are not rebuilt here, so no interval is claimed on the 0.004 gap.

## Addendum 1 (30 Oct 2026, written after L1-L3 were computed and before L2c; L2c is defined after the data)
L2 found the judge level with the original five annotators against all 100 labels, and behind five fresh labels from the same pool against a
50-label half. L2c locates the judge on the fresh-label scale: the error of k fresh labels (k = 1 to 5, drawn without replacement from one
half) against the other half, over the same 200 splits, beside the judge's error there. Descriptive.
