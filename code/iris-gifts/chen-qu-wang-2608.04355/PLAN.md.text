   reverse) counted; the margin (helpful minus harmful)/n with an exact interval from the Clopper-Pearson interval on helpful/(helpful+harmful)
   conditional on the discordant count; outcome per cell (ABOVE, BELOW, CONSISTENT with zero, with the bound printed) beside their bootstrap
   interval. Counts of cells by outcome, by scale (0.8B and 2B against 4B, 9B and Gemma).
D2 Token cap on unparseable first answers: for every cell, the a0 replies with no parseable answer (scout is_bad), re-encoded with the
   Qwen3.5 tokenizer at their pinned model revision (tokenizer.json fetched as data, sha256 recorded), counted against the cap in their code
   (896 multiple choice, 1024 open, 512 code). Histogram of lengths from cap minus 10 to cap printed. Ruler side by side: the same count on
   a0 replies that did parse (a reply that parsed should rarely sit at the cap). A reply counts as at the cap when its re-encoded length is
   at least cap minus 3 (re-encoding decoded text can drift by a token or two; the histogram shows the drift). Split their format-recovery
   margin into recoveries whose a0 sat at the cap and the rest. Gemma cells use the same rule with the count marked approximate (Gemma's
   tokenizer differs; we use Qwen's only for Qwen cells and print Gemma as characters).
D3 GSM8K and MATH change by numeric equality against the string inequality their change rate uses: rows where the two answer strings differ
   but parse to the same number, per cell, and the change rate both ways.
D4 Closure with its population stated and an interval: per constrained cell, closure 1 - |constrained total - content| / |original total -
   content| with a paired bootstrap over task ids (10,000 draws, seed 2026, their seed), and the median over each population: cells with a0-bad
   at least 8% under the original extractor (the population their text names), the ten with a printed closure, and all fourteen.

## Carriers and limits
No model is run. D2 re-encodes decoded text, so a count at the cap is a re-encoding, not their generation log; the histogram is printed so
the reader sees the drift. Any addendum is dated and committed before the reading it changes is computed.
