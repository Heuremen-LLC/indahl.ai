- Settings exactly as their runner: system text "You are an AI scientist. Analyze step by step, then conclude.", the task prompt as the user
  turn, tokenizer.apply_chat_template(..., add_generation_prompt=True), torch_dtype float16, generate(max_new_tokens=512, do_sample=False,
  pad_token_id=eos), decode of the new tokens with skip_special_tokens=True. Their runs were on CUDA (V100 for Qwen-1.5B by their text).
- Items: the first 30 tasks of their task_bank.json (dd_e01 to dd_h05), prompts verbatim; the runner refuses unless each prompt equals the

## Known-good before any reading (L0)
- Prompt equality as above (30 of 30 per model, or the run stops).
- The scorer (L4) passes its synthetic known-good on all 12 ground truths (ours/live/score_selftest.text), before any real reply is scored.
- No external known-good exists for the generator on this seat; the session repeat (L2) is the instrument check.

## Readings (four outcomes; nothing folded)
L1 Qwen-1.5B against their stored outputs: items whose text equals their stored raw_response (they stored text[:3000], so compare our text
   cut the same way), and for the rest the first differing character position. Different hardware and backend (CUDA V100 against MPS), so
   this is a carrier reading.
L2 Session floor: items whose generated token ids differ between our two sessions, per model.
L3 Phi-3.5-mini: whether every item yields model text (no error), new-token counts, and items that hit the 512-token cap.
L4 Ground truth on the 12 easy items that carry it (dd_e01 to dd_e12), one rule (ours/live/score.py) applied to every source: our
   Qwen-1.5B and Phi-3.5-mini replies and their released Qwen2.5-7B, Mistral-7B-v0.3 and Qwen2.5-1.5B replies. Rule: split the ground truth
   on commas outside parentheses; strip a "key=" or "key:" prefix; a part with two numbers joined by "-" or "to" is a range, found if any
   number in the reply falls inside it; a part starting Yes or No is found if that word appears; a token of at least five characters mixing
   letters and digits (a SMILES string, a formula, a UniProt id) is found if it appears verbatim; otherwise the part's first number is found if
   any number in the reply equals it at its printed decimals; a part with none of these is free text and not scored. An item is correct when
   every scored part is found, and an item with no scored part (dd_e07) is CANNOT_TELL. Parts that rest on one small integer (dd_e04 "2 nM",
   dd_e10 "4 aromatic rings") are weak matches and are marked so. Report items correct and parts found per source with Wilson 95% intervals,
   and paired exact McNemar between sources on the same items.
   Synthetic known-good (run before any real reply is scored, output kept): every ground truth scores as found against itself, and as not
   found against an empty reply and against a copy with every number moved by 10% plus one.
