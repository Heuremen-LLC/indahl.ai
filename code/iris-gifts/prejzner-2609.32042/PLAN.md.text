## Addendum 1 (30 Oct 2026): the live arm G1 on the Mac, setup and readings, written before any model call
Build known-good (zero GPU): our env replica passes his 17,973 logged steps with 0 mismatches (ours/env/BUILD.md). Token tie-out with each
GGUF's own vocabulary through llama-cpp-python 0.3.19 (ours/live/tokens_tieout.py): bielik-11b-v3 1,450 of 1,654 trajectories exact,
llama-pllum-8b 1,376 of 1,386 exact; every other trajectory has his count higher by 1 to 26 and never lower, which fits reply tokens counted
past the stop string and cut from his logged text, and leaves our prompt tokens equal to his (tieout_bielik.text, tieout_pllum.text).
Setup: Mac (Apple silicon, Metal), llama-cpp-python 0.3.19 built from the PyPI sdist (sha256 in ours/live/sdist/SHA256.txt) with
LLAMA_OPENSSL, LLAMA_CURL, LLAMA_BUILD_TOOLS and LLAMA_BUILD_SERVER off (the HTTP parts did not compile against the system OpenSSL and are not
used by the bindings). Model files from the repos in his docs/gguf_manifest.tsv, each sha256 equal to his manifest: Bielik-11B Q4_K_M,
Q3_K_M, Q2_K and PLLuM-8B Q3_K_M, Q2_K. The 7B Q3_K_M and Q2_K he requantized cannot be matched and are left out. Calls as his
LlamaCppRunner makes them: Llama(model_path, n_ctx=8192, n_gpu_layers=-1, chat_format=chatml for Bielik and llama-3 for PLLuM, verbose=False),
create_chat_completion(messages, temperature=0.0, top_p=1.0, max_tokens=512, seed=42), repair off, his main67 tasks in the order of his own
run for that file (the library reuses its prompt cache between calls). The library's own formatter output is checked against our rendered
prompt on every call. Two repeats per file, each a fresh process, all ten runs before any reading. Reference runs: his
v3_11b_2026-06-18__q{4,3,2}_no_repair and v3_pllum_2026-07-29__q{3,2}_main_no_repair (4090, CUDA).
Readings (four outcomes; exact tests on discordant tasks):
P1 repeat floor per file: tasks whose pass or fail differs between our two repeats, and tasks whose reply text differs at any step.
P2 ours against his: per file, tasks whose pass or fail differs between his run and our repeat 1, against P1; and tasks whose first reply is
   byte-identical to his.
P3 the collapse step on our machine: Q3_K_M to Q2_K pass counts and paired flips per model (his 11B 48 to 3, PLLuM 15 to 1).
P4 the 11B dip: Q4_K_M against Q3_K_M on our machine (his 36 against 48), paired, against P1.
A difference between his run and ours is read as a machine or build difference only where it exceeds our own repeat floor. The Armature
half needs a CUDA build of the same library and gets its own addendum.
