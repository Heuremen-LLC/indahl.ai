## Their headline (abstract)
The 120B LLM-judge (gpt-oss:120b) loses 47 accuracy points on keyword-stuffed Consumer Duty inputs (0.74 to 0.27), and a second judge from a
different family (kimi-k2.6) agrees only at Cohen's kappa 0.16 on that split, "localising the failure to the model rather than the corpus".
No interval is printed for 0.27 or for the drop.

## Readings (defined after reading the paper; four outcomes; 95% intervals)
S1 The headline drop. (a) Wilson intervals for 35/47 and 3/11 and a Newcombe hybrid-score interval for the difference. (b) Like for like:
   the Consumer Duty adversarial items with an assessment against their own source originals (source_id), paired correctness, discordant
   counts in each direction, exact two-sided McNemar. A source original with no assessment is counted by reason. (c) Bounds with the failed
   scenarios counted as wrong and as right. The drop is ABOVE zero only if its interval clears zero, and the reading says whether the interval
   also tells the printed size (47 points) from a much smaller one.
S2 By flip direction. Consumer Duty adversarial accuracy for flip_positive items (expected escalate) and flip_negative items (expected
   comply), for both judges, with exact intervals. Descriptive.
S3 The second judge on the principle's own scenarios. On Consumer Duty adversarial scenarios that both judges assessed, judge 1 against
   judge 2 accuracy, discordant pairs and exact McNemar. Kappa on those scenarios beside their pooled kappa (n 23, which also scores COBS
   scenarios against Consumer Duty), each with a percentile bootstrap over scenarios (10,000 resamples, seed 42). The paired accuracy test
   is the direct ruler for "model-side". Kappa sits beside it.
S4 Live embedder arm (their items through the same public embedder weights on our setup). Setup record before the first run: models
   sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5 at the Hugging Face revisions we fetch (sha256 of every weight file
   recorded), sentence-transformers and torch versions, Mac CPU (the Apple GPU is held by the lab queue), no prefix, model default
   truncation. Inputs: the text field of every scenario in corpus_principle, paraphrases, boundaries and adversarial, and the 32 exemplars in
   rules.py. Eq. 1 cluster means of cosine similarity against the released avg_positive_sim and avg_negative_sim for every scenario and
   cluster, then Eq. 1 and Eq. 2 with rules.py weights and thresholds against the released escalation verdict. Two repeats. Readings: max
   and median absolute difference per model, repeat floor (repeat 1 against repeat 2), scenarios whose verdict differs from theirs. Their
   embedding settings (prefix, truncation, normalisation) live in a package that is not public, so any difference is a setup carrier and is
   named as such, never a cause. mxbai-embed-large (Ollama) and the Armature half wait for a free GPU slot and get their own addendum.

## Addendum 1 (30 Oct 2026, written after S1-S3 were computed and before S3b; S3b is defined after the data)
S3 printed kappa 0.000 with a flat bootstrap on the 10 Consumer Duty adversarial scenarios both judges assessed. Judge 2 (kimi-k2.6)
escalated all 10 of them, and a rater who gives one verdict to every item has kappa 0 whatever the other rater does. S3b reads, for
Consumer Duty scenarios on their own principle in each split (original, paraphrase, adversarial, boundary), each judge's escalate share and
its accuracy on escalate-expected and comply-expected items separately, with Wilson intervals. It says whether judge 2's 8 of 8 on
flip_positive items comes with escalating comply-expected items too. Descriptive. No new test.

## Addendum 2 (30 Oct 2026, after the referee pass; S3c and S1d are defined after the data)
