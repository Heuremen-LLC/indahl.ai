# TIKTOK: plan addendum for Faults and Guards 2.0, 12 November 2026

This addendum is written before any reading under the new outcome rule. PLAN.md and its addenda are not edited. The sources, the matching choices, the stated and delivered ranges, the known-good checks, the day check and the readings defined after the data are unchanged. Only the way a cannot tell is named, the status of Nothing returned and the counts beside the headline change. Outcomes recorded earlier stand under the rules they named.

## What the plan already names
The gap range is the database range minus the report range, as a share of the report figure for counts and in percentage points for shares. The size that matters is 5 percent of the stated point for counts and 1 percentage point for shares, and the null is a gap of zero. The plan reads consistent when the gap range stays inside plus or minus that size, which is a two-sided band with the size as its edge, closed at both ends. Neither side is a sample, so no level, no draw and no seed apply. Each side is a range across the matching choices fixed in the plan, and the page says it is not a statistical interval. The plan's own reading of above and below is a gap range wholly over zero or wholly under zero, with a separate flag for whether it clears the size. That reading stays as the plan wrote it, and every outcome it gave stands. It is noted for the owner that the standard's reading of an interval over zero but inside the band is consistent.

## Nothing returned is no longer an outcome
The plan gives the outcome Nothing returned to a row where TikTok does not break out a market, where the database holds no removal filed for that market alone, and where the stated figure for a market is missing. The reads of the files succeeded and found no figure on one side, so no interval can be formed. Such a row is CANNOT_TELL with reason NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE and the plain cause stays in its reason text. The status NOTHING_RETURNED is kept for a failed read of a source, and the desk has none when its gates pass. Rows read as Nothing returned before are counted in the CANNOT_TELL share and in its denominator from now on.

## How the desk's own causes of CANNOT_TELL map
- A gap range that holds zero and reaches past the size: TOO_WIDE_TO_RESOLVE, because the range holds the null and the size that matters.
- A check that failed on the files, the database that opened in late September 2023, more than 1 percent of matched statements with no usable posting date, the rule that proactive-rate rows read cannot tell, and the rule that a share which flips with Automation alone reads cannot tell: NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE, because the planned quantity cannot be formed. This is a forced reading with no closed reason, and it maps to NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE.
- A market row with no market figure, no single-market removal, or a share among single-market removals only: NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE.
- A failed known-good gate under the plan as committed: NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE for every row, with the original beside it. The plan as committed reads every row cannot tell for this reason.
- STRADDLES_EDGE is used where a gap range is computed, does not hold zero and is neither above nor below, which no row is expected to be.

## Counts beside the headline
Beside the counts on the page the section prints the REQ-13 line for the EU rows and for all rows: the NOTHING_RETURNED count, then the CANNOT_TELL share split by reason. The section has no headline of its own, because no row is decided under the committed rules.

## What flips
No decided outcome changes. The rows that read Nothing returned read CANNOT_TELL with reason NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE, 498 under the committed rules in the reading of 10 November. The CANNOT_TELL count under the committed rules rises to every row.
