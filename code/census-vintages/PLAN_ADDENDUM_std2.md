# CENSUS VINTAGES: plan addendum for Faults and Guards 2.0, 12 November 2026

This addendum is written before any reading under the new outcome rule. PLAN.md is not edited and its seal is unchanged. The data, the sources, the yardstick, the known-good gates and the row rule are unchanged. Only the way a cannot tell is named and the counts printed beside the headline change. Outcomes recorded on 11 November stand under the rules the plan named.

## Rows are exact reads
A row compares one published value with one stated interval, the earlier figure plus or minus the yardstick half-width of PLAN.md. There is no sampling in that read. Under owner decision O1 it is an interval of zero width and is read with heuremen_kit read_exact against the band from the anchor minus the half-width to the anchor plus the half-width, edges included as in PLAN.md. Inside is CONSISTENT, above is ABOVE, below is BELOW. The interval is a yardstick of ours and the plan says so. No share of rows is read, so there is no point figure, no tolerance, no size that matters and no both-alternatives test. Interval method and level: the multiplier is 1.645, two-sided 90 percent normal, with no resampling, so no draws and no seed.

## How the desk's own causes of CANNOT_TELL map
Each cause gives CANNOT_TELL with reason NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE, because no width can be formed or no value exists to be compared. The plain cause is kept beside the code.
- No width, class A: no published width exists and the plan forbids using the spread between Vintages as one.
- No PES width: the published standard error is missing, zero or below.
- No stated or later value: a blank or absent figure.
- Gate failed: a known-good gate failed, so no row is read.
None of these has an interval that straddles an edge or is too wide, so none maps to those two reasons.

## Failed reads and the counts beside the headline
A failed fetch or parse of a source is NOTHING_RETURNED, a status of the read, listed apart and outside every count. The desk reads copied files and one cross-check call, and a failed read stops the width gate, so the count of failed reads is zero when the gates pass. The headline cell prints the REQ-13 line for its rows: the NOTHING_RETURNED count, then the CANNOT_TELL share split by reason.

No outcome of any row flips. The headline cell, July 1 2020 in Vintage 2025 against Vintage 2020, keeps its counts. The class A rows keep CANNOT_TELL and now carry the reason NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE. The second yardstick added after the referee is a display and keeps its wording.
