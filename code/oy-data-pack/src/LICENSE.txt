OY Data Pack
Heuremen LLC · https://indahl.ai/oy

This pack mixes text from several sources. Each part keeps the terms of its source, and Heuremen LLC adds no restriction to any of them. The parts are listed here by file.

1. Sentences, titles and quotations from schools and other authors

The sentences, course titles and quoted passages are quoted from the public pages and documents named by the address in the same row. They belong to the schools and authors of those pages, who keep their terms. Each row names its source. To correct or remove a row, use https://indahl.ai/correction.

This applies to these files, whose labels, counts and notes are Heuremen LLC's own text as item 3 says:

- json/oy_chatbot_citations.json
- csv/oy_chatbot_citations.csv
- json/oy_schools.json
- csv/oy_schools.csv
- csv/oy_school_sentences.csv
- csv/oy_school_course_titles.csv

2. Federal directory data

The federal ID, the school name and the Carnegie 2021 class come from the IPEDS directory of the National Center for Education Statistics, U.S. Department of Education. https://nces.ed.gov/collegenavigator/ . U.S. government data. This applies to the columns unitid, school, carnegie and nces_url of json/oy_schools.json and csv/oy_schools.csv.

3. Heuremen LLC's own parts

The README, SOURCES.md, NOTICE.txt, LICENSE.txt, TESTED.md, START_HERE.html, verify.py, the build code, the compilation (the reading of the pages into tables, the flattening, the CSV conversion, the column names), the labels, counts and notes written by Heuremen LLC, and these files:

- json/oy_findings.json
- csv/oy_findings.csv
- csv/oy_finding_institutions.csv
- json/oy_list.json
- csv/oy_list.csv
- json/oy_text.json
- csv/oy_text_meta.csv

The labels, counts and notes written by Heuremen LLC are the columns measures_own_policy, released_own_data, public_policy_or_guidance, pilot_results, what_we_found, site_read, read_in_full_label, read_in_full_note, sentences_note and course_titles_note in all files, the finding text, notes and counts of the findings files, and the text file.
