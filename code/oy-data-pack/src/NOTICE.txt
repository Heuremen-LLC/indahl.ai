Quoted from schools and other authors
  Sentences, course titles and quoted passages in these files are quoted from the pages and documents named by the address in the same row. They belong to the schools and authors of those pages, who keep their terms. They are quoted so that each row can be traced to its source. Names of schools and authors appear as published.
  - json/oy_chatbot_citations.json
  - csv/oy_chatbot_citations.csv
  - json/oy_schools.json
  - csv/oy_schools.csv
  - csv/oy_school_sentences.csv
  - csv/oy_school_course_titles.csv
  To correct or remove a row, use https://indahl.ai/correction.

Federal directory
  The federal ID, the school name and the Carnegie 2021 class in json/oy_schools.json and csv/oy_schools.csv come from the IPEDS directory of the National Center for Education Statistics, U.S. Department of Education. https://nces.ed.gov/collegenavigator/ . U.S. government data.
