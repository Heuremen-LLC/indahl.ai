# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Bracketed items are filled per counterparty. When a counterparty insists on its own NDA, sign theirs only if it contains the substance of §3 (exclusions) and §4 (independent work); those two sections are the ones that protect our research.*

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [DATE] between **Heuremen LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company ("Heuremen"), and **[COUNTERPARTY LEGAL NAME]**, a [JURISDICTION AND ENTITY TYPE] ("Counterparty"). Each is a "Party"; a Party disclosing information is the "Discloser" and a Party receiving it is the "Recipient".

## 1. Purpose
The Parties wish to discuss a possible engagement in which Heuremen would measure, review or advise on systems, data or results of interest to Counterparty (the "Purpose"). This Agreement governs information exchanged for the Purpose. It does not oblige either Party to enter any engagement.

## 2. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by the Discloser to the Recipient for the Purpose, in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. It includes business plans, technical specifications, unpublished data, credentials and access details, and the existence and terms of the discussions between the Parties.

## 3. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show by written record:
(a) is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement by the Recipient;
(b) was known to or possessed by the Recipient before receipt from the Discloser;
(c) is received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality to the Discloser; or
(d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 4. Independent work (no restriction on Heuremen's own research)
Counterparty acknowledges that Heuremen conducts and publishes independent research, including measurements of commercially available systems made on Heuremen's own accounts, from public records and from information described in §3. Nothing in this Agreement restricts Heuremen from conducting, publishing or commercialising such work, or any results, methods, code or findings that do not use Counterparty's Confidential Information, whether made before, during or after the term of this Agreement, and whether or not they concern systems that Counterparty makes, sells, uses or competes with. The burden of showing that a disclosure by Heuremen used Counterparty's Confidential Information rests on Counterparty.

## 5. Obligations
The Recipient shall: use Confidential Information only for the Purpose; protect it with at least the care it uses for its own information of similar sensitivity, and no less than reasonable care; and disclose it only to its employees, members, contractors and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than these.

## 5A. Contact and premises
(a) "Heuremen Personnel" means Heuremen's members, managers, employees, contractors and agents. "Related Person" means any person with a personal, professional, social or family relationship with any Heuremen Personnel, including a spouse, partner, parent, child, sibling or other direct relative. "Heuremen Premises" means any property, office, facility or holding owned, leased, used or operated by Heuremen or its divisions, including their systems and accounts.
(b) Counterparty shall not, and shall cause its employees, contractors, agents and advisers not to, initiate or conduct any contact or engagement with any Heuremen Personnel or Related Person concerning the Purpose, Heuremen's work or the Confidential Information, or enter any Heuremen Premises, unless and until this Agreement has been accepted by Counterparty and confirmed by Heuremen under §12. After that, all such contact goes through the contact Heuremen names in writing.
(c) Information obtained through contact or entry in breach of (b) is Heuremen's Confidential Information under this Agreement, and is not covered by the exclusions in §3(b) or §3(c).

## 6. Compelled disclosure
If the Recipient is required by law, regulation or court order to disclose Confidential Information, it may do so after giving the Discloser prompt notice (where lawful) so the Discloser may seek protection, and shall disclose only what is legally required.

## 7. Return or destruction
On the Discloser's written request, or at the end of the discussions, the Recipient shall return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information within thirty (30) days, except copies retained in automatic backups or as required by law, which remain subject to this Agreement.

## 8. No licence, no warranty
All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. No licence under any patent, copyright or other right is granted except the limited right to use it for the Purpose. Confidential Information is provided "as is".

## 9. Term
This Agreement covers disclosures made within two (2) years of its date. Obligations for each item of Confidential Information last three (3) years from its disclosure, and for trade secrets and credentials for as long as they remain such.

## 10. Remedies
Unauthorised use or disclosure may cause irreparable harm, and the Discloser may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy, without posting bond where permitted.

## 11. General
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules; the Parties submit to the state and federal courts located in New Jersey. It is the entire agreement on its subject, may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties, and may be signed electronically and in counterparts. Neither Party may assign it without the other's consent, except to a successor of its business. Nothing in it creates an agency, partnership or obligation to purchase.

## 11A. Severability
If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted and the remaining provisions remain in full force.

## 12. Electronic acceptance
Counterparty may accept this Agreement electronically through Heuremen's inquiry process. Acceptance is made by a person who reads this Agreement, confirms their authority to bind Counterparty, and gives their full name, title and organisation from an identity verified by that process. Heuremen records the version of this Agreement accepted, the time, and the verified identity. The Agreement takes effect when Heuremen confirms acceptance in writing. Each Party agrees that this electronic acceptance and record have the same effect as a handwritten signature.
