# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

*DRAFT, 23 October 2026, built from the clause list in specbook §10, for one-time review by a business attorney before first use. Signed once per client; each engagement is a Statement of Work (SOW_TEMPLATE.md) under it.*

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [DATE] between **Heuremen LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company ("Heuremen"), and **[CLIENT LEGAL NAME]**, a [JURISDICTION AND ENTITY TYPE] ("Client").

## 1. Services
Heuremen will perform the services described in each statement of work signed by both parties (a "SOW"). Each SOW incorporates this Agreement. If a SOW conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls unless the SOW expressly states that it overrides a named section.

## 2. Independence
Heuremen performs its work independently. Its findings are its professional measurement and judgement and are not influenced by the Client's preferred outcome. Fees are not contingent on findings. Heuremen is not the Client's agent, and nothing here creates a partnership, joint venture or employment relationship.

## 3. Fees and payment
Fees, deposit and schedule are set in each SOW. Unless a SOW states otherwise: 50% of the fee is due on signature and the balance on delivery; invoices are payable net fifteen (15) days from the invoice date; overdue amounts bear interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum lawful rate, if lower. Third-party costs (including quantum processor time) are billed at cost, itemised. Work does not begin until the deposit is received. Fees exclude taxes, which the Client pays except for taxes on Heuremen's income.

## 4. Client responsibilities
The Client will provide the access, data and information described in the SOW, and represents that it is authorised to provide them and to commission the work. Where the work uses the Client's accounts on third-party systems, the Client confirms that such use is permitted under its agreements with those providers.

## 5. Credentials and access
Heuremen prefers credentials that the Client owns and can revoke. Heuremen will use credentials only for the engagement, will not write them into code, reports or published material, and will stop using them at the end of the engagement. The Client should revoke them when the engagement ends.

## 6. Confidentiality
Each party will protect the other's confidential information under the terms of the mutual non-disclosure agreement between them, or, if none is in force, under terms no less protective than Heuremen's standard Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement, including its sections on exclusions and on independent work. Within thirty (30) days after final delivery, Heuremen will delete or return Client data on request, except copies required by law or held in automatic backups, which remain confidential.

## 7. Intellectual property
(a) **Client materials.** The Client owns its data and pre-existing materials.
(b) **Deliverables.** On full payment, the Client owns the report or case statement delivered to it under a SOW.
(c) **Heuremen materials.** Heuremen retains all rights in its methods, procedures, software, measurement harnesses, analysis code, readers, templates and know-how, whether pre-existing or developed during the engagement ("Heuremen Materials"). To the extent Heuremen Materials are included in a deliverable, Heuremen grants the Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use them for its internal purposes.
(d) **Independent work.** Nothing in this Agreement restricts Heuremen's independent research or publication that does not use the Client's confidential information.

## 8. Publication
Heuremen will not publish or disclose the Client's name, the existence of an engagement, or any results derived from the Client's confidential information without the Client's prior written consent. With such consent, publication may be anonymised or attributed as the consent specifies.

## 9. Scope changes
If the work reveals matters beyond the agreed scope, Heuremen will stop work on those matters and propose a written change before continuing. No change is binding until signed by both parties.

## 10. Warranties and disclaimers
Heuremen will perform the services in a professional manner consistent with the methods described in the SOW. Measurements carry the uncertainties stated with them. EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION, THE SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES ARE PROVIDED WITHOUT WARRANTY OF ANY KIND, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE. Heuremen does not warrant any particular finding or outcome, or the performance of any third-party system.

## 11. Limitation of liability
EXCEPT FOR A PARTY'S BREACH OF CONFIDENTIALITY OR ITS INDEMNITY OBLIGATIONS, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR LOST PROFITS, REVENUE OR DATA, AND HEUREMEN'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT AND ALL SOWS IS LIMITED TO THE FEES PAID TO HEUREMEN UNDER THE SOW GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS BEFORE THE CLAIM.

## 12. Indemnity
The Client will defend and indemnify Heuremen against third-party claims arising from the Client's data, the access the Client provides, or the Client's use of the deliverables, except to the extent caused by Heuremen's breach of this Agreement.

## 13. Term and termination
This Agreement continues until terminated by either party on thirty (30) days' written notice; termination does not end a SOW in progress unless the notice says so. Either party may terminate a SOW for material breach not cured within fifteen (15) days of written notice. On termination the Client pays for work performed and costs incurred to the termination date; the deposit is non-refundable once work has begun. Sections 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12 and 14 survive.

## 14. General
Governing law: the State of New Jersey, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules; venue in the state and federal courts located in New Jersey. Notices: in writing to the addresses in the SOW, or by email to an address each party designates in writing. Entire agreement (with the SOWs and the NDA); amendments only in writing signed by both parties; electronic signatures and counterparts accepted; no assignment without consent except to a successor of the business; force majeure excuses delay beyond a party's reasonable control; if a provision is unenforceable the rest remains in effect.
