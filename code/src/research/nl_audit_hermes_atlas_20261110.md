## Read this first

1. **MODEL, Lenovo "15 vs 7" (serious).** The recount gives 7 and 8 of 12, as the desk says. But the desk ranks Lenovo's datacenter entries only against entries with the same accelerator (8x B200), and the SE100 edge entry against every closed edge entry. Rank the SE100 the same way the desk ranks the datacenter entries (same accelerator, RTX 2000E Ada) and it is first in 12 of 12 with a field of 1 in each. That is exactly Lenovo's table (2 + 1 + 12 = 15, "1/1, no competitors"). The BELOW on LN1 and LN4 comes from applying two comparison rules in one statement. Do not print "15 against 7" as a gap in Lenovo's claim. The narrow, true statement is that 8 of the 12 SE100 categories have other closed edge entries in the file.
2. **SUPPORT, code departs from the sealed plan.** The plan says a cycle is supported only if its stated support end has not passed. The code counts a cycle whose support end has passed as supported whenever its end of life is in the future (AlmaLinux 8, support ended May 2024, end of life 2029). The page describes the code's rule, not the plan's. The headline barely moves (plan rule on a fresh pull, 99 of 424 = 23% [14.8, 33.3] by products, code rule 123 of 508 = 24%). Outcome ABOVE either way.
3. **TRIALS, plan floor not built, and "other government" is not US government.** The plan says records 13 to 15 months past completion are a lag zone and not scored. They are scored (395 records, 62% with no results). Without them the headline is 21.5% [21.0, 22.1] and the 2025 cohort is 40%, not 44%. The "other government" class (55 records, 39 sponsors) is mostly non-US public universities and hospitals (Zagazig, Sahiwal, Gaziosmanpasa, Tel-Aviv Sourasky, Instituto Nacional de Cancer). Never write "government sponsors" without "non-US federal".
4. **CADENCE, page says 16 years, ledger says 15.8.** 5,782 days is 15.83 years. The page rounds up. Print "nearly 16 years" or "15.8 years".
5. **Intervals that read differently once clustering is honest.** REVISE benchmarked: ABOVE on months, CANNOT_TELL on calendar years [0.10, 0.33]. TRIALS: sponsor-clustered interval is about 3.6 times wider than printed ([20.2, 24.2] against [21.7, 22.8]). WIKI: country interval [80.1, 90.4] against Wilson [84.7, 86.2]. NEO: 13 of the 14 outside rows come from one archived copy, and without it the cell reads CONSISTENT. Print the honest interval or the outcome that survives it.
6. **No ledger row** for: TRIALS breakdowns (NIH 2%, other government 82%, device 33%, 2025 44%), AIRQ 50%, REVISE 13 of 120, CADENCE 633 of 1,219, FEDSEP 6 of 41. The sweep list is right on these. A newsletter number needs its row first.

No MISMATCH between a recount and a published count, except the CADENCE rounding (page side) and the small CADENCE rule-ambiguity drift (recount side, below). Nothing is STALE: every live feed matched its page lead at fetch time. The feeds refresh every 3.5 to 12 hours, so print each number with "as read on 10 Nov".
