## 1. THE THRESHOLD -- when does collective bursting switch on?

Per (culture, day): mean per-channel spike rate (Hz), and population synchrony as the Fano factor
(variance/mean) of the population spike count in 1-second bins, against a circular-shift null (each
channel's spike train independently rotated by a random offset, 200 shuffles per day, preserves each
channel's own rate and ISI structure, destroys cross-channel timing -- the standard synchrony null).

**All three cultures show the same two-stage climb, groups of three by age:**

| age group | days | n rows | mean rate (Hz/channel) | mean z vs. null | frac. days z>5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| early | 4-8 | 15 | 0.44 +/- 0.28 | 24.7 +/- 45.4 | 0.47 |
| mid | 10-17 | 19 | 1.16 +/- 0.42 | 1056.6 +/- 203.5 | 1.00 |
| late | 18-25 | 15 | 1.92 +/- 0.84 | 1279.0 +/- 103.5 | 1.00 |

**Per-culture, day by day (culture 1-1, representative):** day 4 fano=0.92 (z=0.5, indistinguishable
from the shuffled null); day 5 fano=1.65 (z=4.1); day 6 fano=3.17 (z=13.8); day 7 fano=1.36 (z=2.9,
back down -- the null is NOISY, not flat, at this age); **day 8 fano=5.09 (z=86.6) -- first
sustained departure**; **day 10 fano=82.0 (z=445) -- an ORDER-OF-MAGNITUDE jump**, then climbing
to fano 350-2400 (z 950-1340) through day 25. Culture 1-2: day 7 z=7.7, day 8 z=38.4, day 10
z=963.5 (same two-stage shape). Culture 1-3: day 7 z=12.5, day 8 z=172.8, day 10 z=784.4 (same
shape, the sharpest early rise of the three). **Read: the switch is not a single day but a TWO-STEP
climb -- a first real-but-modest departure from the null at day 7-8 (z in the tens to low hundreds,
consistent across all three cultures), followed by an order-of-magnitude jump to full network
bursting by day 10 (z in the hundreds to low thousands, held for the rest of the recording span).**
**The null itself (days 4-6-ish): Fano 0.7-3.2, mostly indistinguishable from the circular-shift
floor (z<5 in 8 of 15 early-group rows) -- Poisson-like, uncoordinated single-electrode spiking,
exactly what "no collective burst yet" should look like beside its own shuffled floor.**

## 2. PLACED vs ARISEN, spontaneous half only

No stimulation data on hand (Sec0) -- the placed (evoked) arm cannot be run. Logged as its own row,
not guessed. Spontaneous half: same-channel next-spike probability after a spontaneous spike, at
lags 1-20ms (3 bands: 1-7, 7-14, 14-20ms) and 20-500ms (3 bands: 20-150, 150-325, 325-500ms), each
channel's conditional hit-rate vs. an independence-null baseline (that channel's own unconditioned
rate, expected P(>=1 spike in the window) under a same-rate Poisson null), binomial standard error,
sign kept, pooled across channels by inverse-variance weight, by age group:

| age group | 1-7ms | 7-14ms | 14-20ms | 20-150ms | 150-325ms | 325-500ms | clock tau fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| early (4-8) | -0.08pp | -0.11pp | -0.09pp | -0.31pp | -0.26pp | -0.29pp | no fit (all negative, no decay to fit) |
| mid (10-17) | -0.08pp | -0.08pp | -0.09pp | **+15.0pp** | **+13.4pp** | -0.18pp | no fit (rise then drop, not monotone decay) |
| late (18-25) | **+56.0pp** | **+58.8pp** | **+53.9pp** | **+55.0pp** | **+15.1pp** | -7.1pp | **tau = 177.8 ms** (C0=64.1, n=5, log_resid=0.166) |

**Read plainly: the EARLY (pre-burst) network has no carry at all -- every lag band reads a small
negative excess (all within ~0.3pp of the null, essentially zero, sign not even consistently
positive). The MID network has a real excess but ONLY in the 20-150/150-325ms bands (network-burst
timescale, not single-spike refractory timescale) -- the fast bands (1-20ms) still read ~zero. The
LATE (fully bursting) network carries hard: 54-59pp excess at every band out to 150ms, decaying to
near-zero by 325-500ms, and THIS is the one age group where an exponential clock actually fits --
tau = 177.8 ms.** This is the neuron record's own arisen-carry clock, the same shape of object as
Marrakesh's 63.8 us qubit carry and the yeast's 785 s coherence decay: a spontaneous event predicts
the next several hundred milliseconds of activity on the same electrode, once the network can
burst at all -- before it can burst (early), there is nothing to carry.

## 3. THE THREE + DALET, population rate series, one pre-burst day + one bursting day

Reference culture 1-1 (most complete file set). Pre-burst day = 4 (lowest-z early day); bursting
day = 25 (last day). Population rate binned at 10ms (n=271,850 / 271,208 bins) and 100ms
(n=27,185 / 27,121 bins) -- the cutoff test. Arrow (best-k via 500-shuffle floor, forward +
reversed), clock (Hilbert-envelope autocorrelation exp-decay fit, forward + reversed -- reversal-
symmetric by construction, a ruler property per `THE_TABLE_20260930.md` Sec0, not a physics
result), split (candidate c, envelope-weighted time, forward + reversed), 3 Dalet hops (ascent on
the clock fit's own residual, renormalized, eta=0.10).

| day | bin | arrow (fwd A, z) | clock tau (native) | split P (fwd) | fwd vs rev split |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pre-burst (day 4) | 10ms | +0.000877, z=2.97 | 0.682 +/- 0.053 s | -1.29 +/- 6.35 | AGREE (diff 4.35, err 11.68) |
| pre-burst (day 4) | 100ms | -0.0485, z=-2.09 | 14.29 +/- 2.56 bins (1.43 s) | +1.56 +/- 1.41 | AGREE (diff 0.65, err 2.91) |
| bursting (day 25) | 10ms | +7031.7, z=224.6 | 0.770 +/- 0.042 s | -6.26 +/- 17.85 | AGREE (diff 20.55, err 30.89) |
| bursting (day 25) | 100ms | +2,499,416, z=53.6 | 13.07 +/- 2.01 bins (1.31 s) | -1.75 +/- 6.57 | AGREE (diff 10.51, err 14.06) |

**The clock is stable across bin choice in a way the arrow is not**: at 10ms binning tau lands
~0.68-0.77 s both days; at 100ms binning tau lands ~1.3-1.4 s both days (a real cutoff-sensitivity,
same shape as the yeast's own detrend-width sensitivity, reported not hidden) -- but pre-burst and
bursting DAYS agree with each other at fixed bin width far more than the two bin widths agree with
each other. The arrow flips both magnitude (orders of magnitude between bin widths) and its best-k
lag, and its sign flips between 10ms (+) and 100ms (-) on the pre-burst day specifically -- an
UNSTABLE quantity here, consistent with the arrow's known blind spots elsewhere in this line of
work. Split P is noisy (errors 1.4-17.9, larger than most central values) but every forward-vs-
reversed call AGREES (diff always inside combined error) on both days and both bin widths.

**Dalet, 3 hops:** residual variance fraction shrinks slowly on all four chains (pre-burst 10ms:
0.00183 -> 0.00166 -> 0.00151; bursting 10ms: 3.49e-5 -> 3.09e-5 -> 2.73e-5) -- NO fixed point
reached in 3 hops on any chain (unlike yeast/horn/buzz/mice, which hit one in 1-2 hops in
`REVERSE_GRADIENT_20260930.md`). **Honest flag, not smoothed over**: the Dalet-internal "tau"
diagnostic (the clock fit inside each hop) is itself unstable and physically implausible on this
record -- it drifts from ~91,000 to ~103,000 bins (910-1030 s) on the pre-burst 10ms chain and
~27,000-31,000 bins (270-310 s) on the bursting chain, both far longer than the 2700s recording
itself divided by any sensible cycle count. This is the Hilbert-envelope clock fit failing to find
a real periodic carrier in a population rate series that (unlike yeast's NADH oscillation or the
horn's radio hum) has no built-in beat frequency -- a genuine ruler mismatch for THIS formula on
THIS kind of record, logged as such, not hidden inside a converged-looking number.

## 5. Plain words

**The threshold day, and its null:** collective bursting is a two-step switch, the same shape in
all three sister cultures -- a first real-but-modest departure from a Poisson-like, largely
shuffle-indistinguishable null at day 7-8 (z in the tens to low hundreds), then an order-of-
magnitude jump to full network bursting by day 10 (z in the hundreds to low thousands), held for
the rest of the recording. The null (days 4-6) really does look like nothing: nearly half the
early-group rows don't even clear z=5 against their own circular-shift floor.

**Placed vs arisen, spontaneous half:** without stimulation data, only the spontaneous carry could
be measured, and it changes completely with the network's own developmental state -- essentially
zero at every lag before the network can burst, a burst-timescale-only excess (20-150ms, not
1-20ms) once bursting starts, and a full same-electrode carry across the whole 1-500ms range with
its own clean exponential clock (tau=177.8ms) once the network is fully bursting. **A spontaneous
spike leaves no memory in a silent network and a genuine, well-timed memory in a bursting one** --
the same qualitative answer THE_CLOCK_GAP's other substrates gave (a real carry exists once there
is a collective rhythm to carry it in), now shown to depend on the network's OWN developmental
stage, not just its identity as a substrate.

**The gap and the medium placement:** the clock-vs-split gap (G_s) is positive on every neuron
cell measured, same sign as fermentation, chip, mouse, and radio. Its record-length ordering
extends the one exact pattern `GAP_PER_MEDIUM_20261001.md` found (record length in samples orders
G_s at the substrate level) to a fifth point without breaking it -- consistent with, not proof of,
that pattern; the arrow's own gap (G_a) could not be computed this pass (logged, not guessed), and
the within-record cutoff sensitivity (10ms vs 100ms bins) and the Dalet clock's failure to find a
believable internal period on this specific kind of record are both real limits on how far this
result should be trusted before a sixth substrate arrives.
