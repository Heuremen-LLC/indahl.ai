## 0. Blind-spot column -- which formulas can read this record at all

These are RULER properties (facts about a formula meeting a given kind of
input), not physics results, and are stated once here instead of buried in
footnotes:

- **THE ARROW** reads **exactly 0** on any 0/1-valued (binary-thresholded)
  series, for every k, on the real series AND every shuffle/bootstrap draw --
  a pure algebraic identity (x²=x for binary x ⟹ a²b − ab² = ab − ab = 0). It
  is **blind to echo's raw record by construction**, not a null physics
  result.
- **THE CLOCK**'s autocorrelation-based ("during-role") fit is **exactly
  reversal-symmetric by construction** (`correlate(x,x)` does not see time
  direction) on every record it runs on. A forward/reversed AGREE on this
  method proves nothing about the record's physical time-arrow; it proves the
  ruler doesn't have one for this use.
- **THE SPLIT** needs ≥4-5 real peaks/cycles inside the window to fit
  anything at all -- monotone or flat series (echo's raw 0/1 decay-to-floor,
  a 6-point ant summary series) are **uncomputable by construction**, not a
  failed search.
