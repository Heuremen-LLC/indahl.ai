# THE DISCO — re-cutting THE LINEUP backwards, 2026-09-30

Wayfinder's hat (§43/43b, THIRD_RUNG_20260928.md): the wave goes backwards down the number line.
10 = SUBSTRATE (necessary, unseen: null/absence/excluded channel/reset/subtracted model).
9 = MESSAGE (what crossed: the choice or the arisen thing, with its number).
8 = CLOCK (the timing the record carried — including an explicit "no time structure" if that's what was measured).
7 = GRADIENT (did the witnesses relate — two+ detectors/chips/looks/runs, with the number).
Cells are filled ONLY from numbers the cited reports actually measured. No cell is invented; a record that
never addressed a dimension at all (not even to say "no time structure") gets that cell left empty, and if
that missing cell is 10 or 9-and-8 together (nothing crossed at all) the record CAN'T BE READ BACKWARDS.

Verdict rule: SHOWED UP = all four cells filled and they cohere (clock belongs to the message crossing that
substrate, witnesses agree). PARTIAL = a cell is filled but contradicts another, or one non-essential cell
(usually 7) is empty. CAN'T = no substrate cell, no message cell, or no clock cell anywhere in the report —
named as the absence itself, per the hat's own prediction that this is where the data would be.
