## 3 October

### The crossing and idle heating
- **What we said.** Heating while a qubit sits idle was a candidate cause of the crossing.
- **What the record showed.** Each push job's own idle arm (a qubit left in |0> for 300 us) reads +0.02 +- 0.15 % (run 121); across the three chips' push jobs it reads -0.43 to +0.26 % per 300 us. Measured against each stretch's own decay, the crossing is non-exponential decay.
- **What changed.** The status, Finding Two and the laws page now say nothing stands above the textbook mechanism yet. One arm (10-us |1> stretches) still reads +1.70 +- 0.64 %: one arm in ten, 7.6 % family-wise.

### One record, both directions
- **What we said.** "One record runs backwards as well as forwards" was listed as closed.
- **What the record showed.** The arrow statistic flips sign under reversal by identity, random noise included.
- **What changed.** It is now a math line and is not counted as a measurement.

### Seven numbers, traced
- **What we said.** Seven figures on the page.
- **What the record showed.** Tracing each to its read or ledger line found them wrong: Pittsburgh's crossing is three reads, not six; the 0.92 spectator was free-running, not frame-held; Finding Four's "~1.9" is a separation (+1.88 +- 1.16), not a ratio, so step two has no ratio on the chip; "0.59" was mislabelled (it is the halfway state's early clock over the |1> state's); two citation sets were wrong; "random groups of three" holds only for the nine-qubit runs.

- **What changed.** The page says "mean over qubits" and says where the model's rows came from. Dropped widths restored; neutron widths put back as published (stat / sys); a figure (0.760) that appears in no read was replaced by traced values.
