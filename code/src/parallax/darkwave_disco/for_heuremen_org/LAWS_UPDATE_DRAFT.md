# SIX PLUS NONE -- the framework, with what the data showed (draft for heuremen.org, 3 October 2026)
Each law keeps its light expression (observable, forward) and its dark expression (unobservable, backward). Under each one: what our
runs and our readings of other labs' records showed, marked MEASURED (ours, on qubits, with widths), OUTSIDE (another lab's record,
read by us), or READING (our interpretation). Seven -- the findings -- has its own page.

## 1. Propagation -- energy forward, structure backward
- Not yet tested by us. Nothing in our runs reaches a backward wave directly.

## 2. Finite Structure -- forward meets backward, a countable pattern falls out
- MEASURED: a free-running spectator qubit keeps 0.921 +- 0.004, 0.826 +- 0.005 and 0.760 +- 0.006 of its coherence through 3
  looks at other qubits, 9 looks and 30 us -- near eleven, ten and nine twelfths (0.917, 0.833, 0.750).
- READING: the steps are countable; whether they keep counting by twelfths is a call (27 looks -> 0.750).

## 3. The Oscillator -- four states, and every binary measurement discards half the system
- MEASURED: reading the raw I/Q signal instead of 0/1, a third level shows on a minority of qubits on IBM and on Rigetti, in three
  behaviours (standing, carried, blinking). On Rigetti it survives the reset and carries into the next shot; on IBM, shots don't talk
  (-0.002 +- 0.002).
- OUTSIDE: with the which-path device itself in superposition, a photon shows wave and particle together past the textbook limit of
  one: V^2 + D^2 = 1.428 +- 0.043 (Tang 2012).
- MATH: on a record cut to 0/1, the arrow statistic <x^2 x'> - <x x'^2> is zero by construction (x^2 = x) -- a binary readout can't
  show direction at all.
- READING: the half a 0/1 readout throws away is where the memory is.

## 4. State Change, Not Loss -- the absence is the data
- MEASURED: a qubit parked in |0> doesn't age -- per-qubit bookkeeping closes to 0.002 across three parking schemes.
- MEASURED: what the reset erases is what was placed (0.067 %); what arose on its own stays (+23 points at 20 us, gone by 500).
- MEASURED, after review: a qubit pushed ten times ends more |1> than a single decay clock allows (+2.0 to +2.9 % on three chips), but
  against each stretch's own decay most of that goes away -- it is mostly non-exponential decay. Heating while the qubit idles is
  ruled out by each job's own idle arm (+0.02 +- 0.15 % after 300 us in |0>). What's left is one arm in ten (7.6 % family-wise):
  nothing stands above the textbook mechanism yet.
- OUTSIDE: a free neutron's two decay witnesses disagree: counting protons gives 887.7 s, counting electrons 877.2 s (NIST; J-PARC).

## 5. Triangulation -- the instrument is the distance between observations
- MEASURED: one readout timed from three positions: the maker's stated 2.18 us, the spectator qubits' 2.37 +- 0.03 us, the measured
  qubit's own 3.5 +- 0.2 us. None of them is the error; the gaps between them are the measurement.
- MEASURED: a qubit's short-time and long-time decay sit 2.2-2.7 % apart at every push count -- two rulers, one qubit, one delta.
- METHOD: every error we caught was read from two positions and from the ruler itself before it was filed; the log is public.

## Plus None -- structurally required, unobservable from within, present but unmeasured
- MEASURED: a spectator qubit, after everything we know how to cancel is cancelled, keeps a leftover of +0.6 degrees.
- MEASURED: the inside never reads the outside's laid time -- a spectator's clock is blind to how long we waited.
- READING: the none is what every two-position measurement leaves over.
