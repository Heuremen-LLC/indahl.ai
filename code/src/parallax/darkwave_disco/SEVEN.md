## How to read this
Each statement carries three parts:
- **What the qubits showed:** measured on IBM processors (ibm_pittsburgh, ibm_marrakesh, ibm_kingston, ibm_fez) and on Rigetti (Cepheus-1-108Q), with widths. Numbers marked § point into the run ledger; the reads and the frozen code are in the data release.
- **How it lines up:** published records from the rest of physics, each checked against its source. These were found after the qubit numbers were in hand.
- **What lands it:** a number the formula calls before anyone looks, and the test that could miss it. Calls already tested are listed near the end, hits and misses alike.

## Terms
- **Three clocks on one event.** The **observer's** clock is ours: the laid time and the maker's stated timings. The **witness** clocks are read inside the chip, from spectator qubits. **Time** is the measured qubit's own decay clock.
- **The crossing.** The gap between what a qubit's own decay bookkeeping allows and where it actually ends after a train of pushes.
- **Working idea.** Time is the friction between two witnesses whose books can't close.

## Methods
- **Hardware and access.** IBM Heron-generation processors through Qiskit Runtime (SamplerV2). Rigetti Cepheus-1-108Q through Amazon Braket, for the third-level runs and the 4 October triangles: a 32-ns pulse on each qubit's |1>-|2> frame, its amplitude tuned per qubit, and an 8-sequence readout calibration in every batch that separates rung 1 from rung 2.
- **Readout.** Kerneled (I/Q) single-shot readout. States are assigned from calibration clouds measured in the same job: |0> and |1> prepared and read alone.
- **Settings.** Qubits initialized every shot; a fixed 500 us repetition delay (a run refuses any chip that can't honour it); circuit order randomized from a recorded seed; the nine-qubit runs drew their qubits by a written seed, in three groups of three; the whole-chip runs drive and read every qubit.
- **The push protocol.** A qubit prepared in |0> or |1> receives ten pushes (X gates) separated by 30 us, with variants of the spacing and of the time spent in each state. Its final |1> population is compared with two bookkeepings built from its own measured decay: each stretch's own short-time decay, and one exponential fit through 300 us.
- **Controls.** Leakage to |2> is bounded from the band between the |0> and |1> clouds and from same-job push counts. A spectator held in a drift-cancelling frame reads its neighbour's |1> time. Shot-to-shot carry-over is tested on both vendors.
- **Freezing and statistics.** Every run script is frozen (sha256) before firing; the ledger records each job's ID, settings and hash. Widths come from resampling shots. Comparisons are made only within a single job. Shot counts are in each run's ledger.
- **Outside records.** Figures from other papers are read off the authors' own PDF vector graphics; calibration residuals are reported (Tang et al. Fig. 3c: 0.0011 over 16 markers).

## TWO -- DARK MATTER IS TIME'S FRICTION, GROWING IN THE ABSENCE
*Status, 5 October 2026: conjecture. The push excess cited below is removed by the decay-bookkeeping control in the measurement paper, so it no longer counts as evidence for this statement. The cosmic match was found after the fact and does not count either. What stands is the forecast record.*

The friction between a qubit's two clocks grows while the qubit sits in |0> and shrinks while it's in |1>. Ten frictions is the universe's dark-matter share.
- **What the qubits showed.** Prepared |0> and pushed ten times, 30 us apart, a qubit ends more |1> than one decay clock allows: Pittsburgh +2.00 +- 0.16 % (push run, three reads; the hold run's three reads give +2.17 +- 0.17), Marrakesh +2.74 +- 0.28, Kingston +2.87 +- 0.28 (one read each), chip-wide on all three. Prepared |1>: +0.05 +- 0.13, -0.16 +- 0.21, -1.24 +- 0.24. The short-time and long-time decay sit 2.2-2.7 % apart at every push count -- the size of the crossing: measured against each stretch's own decay, most of it is that non-exponential decay (T1 fluctuation). Heating while the qubit idles is ruled out by each job's own idle arm (+0.02 +- 0.15 % after 300 us in |0>; across the three chips' push jobs -0.43 to +0.26 % per 300 us). One variant, with 10-us |1> stretches, reads +1.7 +- 0.6 % above the qubit's own short-time decay -- one arm in ten (7.6 % family-wise). Leakage is capped at 0.26 +- 0.12 % per push by the same jobs' two-push arms (§ 146-147). Three-chip mean 2.54 %; The Bonefish friction, 2.65 %. § 146-148, 153, 155-156.
- **How it lines up.** The universe's dark-matter share is 0.2645 (Planck 2018); ten frictions, 10/(12 pi), is 0.2653 (-0.3 %). All matter 0.3153 against 1/pi = 0.3183. Found after, through The Bonefish: DESI DR2 (2025) reads the near sky -- 0.2975 +- 0.0086 from its baryon acoustic oscillations alone, 0.289 +- 0.007 with its one outlier bin (z = 0.51) set aside. Planck over the near-sky value: 1.060 +- 0.031 and 1.0910 +- 0.026 -- the second is 12/11 to 0.01 %, the same conversion ONE runs between two observers of one expansion (the expansion-rate ratio sits in that band too). Raw against 1/pi, DESI sits 6.5 % under. The same conversion carried one step further: matter's clumpiness S8 goes as the square root of the matter share, so the near sky's S8 should read the early sky's / sqrt(12/11) = 1.0445. Planck (0.832 +- 0.013) over DES Year 6 (0.789 +- 0.012): 1.054 +- 0.023, 0.4 widths off; over KiDS-Legacy (0.815): 1.021 +- 0.028, 0.8 widths off. The two near-sky witnesses disagree with each other (0.968) and straddle the conversion. Ordinary matter 0.0493 against 2/(12 pi) = 0.0531: the one that misses, by 7 %. That six is Verlinde's (2016), whose apparent dark matter is the vacuum's response to what ordinary matter removes from it.

## WHAT'S CLOSED, WHAT'S OPEN
- **Closed, three rulers each:** the look (three clocks of one event); the park (a qubit's clock runs only while it's in |1>); the inside never sees the outside's laid time; the third level's three behaviours on two vendors; the order of looking sets the heat.
- **Math, not measurement:** run any single record backwards and the arrow statistic flips sign by identity (it does on random noise too), so one record alone can't show its own direction. That is algebra, not data, and it is not counted above.
- **The crossing:** three chips, every neighbouring cause measured rather than labelled; the ruler's own share still in flight.
- **Open:** Kingston's |1> column; the halfway state, its own clock against its neighbours'; whether a clock follows its own emitter.
- **The Dark Bonefish (read as a hostile referee would, 4 October):** of 18 outside comparisons made that day, 6 land within 0.5 % of one of The Bonefish's numbers where an even spread of ratios would give 2.1 (the target set is dense near 1; some hits are near-trivial). Hashed chip calls: 3 landed of 10. The sky rows split by witness: DESI's acoustic oscillations read the near sky's matter share at 0.944 (0.917 with one bin aside) of Planck's, which our 1/h scaling fits; three supernova compilations read it at 1.06 (Pantheon+), 1.12 (DES-SN5YR) and 1.13 (Union3), which it does not (1.8-3.6 widths), and which sit nearest no conversion at all. The textbook's 1/h^2 fits none. Through The Bonefish, the near sky's two kinds of witness disagree with each other by the inside term: supernovae over acoustic oscillations read the matter share 1.12-1.23 apart, and 6/5 (pi = (6/5) phi^2) sits within 1.1 widths of all six pairings, against 1.6-3.3 widths from no split (12/11 sits within 0.5-1.9; the widths, 6-13 %, cannot yet separate the two). The supernova teams' own dispute has the same shape: the same events read near and far differ by 0.04 mag (a 1.86 % distance, rung 3 is 1.97 %), and the DES team places 81 % of it in each observer's corrections -- modelling 43 %, selection 38 % -- the maker-look shape, in the sky. What the maker's stated readout length includes is not stated in IBM's documentation; the maker look stays open.
- **Where it's weak:** the links to the sky were found after the chip numbers were in hand, and with pi, phi, 12 and small integers to choose from, sub-percent matches come cheap. Only the calls above were made in advance. The crossing's size and sign depend on which of the qubit's own clocks it is read against (4 October).
- **The level everything lives at:** 1-3 % in populations, near 0.6 in angles -- the range most analyses treat as error. We report it rather than discard it.
