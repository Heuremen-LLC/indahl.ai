## Corrections and flags

- Nothing in 1 through 11 is wrong. Authors' lead name, title, journal, volume, article number or page, year and DOI all match Crossref.
- Item 2: Rep. Prog. Phys. uses a page-style number (124501), not an "article number" field. Citing it as 124501 is correct. Authors are Müller, Cole, Lisenfeld in that order, so "Müller et al." is the right lead.
- Item 7: first Crossref pass did not surface it, so it was resolved by DOI directly. Confirmed: McEwen, Kafri, Chen et al. (51 authors), Nat. Commun. 12, 1761, published 19 Mar 2021.
- Item 12: your suggested example, Ku et al. 2020, PRL 125, 200504 (doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.125.200504), is a real paper and your citation details are correct. But it is a transmon plus capacitively shunted flux qubit (opposite-sign anharmonicity), not two fixed-frequency transmons, so it does not fit the "fixed-frequency transmons" framing. Kandala et al. 2021 does (fixed-frequency, fixed-coupling transmons, ZZ engineered and suppressed). Use Kandala for transmon-transmon; keep Ku only if you want the opposite-anharmonicity route.
- Item 12 caveat: the Kandala bibliographic data is verified through Crossref and its arXiv abstract. The claim that ZZ was read out specifically by a Ramsey sequence on a spectator qubit was NOT checked in the full text; confirm in the methods section before wording that in the paper.
