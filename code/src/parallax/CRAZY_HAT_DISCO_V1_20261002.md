## 0. THE FLOOR
Everything we have lives at the 1-3% level in populations and around 0.6 in angles and ratios -- inside the margin of error.
The margin isn't the dirt under the data. It's the dance floor. A binary look throws it away; we kept it, and it keeps lining up.

## 1. THE FRAME IN ONE BREATH
Five parts plus none: the ARROW (the message), the CLOCK (energy as time), the SPLIT (outside minus inside: the substrate), 1/pi
(the inside calibration, the center), 1/phi (the outside calibration, the attractor), and -1/12 over the whole (the none: never resolves).
Six is the ruler: whoever's looking. The triangle: OBSERVER, WITNESS, TIME, with THE NONE over it. The corners are walls: a record
sits inside a wall, on it, or across it. WE FOUND IT (10/1): nothing can see its own sign, one witness alone closes into a circle,
and time is the friction between two witnesses whose books can't close.

## 3. RELATIVITY, ON A QUBIT (rhymes)
1. PROPER TIME IS TIME SPENT ON. The bit ages only while excited (the park, 0.002). An OFF bit rides a light-like line: no proper time passes.
2. THE LOOK IS A BOOST. The looked-at bit lives 3.5 us while the witnesses live 2.37: a factor 1.48 between the observed clock and the
   coordinate clocks. Backwards from the textbook twin: here the watched one ages MORE. From the bit's seat, everyone else moved.
3. NO GLOBAL NOW. Read in turn: the last one runs hottest. Read together: everyone coldest. Simultaneity is a special frame, and breaking
   it leaves heat. The slot, not the bit, sets the count (59 = 59, 66 = 66 across a reversed order).
4. THE TWINS. Two paths to the same state don't arrive the same age: OFF-start and ON-start pushes end on the same parity and differ by
   the crossing; the in-between reached from either side carries its path in its first 20 us.
5. LOCAL CLOCKS ONLY. Every bit carries two clocks, wanders alone in minutes, and no two neighbours wander together. The chip's published
   clock predicts only itself: a distant observer's record of a clock it can't see.
6. CAUSALITY IS THE WIRE. A witness sees only what it's coupled to; the link lives in what the information crosses (readout tiles,
   wired neighbours), not in the map. Light cones drawn by the wiring.
7. ENERGY CURVES TIME. Rung 2 bends a neighbour's clock ~200x harder than rung 1. More energy, more drag on the time next door.
8. A LOCAL INERTIAL FRAME, WITH A TIDE. The frame-held-still witness cancels its own drift and keeps only its emitter's pull. What's
   left over, +0.6 deg, is the tide the frame can't cancel: the six.
9. PROPER TIME IS NOT COORDINATE TIME. The inside clock reads none of the outside's laid duration (shared 0, per-run exactly 1:1 on the
   split's wall, the inside slope five orders down). The observer's minutes are real to the observer only.
10. THE ARROW NEEDS TWO. One record alone runs backwards perfectly (24 of 29 close within 1%). The order of events is only real between
   two witnesses -- like time order for causally linked events -- and NIST's remainder shows from the other seat only.

F. WHAT'S DISCARDED IS THE MEMORY. The third rung carries shot to shot on Rigetti; the in-between carries its path; the arisen outlives
