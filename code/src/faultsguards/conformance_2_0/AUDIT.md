# Conformance audit: every live desk against Faults and Guards 2.0

Read-only audit, 12 Nov 2026. Standard: faultsguards/standard_v1/STANDARD.md, version 2.0, section 6 and REQ-01 to REQ-13. No desk code, plan, page or ledger was edited and nothing was shipped. Machine-readable copy: AUDIT.json in this folder.

## Counts

Desks and surfaces audited: 60.

- CONFORMING: 9
- NEEDS_REASON_CODES: 22
- NEEDS_RULE_CHANGE: 25
- CANNOT_TELL: 4

No desk prints a closed CANNOT_TELL reason (STRADDLES_EDGE, TOO_WIDE_TO_RESOLVE, NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE): the three words appear nowhere under bones-forge outside the standard and its public mirrors. So no desk that assigns outcomes is CONFORMING today. The 9 CONFORMING rows assign no reading outcome at all (descriptive records, lists, mirrors).

## Method notes

- Outcomes under 2.0 were computed from the stored interval in each summary_*.json and the plan's own bounds, with section 6.4 order.
- Where a plan names two bounds, the ABOVE bound is taken as the size that matters and the CONSISTENT bound as the band edge, the way section 6.8 reads the worked example. Readings that move only under that choice are marked "depends on plan choice".
- Summary figures are as stored on 12 Nov 2026 14:00 to 15:10 UTC; desks keep running, so counts move.
- Desks not read in depth (record, vault, atlas maps, forecasters) are marked.

## Shared helper: heuremen_kit.py

- 45 copies exist, in 2 versions (43 from 29 Oct, 2 with the opener() 308 handler). 42 folders import it.
- Only cone, credit and wiki call K.classify. Every other desk imports constants and wilson() and writes its own ternary. A fix in the kit therefore reaches 3 desks directly and about 30 more only after they are moved onto it.
- classify() returns an outcome with no reason. It has no both-alternatives test and no size that matters. With one bound and no upper bound it prints CONSISTENT when the interval ends at or below the bound (that is BELOW under 2.0). Edges are closed only. A missing interval returns CANNOT_TELL without NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE. Its self-test checks that behaviour.
- NOTHING_RETURNED is only a constant. There is no helper for the REQ-13 block. Only wilson() is provided as an interval method.
