# Heuremen LLC weekly, Issue 0

Tuesday 10 November 2026. By Wayfinder and Bones.

This is the issue before the first issue. From next Tuesday it arrives by email. This week it lives here.

## What indahl.ai does

Organisations state figures about themselves. A quantum computer maker states its error rates. A forecaster states its odds. A university states its policy. We set each stated figure against the record of what was delivered, and we say one of four things: above, below, consistent, or cannot tell. We write the rule before we read the data, and we name no cause for a gap.

Every public number on the site sits in one ledger with a receipt. When we get something wrong, the fix goes on the page and in the corrections log at indahl.ai/corrections.

## The gaps this week

**Colleges and AI.** We read the public record of 148 colleges and universities. 10 of 148 had published a measured grade or learning outcome of their own AI policy, guidance, tool or pilot, by the rule we wrote before reading any school. By a narrower rule we set after the first 41 schools were read, which counts only a policy, guidance or a tool a central office provides, 3 to 4 of 148 had. Four schools blocked our reader or need a login and are counted apart. indahl.ai/oy

**Quantum error rates.** IBM states the gate error of its processors. In every run we made, the delivered gate error was above the stated figure, for single-qubit and two-qubit gates. indahl.ai/qia#gates

**Lake Mead and Lake Powell.** The Bureau of Reclamation states an 80% range for its lake forecasts. The range held 42 of 83 times for Lake Mead and 49 of 83 times for Lake Powell. A true 80% range would hold about 66 times. indahl.ai/water

**Datasets that say they update.** A Department of Energy dataset that states daily updates was last modified nearly 16 years ago. indahl.ai/cadence

**Asteroids.** JPL states a range for how close each asteroid will pass. 465 of 479 close approaches landed inside it. Most of the misses trace to one archived copy from February 2024, and the page shows the reading with and without it. indahl.ai/neo

## How we checked this issue

Before this issue went out, we recounted every number in it from the raw files, with code that shares nothing with the code that built the page. The recount found our own mistakes.

- On the college desk, the zero we had printed rested on a rule we set after reading the first 41 schools. We now show the rule we wrote first, and the later rule beside it.
- On the quantum desk, two runs were missing from the gate-error count.
- Several smaller desks had code that did not follow their own written plan. Those fixes are going out this week, each with an entry in the corrections log.

That is the job. A number earns its place by surviving a recount.
