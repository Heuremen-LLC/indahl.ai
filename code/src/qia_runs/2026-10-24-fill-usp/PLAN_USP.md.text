OUR DESK FINDING (qia_leads/audits/fresh_usp_2609.33255/KNOWN_GOOD.md): their 7 kingston batches are 3 stated records on 3 qubit sets, perfectly confounded:
A = batch 0 on (138,151,152,153), B = batches 1-3 on (140-143), C = batches 4-6 on (82,83,96,103). The Qiskit-PennyLane gap is present on A and B, absent on C.
So "a new calibration removed the gap" and "a different qubit set removed the gap" cannot be told apart from their data.

DESIGN (breaks the confound by holding the calibration FIXED):
- Their exact submitted circuits (manifest isa_qasm, read as data, rebuilt by our own parser usp.parse_isa; 60/60 equal to their op count, depth and 2-qubit-arity
  count, parse_check_usp.text): the 20 non-mutant entries (Qiskit, PennyLane, Cirq controls on cases 10, 11, 0111, 0110; the Qiskit null duplicate; readout rows)
  for each of A, B, C = 60 circuits, sent unchanged (level-0 transpile with identity layout leaves them equal; dry_run_usp.text).
- ONE job holds all three sets and all builders (calibration fixed within the job). Two copies: G1 (seeded shuffle, seed 20261024), G2 (same circuits, reversed
  order). 2048 shots per circuit (their 1024 x 2). Bracketed by our committed Part 0 sweep before (usp_pre) and after (usp_post): sweep_run/fire_sweep_rep.py,
  sweep.py unchanged (CODE_SWEEP.sha256).
- Backend record saved before every G job. One restart then HOLD on an IBM-side ERROR. Cap 160 s for the G jobs (estimate 73 s) + ~38 s of sweeps.

KNOWN-GOOD: their released counts run through THIS committed code (usp.py --known-good, known_good_usp.text) must reproduce their Table II (ibm_kingston, first valid batch) in all 16 cells to printed precision and their text's Qiskit-PennyLane gap on 0110 of 0.16 -- result: 16/16 PASS, +0.159; per record A +0.116, B +0.093, C +0.000 (mean of the 4q cases). Not a hardware known-good: the device record changed since 13 Sep, so today's values are not expected to equal theirs; reported as a bound (R0).

READINGS (four outcomes; 99 % band for every reading; parametric bootstrap over shots plus the within-job excess variance measured from their own built-in
duplicate (Qiskit control vs Qiskit null); copy gap G1 vs G2 -- a resolved copy gap makes that set CANNOT_TELL):
 R0  today's values against their batches, per set, as a bound (device change since 13 Sep; not scored).
 R1  per set S in {A, B, C}: gap_S = mean over the 4q cases (0110, 0111) of p(Qiskit) - p(PennyLane), both copies pooled.
 R2  contrasts gap_A - gap_C, gap_B - gap_C, gap_A - gap_B, and the pooled gap over the three sets.
     VERDICT (set dependence is claimed ONLY from a resolved contrast; a uniform gap is read from the pooled value):
       SET      A-C and B-C ABOVE, C not ABOVE: the qubit set carries the gap; a calibration change is not needed to explain their batches.
       BOTH     A-C and B-C ABOVE, C ABOVE too: a gap everywhere plus extra on A and B.
       BUILDER  no contrast resolves, pooled ABOVE: one gap common to all sets; "calibration/qubit choice removed it" is BELOW for today.
       NONE     no contrast resolves, pooled CONSISTENT: CONSISTENT with the gap being tied to their past record/placement; CANNOT_TELL between the two.
       OTHER_SET / REVERSED / CANNOT_TELL as printed.
 R3  supporting, descriptive: delivered vs stated sx per qubit and CZ per path coupler on the 12 qubits (sweep usp_pre and usp_post, same committed analysis),
     and whether today's delivered CZ ranking of A, B, C matches the stated ranking. No outcome claimed beyond agreement of the two sweeps.
 Secondary (printed, not scored): per-case gaps, Cirq vs the other two, null-vs-control differences, readout rows.

SYNTHETIC (usp.py --synth, synth_results_usp.text; worlds on their Table II baselines with a set offset sd 0.03 and per-circuit drift sd 0.008 between copies):

 Result: per-reading false calls 11/1800 = 0.6 %; VERDICT wrong 14/300 = 4.7 % (6 of the 14 are CANNOT_TELL from a resolved copy gap; false claims 8/300 = 2.7 %).
