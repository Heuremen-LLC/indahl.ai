## Readings (lums.analyse, per size; four outcomes; 99% bands)
CVaR_0.5 per row per job (his energies and normalisation). d_j = job mean deviation from the arm's row means.
- R1  DRIFT (O arm, no twirl): variance of d_j over the 6 O jobs against a shot-noise parametric null (each job x row re-drawn from the
      arm's pooled row distribution, his shots). ABOVE = job-to-job spread beyond shot noise without twirling. Excess job SD with an upper bound.
- R1b T ARM: the same on the 6 T jobs (drift and/or job-level twirl instance variance).
  ABOVE on R1/R1b -> CANNOT_TELL if either half of the run (jobs 1-6 / 7-12) sits below its own null median.
- R2  TWIRL, JOB LEVEL: var(d_T) - var(d_O), exact permutation test over all 924 6/6 relabelings of the 12 jobs (calibrated under drift,
      which both arms share).
- R2b TWIRL, ROW LEVEL: mean row-specific residual sum, T minus O, same permutation test.
- DECISION RULE for his question (per size, fixed now): CANNOT_TELL if R1 or R1b is CANNOT_TELL; else R1 ABOVE -> DRIFT (instance
  variance not separated; R2 reported); else R1b ABOVE and R2 not BELOW -> TWIRL-ONLY SPREAD; else NEITHER RESOLVED (bounds printed).
- R3 his single-pair shift (n=8 +0.0426, n=10 -0.0499) against today's T-arm job-mean SD (95% from chi2 on 5 dof; a pair has sqrt2 x the
  SD): ABOVE if |his shift| > 2.576 sqrt2 x upper; else CONSISTENT. Different day: this asks whether today's spread could produce his shift.
- Context only: mean CVaR today (T, O) vs his bps1 mean.
Resolvability scale fixed from HIS numbers: one-pair shift 0.0426 (n=8) = sqrt2 x a job SD of about 0.03 -> planted size 0.03.
