## Readings (analyze.py, committed; all pre-registered here)
- R1 (primary): k/n in the 10 lowest-energy states of 1024 (the "lowest 1%") vs uniform null 10/1024 = 0.009766; Clopper-Pearson 95%.
- R1b: share of the noiseless-QAOA excess (f-null)/(ideal-null) with CP ends (ideal: P 0.02177, S 0.01910 from known_good.json).
- R2: same k vs the product-of-own-marginals null (separates bit bias such as decay toward 0 from low-energy concentration).
- R3 (descriptive): mean QUBO energy of shots vs uniform mean (bootstrap 95%); lowest-energy sampled state, its rank; optimum sampled yes/no.
- Combined over P and S: same outcome -> that outcome; different -> CANNOT_TELL.

## Four outcomes (per variant, R1 read WITH R2)
- ABOVE: R1 lower CP end > 0.009766 AND R2 ABOVE -> the kingston sample concentrates on low-energy states beyond random and beyond its own bit bias. (R1 ABOVE with R2 CONSISTENT is reported as "ABOVE explained by bit bias", not as QAOA signal.)
- BELOW: R1 upper end < 0.009766 -> the sample avoids low-energy states (worse than random).
- CONSISTENT: null inside the interval -> print the bound: upper end / null and the upper excess share (how much of a perfect device's excess is ruled out).
- CANNOT_TELL: n < 4000, the noiseless ceiling would not itself read ABOVE at this n, the two variants disagree, or the job errors twice. Deciding run if so: same circuits, 4 x 4096 shots more per PUB, ~12 s.
