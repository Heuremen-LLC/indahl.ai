ADDENDUM 2 -- 30 Oct 2026, written after the data (a correction, zero QPU).
The public QIA line for 2605.30676 said: "Our kingston CZ delivered is 1.21 to 1.39 times stated; as an upper bound it closes about half of the
N=9 gap and widens the N=2 gap by about 40%." That is wrong. Recomputed with our own twin code (twin_cauca.py, the paper's twin, their record
theirs/properties_L2.json, their published hardware means), rescale_check.py -> rescale_check.text:
  CZ factor alone 1.21 to 1.39: closes 9 to 16% of the N=9 gap; moves the N=2 gap by about 1% (widens it, the twin sits below hardware at N=2).
  CZ 1.21 to 1.39 with single-qubit 1.306 to 1.366: closes 13 to 21% of the N=9 gap; widens the N=2 gap by about 2%.
This agrees with the PLAN's own figure (about 17%, 13 to 22%, "not half"). The "about half" line was carried into the public matrix from an
earlier estimate and never rechecked. Corrected line goes out through the ledger and ship.sh.
