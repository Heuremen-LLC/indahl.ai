# PLAN -- free calibration run for the Eritrea Institute of Technology teleportation benchmark (V. Rai, S. Farhat; Scientific Reports 2026, DOI 10.1038/s41598-026-63793-5; Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.20767111; ibm_fez). Written and committed BEFORE any job. Heuremen LLC / QIA, one-for-one side.
Source read: the accepted manuscript (audit: ~/bones-forge/qia_leads/audits/eritrea_20767111/AUDIT.md, BONES FINAL 24 Oct) and the Zenodo record (v1, MIT; teleportation-benchmark.zip md5 2580ad509cd629d60de0c046569e654f); copies and sha256 in theirs/ (raw_counts.json, main_calibration.json, job_metadata.json, circuits/*.qasm, processed_data/*.csv, README, and their code as text). Their code was never executed.
Why, verbatim: "The measured fidelity is left unconnected to device calibration. It is precisely this gap that we set out to fill." and Limitations: "the calibration was drawn from a single snapshot taken at execution time."
Their record: main run 14 Jun 2026, one job, one stamp (2026-06-14 16:47:45 UTC, submitted 27 minutes later); fez; qubits 0,1,2; 8,192 shots; optimization_level 1. Our run is 132 days later: every comparison below is the size of the quantity on this chip, today, for their circuit, not a finding about their 14 and 17 Jun runs.
Design (eritrea.py, fire_eritrea.py): their full-protocol dynamic teleportation circuit (q1 and q0 read out mid-circuit, X and Z corrections conditioned on them, q2 read in X, Y or Z), 4 angles (pi/6, pi/3, pi/2, 2pi/3: sin(theta) > 0.3) x 3 bases = 12 circuits, 8,192 shots (their count), physical qubits [0,1,2], optimization_level 1 as theirs, rebuilt from circuits/full_protocol.qasm and the Methods (their code not run).
Plus the Ramsey arm on the feedforward neighbour (physical q2, adjacent to q1): q0 and q1 in |1>, q2 in |+>; arm R = mid-circuit readout of q0 and q1 (two readouts), arm I = idle of the stated measure duration (1660 ns = 416 dt); q2 read in X and Y; both arms end with the same readout of all three qubits; 4 circuits, 4,900 shots.
All 16 circuits transpiled ONCE (seed_transpiler 42) and sent unchanged, same layout, in four jobs, order ABBA over circuit order: A1 (12 teleportation then 4 Ramsey) | B1 (reversed) | B2 (reversed) | A2 (A order). Each job is submitted after the previous finishes; the backend record (properties), the remaining seconds and the planned job are saved BEFORE every job and once after the last (props_eritrea_before_<job>.json, props_eritrea_after.json).
The model: their "device-informed noise model" rebuilt as an exact 8x8 density-matrix calculation of the same circuit pieces (depolarising channels from the stated gate errors, symmetric readout, no relaxation, as their README describes it), predicted from THE RECORD SAVED BEFORE THAT JOB with BOTH conversions: A = their convention (stated gate error passed straight in as the depolarising parameter), B = the correct one (the stated gate error is RB average infidelity: lambda = 2 r one qubit, r/0.75 two qubits). The calibration-to-fidelity link they asked for is measured the same day.
Readings (eritrea.py, fixed now; four outcomes each: ABOVE / BELOW / CONSISTENT / CANNOT_TELL; 99 % band, 0.5-99.5 percentile of the shot-noise null; signed):
  R1 LINK-A   mean over the 4 jobs of (hardware F - model F, convention A, that job's record). F = mean over the 4 angles of (1 + <X> sin + <Z> cos)/2, their definition.   R2 LINK-B   the same with convention B. Null: hardware drawn at the model's own expectations.
  R3 DRIFT    F(A2) - F(A1), identical circuits and order, jobs 1 and 4.   R4 KICK   (psi_R - psi_I)/2 in degrees per readout, psi = atan2(<Y>, <X>) of q2 in the Ramsey arm, pooled over the four jobs; two readouts in arm R.
  Reported, not scored: per-job D_j and per-job kick, STEP (jobs 3,4 minus 1,2)/2, SLOT (A minus B)/2, the stated stamp/sx/cz/readout of the four saved records, and the RECORD BRACKET (R1 and R2 again with each job modelled from the record saved AFTER it; if the outcome differs the reading is read as CANNOT_TELL as to the record).
  Resolvability scale fixed in advance from THEIR numbers (their fidelity 0.9454 from their raw counts, their model value 0.953, their 18 published fidelities, their raw full-protocol <X>,<Y>), never from ours: a reading is CANNOT_TELL if its null half-width exceeds the scale. S_F = |0.953 - 0.9454| = 0.0076 for R1, R2, R3; S_psi = |their pooled apparent Z-rotation of the received state, full protocol, four angles| = 1.79 deg for R4.
KNOWN-GOOD: their 18 published fidelities (3 modes x 6 angles; source_data_full.csv at 4 decimals, Table 2 at 3 decimals) must reproduce from their raw counts through eritrea.py, AND their model value 0.953 by their convention (A, their own 14 Jun stamp, exact density matrix) within 0.005, the tolerance their README states ("version-dependent to ~0.005") -- PASSED: 18/18 at 4 decimals, 18/18 at 3 decimals, mean full protocol 0.9454; model A full 0.9516 (|diff| 0.0014; no-entanglement 0.7544 vs 0.752, no-feedforward 0.5000 vs 0.496), known_good_eritrea.text.
Synthetic: synth_eritrea.py plants 12 worlds on their own stamped record, 300 runs each: NEITHER (hardware = converted model) / CONVENTION-A TRUE / GAP (6 % worse than both models) / DRIFT step / DRIFT linear / KICK only (-1.62 deg per readout, our fez full-pack size) / BOTH / JITTER (job offsets, neither linear nor step) / SMALL (below detection) / RECORD MOVES with hardware following it / RECORD MOVES with hardware ignoring it (their two stamps' CZ(1,2) 0.0362 and 0.0021, one per job) / LOW SHOTS (300 and 100: CANNOT_TELL). Expected outcomes scored by a rule fixed in the file; all 12 worlds PASS (synth_results_eritrea.text).
Floor, said plainly: NEITHER false rate per scored reading R2 LINK-B 1.7 %, R3 DRIFT 1.3 %, R4 KICK 1.0 % (R1 LINK-A 0.0 %: its expected outcome in NEITHER is BELOW, hardware sits below their-convention model; it did). The first design (2.5-97.5 band) gave 5.7 %, 4.3 %, 4.3 %; widened before any job (ADDENDUM_ERITREA_1.md.text; first-design output kept as synth_results_eritrea_band95.text).
Lessons applied: scale from their published numbers, ABBA arm order, every world planted incl. linear and step drift, job-level jitter, a moving record and a stale record, same transpiled circuits and layout in every job, record saved before each job, dry run first, NEITHER false rate measured and the band widened in advance.
What the Ramsey arm can and cannot say (/q 29): our dose rows on fez are -0.084 deg per readout (1 of 39 read), -0.133 (10 of 39), the dose here is 2 of 156 and the neighbour distance is one coupling; the null half-width is about 0.83 deg per readout, so a CONSISTENT is a bound of about 0.8 deg per readout, wider than the 0.15 deg dose bound; it resolves the full-pack size 1.62 deg (synth KICK world 99.7 %), not the dose size. The effect on their fidelity of any kick in this range is at most 8e-4 (audit section 3e).
Cost: dry run estimate 141.5 s of QPU time on Darkwave Disco (4 jobs x 35.4 s at 300 us per shot; dynamic-circuit shots may cost more), hard cap 200 s inside fire_eritrea.py (per-job max_execution_time 50 s; the next job is estimated at the larger of the plan and the last actual and refused if spent + next > cap), remaining checked before every job. Remaining at dry run: 1415 s.
Results go to the book (FILL or CLEAN entry) and, when the portfolio launches, to the authors as a free calibration. Nothing is published or sent before then. The run is BUILT, NOT FIRED.
