THEIR QUESTION, their words (theirs/paper_v2.txt): the working qubit is "particularly affected during the waiting intervals associated with ancilla
measurement and reset"; "Reducing these waiting times ... promising routes towards net heat extraction"; SM Table 1: "The simulation reproduces 70-90%
of the measured even part for T >= 40, and 33-72% for T <= 20", and "readout assignment and reset errors alone produce changes below 0.01 T". Their
noise model treats a measurement as 2.1 us of T1/T2 plus an assignment error: an idle with a label.
OUR QUESTION FOR THEM: does a readout cost the working qubit more than the waiting time it occupies (a readout is not an idle)?

DESIGN (ibm_kingston, Darkwave Disco; delta_B = 0.45; tau = 20 and 60, the short and the headline period):
  A   their circuits exactly (reset, R_y(prep), MEASURE b_in, XXPlusYY(2 varphi), measure b_out), 4 circuits x 3000 shots per tau.
  Ap  b_in drawn at random (bit 0 w.p. p_exc) and PREPARED as a basis state (reset, X if 1), then MEASURED, then XXPlusYY, measure b_out.
  Bp  identical to Ap but the incoming measurement is replaced by a delay of its scheduled length (545 dt = 2.18 us on kingston).
      Measuring a basis state ideally does nothing, so Ap = Bp ideally; Ap - Bp is what 4N = 192 incoming readouts cost beyond their waiting time.
  K = 12 realizations of the incoming bits per (direction, branch), 250 shots each, Ap_k and Bp_k on the SAME bits, next to each other in the job.
  Heat: Q = 2 B_j (b_in - b_out) per collision, their estimator (branch-weighted; odd = (fwd - rev)/2, even = (fwd + rev)/2), our own code.
  In R2 both Ap and Bp use the PREPARED b_in (Bp has no measured one); their common uncancelled b_out assignment bias cancels in the difference.
  Ap_rec = Ap scored with its MEASURED b_in (exactly their estimator) is the control against A.
  Two copies (jobs) of 200 circuits; copy 2 runs copy 1's order reversed (ABBA) with its own fresh realizations; same structure as the synthetic.
  Layout: their (47, 48, 46) unless, in the record saved before the job, a CZ error on S-aL or S-aR or a readout error on any of the three exceeds
  3x the chip median, or a T1/T2 is missing (then STOP and choose by addendum). Dry run 24 Oct: CZ 0.00321 / 0.00429 vs median 0.00174 (1.8x, 2.5x),
  readout 0.0098 / 0.0037 / 0.0060 vs median 0.0091: PASS.
  Transpile: their settings (optimization_level 3, seed_transpiler 0, fixed initial layout). CHECK 8 (equal idle): for every k the ASAP start times
  (target durations) of all 1320 operations on S are identical in Ap_k and Bp_k -- dry run PASS on all 96 pairs of both copies; Bp has 193
  measurements + 192 delays where Ap has 385 measurements; circuits 0.679-0.684 ms (theirs: about 0.69 ms). Same compiled circuits are fired.
  Estimate 134 s (2 x 67 s; shots x (duration + 250 us)); cap 220 s; remaining 3,430 s at the dry run. Record saved before every job; one restart,
  then HOLD, on an IBM-side ERROR.

KNOWN-GOOD: their released raw ancilla bits (12 ibm_kingston jobs, Sept 2026) through OUR independent estimator and OUR own implementation of their model (geopump.py, known_good.py) must reproduce their published odd heats to <= 1e-8 and their SM Table 1 hardware even-part noise contributions to the printed 2 decimals. Result (known_good_geopump.text): max |odd diff| 4.3e-11 over 12 jobs (e.g. tau 60 job dap8pvopqrnc739b0oi0 QL_odd +0.280493 vs +0.28049), Table 1 even rows 6/6 matched: PASS. A hardware known-good on today's device is not possible (the device changed since Sept). Our committed kingston readout kick is not measured by any arm here (see DOSE below).

DOSE (coordinator /q check 9, 24 Oct, before any job): their circuit reads ONE ancilla at a time, so our kick applies at dose K = 1: on kingston at most
0.09 deg per readout (main.tex: "one of 39 read out leaves at most 0.15 deg on fez and marrakesh and 0.09 deg on kingston"), NOT the full-pack 2.86 deg.
Across 385 readouts that is at most ~35 deg, and only if every kick adds coherently with the same sign. No prediction or synthetic world is scaled from
the full-pack value. AND (measured on the synthetic, 24 Oct): the heat is essentially blind to phase-type costs on S -- dephasing up to 10 % or a
coherent kick up to 5 deg per incoming readout moves Qtot_even by < 0.03 T. So this run decides POPULATION-changing readout costs (extra relaxation or
excitation of S, or readout-induced transitions of the ancilla beyond the idle), and says nothing about the phase kick; that stays CANNOT_TELL here.

READINGS (all four outcomes; bootstrap over shots, realizations resampled jointly for Ap, Ap_rec and Bp; 2,000 draws; 99 % band):
  R2 PRIMARY  Ap - Bp, Qtot_even = QL_even + QR_even, at tau 20 and at tau 60 (two primary readings). Secondary: QL_even, QR_even, QL_odd, QR_odd.
  R4 CONTROL  A - Ap_rec, every key: random-basis preparation equivalent to their protocol (expected CONSISTENT).
  R1          A today minus their published value at the same point (their tau-scan job at tau 20 and tau 60, their raw bits through our estimator,
              their bootstrap width added): device change since Sept, reported as a bound.
  R3          PROJECTION, labelled: 2 x R2 even part per bath (all 384 ancilla readouts, assuming the outgoing ones cost the same, linear) divided by
              their missing even part (SM Table 1 hw - sim: tau 20 L 0.83, R 0.48; tau 60 L 0.36, R 1.35 T). Not a measurement of their device or date.
  DIAGNOSTIC  incoming-readout assignment P(1|0), P(0|1) on aL, aR from Ap (recorded vs prepared b_in), beside the stated values in the saved record.
  FINAL per reading: CANNOT_TELL if the copy-gap interval excludes 0; otherwise the outcome of the pooled interval (ABOVE / BELOW / CONSISTENT, a
  CONSISTENT printed as its bound). A missing copy makes every reading CANNOT_TELL.

SYNTHETIC (synth_geopump.py; shot-by-shot trajectories of S through the published circuit with T1/T2 of q47 over each collision interval and

  false-call rate over every reading where the truth in the heat is zero (all of W0 and W2, R4 in every world): mean 0.002, max 0.025 per reading;
  W1 and W3 called ABOVE 40/40 on every even-part R2 reading at both taus; W2 CONSISTENT 40/40; W4 called on 0-3 of 40 (below resolution, as planted).
  First catch on the synthetic (before any job): Ap scored with PREPARED b_in is biased against A by ~-1.1 T per bath (b_out assignment error no

  Loader test: simulated bits written in the job format (meta + raw npz) through run_real.py recover a planted W1 as ABOVE, R4 CONSISTENT.
