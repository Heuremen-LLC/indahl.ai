# PLAN -- free calibration run for arXiv:2607.20377 (R. Sipakov, KNUBA Kyiv; ibm_fez). Written and committed BEFORE any job. Heuremen LLC / QIA, one-for-one side.
Why: the author writes (Sec. 5) that "any drift in device conditions over that interval is a competing explanation for the observed ordering that the
recorded data cannot exclude"; layout, seed and calibration table "were not persisted". Our record: on fez, 15.4 % of qubits show between-job
variance beyond shot noise within 4.5 min (presence, not size). This run measures the size for THEIR circuits.
Design: their 300 public H0 circuits (identical logical circuits to H2), transpiled ONCE on ibm_fez (optimization_level=1, seed_transpiler=24),
sent unchanged in four jobs in the order H0, H2, H0, H2, 1024 shots per circuit (their shot count), their runtime options per regime.
Each job is submitted after the previous one finishes. The backend record is saved before every job and after the last; the layout is saved.
Readings (kyiv.py, fixed now; four outcomes each, 95 %, shot-noise null by binomial resampling, 2000 draws):
  R1 DRIFT: dH0 = RMSE(H0, job 3) - RMSE(H0, job 1). ABOVE/BELOW if outside the shot-noise null; CONSISTENT inside (bound printed);
     CANNOT_TELL if the null half-width exceeds their published H0-H2 gap 0.0451.
  R2 ORDERING: RMSE(H0 job k) - RMSE(H2 job k+1) for both pairs vs null; and |dH0| / 0.0451 = the share of their gap job-to-job drift covers.
  Neither R1 nor R2 is a finding about their 9 May 2026 jobs: it is the size of the drift term on this chip, today, for their circuits.
KNOWN-GOOD: the author's published raw counts (hardware_results/zz4_H*_raw_results.json) must reproduce their published RMSE H0 0.08777, H1 0.08643, H2 0.04273 to 1e-4 through kyiv.py -- PASSED (R3 line in synth output and `python3 kyiv.py`).
Synthetic: synth_kyiv.py plants TWIRL / DRIFT / BOTH / NEITHER worlds built from the author's own kernels; all four return the expected outcomes
(synth_results_kyiv.text). One rule was fixed on synthetic data before any job: R1's resolvability scale is their PUBLISHED gap, not the measured one.
Cost: ~336 QPU s on Darkwave Disco (their jobs billed 80, 80, 84 s). Remaining at plan time: 2244 s.
