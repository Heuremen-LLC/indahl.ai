## What the run reads
The Ang circuit (their Sec. II B 1 + II C 1): RX angle embedding of two L1-rescaled (sum = pi) 9-tone tuning curves on qubits 0-8 and 9-17, CNOT(i -> 9+i), H on 0-8, all 18 measured; estimate F^ = mean over shots of (-1)^(sum_i s_i t_i). Exact value of this circuit |<a|b>|^2 = prod_i cos^2((a_i - b_i)/2) (checked against a statevector of their own released qasm, 0.873645 for pair 23-32). Circuits rebuilt by MY builder (swaprun.build) from their released rescaled tuning curves; it equals their released qasm2 instruction-for-instruction in 2926/2926 pairs (known_good_swap.text).
20 pairs (swaprun.select, deterministic, seed 2508168950): IDENT 4 identical pairs a = b (neurons 37, 50, 104, 419; exact 1); ORTHO 4 CONSTRUCTED orthogonal pairs (delta tuning curves, pi on one tone each, different tones; exact 0; NOT their data, labelled so); THEIRS 12 of their distinct neuron pairs (the 6 lowest exact overlaps 0.445-0.571 and 6 at exact-overlap quantiles 10/28/46/64/82/97 %: 0.848-0.995).
ONE job on ibm_kingston: 40 circuits = 20 pairs x 2 copies, order copy 1 pairs 0..19 then copy 2 pairs 19..0; each pair transpiled ONCE (preset level 1, seed_transpiler 1689, layout chosen by the transpiler, matching "default settings"; dry run: depth 8-10, 9 CZ each, theirs depth 8, 9 CZ) and the SAME transpiled circuit sent for both copies. SamplerV2, 1000 shots (theirs), twirling OFF (gates and measure), dynamical decoupling OFF, no other mitigation (SamplerV2 defaults are the same; stated explicitly). Layouts, depth and ops saved in job_swap.json.

## Scored readings (swaprun.readings, fixed now; units = pairs; copy-averaged per pair; positive = hardware above exact)
  IDENT   mean over 4 pairs of F^ - 1.            Scale S = 0.10.
  ORTHO   mean over 4 pairs of F^ - 0.            S = 0.10.
  THEIRS  mean over 12 pairs of F^ - exact.       S = 0.10.
  COPY    mean over 20 pairs of copy 2 - copy 1 (within-job drift; copy 2 runs later in the job).  S = 0.05.
  SE = max(sd over pairs / sqrt(n), sqrt(mean per-pair shot variance (1 - F^2)/(1000 x 2) / n)); band t(n-1) two-sided 99 %.
  Outcomes: ABOVE lo > 0; BELOW hi < 0; CONSISTENT 0 inside and half-width <= S (print the bound); CANNOT_TELL otherwise, or any circuit returns < 900 shots, or the job fails.
  S = 0.10 is set so a CONSISTENT bound excludes a deviation the size of their own 2025 kingston data (identical-pair F^ median 0.853, i.e. -0.147; known_good_swap.text).
Descriptive (not scored): per-pair F^ and exact; ratio F^/exact; Spearman(F^, exact) over the 12 THEIRS pairs (ordering, which is what their Mantel statistics use); today's values beside their 2025 values for the same 16 pairs (their column un-scaled, below). Today vs 2025 is a drift reading across ~14 months and records, never a replication of their date.

## Book meaning of the four outcomes (the paper reports no hardware accuracy; its hardware claim is a rank correlation)
  BELOW on IDENT and THEIRS (the 2025 pattern): kingston's swap estimate is compressed toward 0 at their circuit; their "+IBM" metric is a shrunk version of the exact overlap, so "hardware more similar to classical" is the exact answer showing through noise (see the free reading below), not a hardware property. ABOVE on ORTHO with BELOW on IDENT = BOTH (compression toward a constant). CONSISTENT everywhere = kingston delivers the exact overlap within the printed bound today. CANNOT_TELL = wider than S; the deciding run is more pairs/shots (cost printed with the result).

## Free reading (zero QPU, from their released files; defined after reading their data, labelled post-hoc, not scored)
  1. Their released Ang+IBM column is 1/2 + S/2^19 with S an integer in 2926/2926 rows (S = sum of (-1)^(s.t) x counts, range 366..906 at 1000 shots): circuit_postprocess.swap_expectation_dict rescales any count array with a value > 1 by 2^18 = 262144 instead of the 1000 shots, so the stored "fidelity" is (1 + 0.0038 F^)/2 = 0.5007..0.5017 instead of (1 + F^)/2. Rank-preserving (their Mantel statistics are unchanged by it); the absolute values and the Ang+IBM heatmap scale are not. Un-scaled, their 2025 F^ minus exact: median -0.142 [2.5-97.5 %: -0.194, -0.064]; identical pairs median 0.853 (n = 76).
  2. Their simulator Ang column (results_simulator_ang.csv) is NOT the output of their released Ang circuit: Spearman +0.116 against the circuit's exact overlap; max |diff| from (1 + exact)/2 = 0.268 (pair 23-32: theirs 0.9961, exact (1+F)/2 0.9368). Cause not identified (their simulation notebook output not re-run).
  3. The exact overlap of the Ang circuit is rank-identical to Euclidean distance between the same rescaled curves (Spearman -0.999999 over 2850 pairs). So for Ang, "quantum metrics on simulators are weakly correlated with classical" (their Sec. IV B) rests on the simulator column of item 2; the circuit itself, computed exactly, is a monotone function of their Euclidean baseline.
  4. Released classical_fidelity.csv is byte-identical to results_euclidean.csv (sha256 ac70ebd5...), so "Euclidean and Fidelity (r = 0.994)" and "Fidelity and Angle+IBM (r = 0.702)" cannot be recomputed from the release (mine 1.000 and 0.712 on the duplicate).

KNOWN-GOOD: their published numbers reproduced from their released data files (src/theirs/, data only) through my committed known_good.py: 8 of the 10 Mantel statistics printed in Sec. IV B reproduced to the printed digit (0.488, 0.349, 0.039, 0.145, 0.030, 0.880, 0.919, 0.904 -> mine 0.4879, 0.3493, 0.0386, 0.1454, 0.0305, 0.8796, 0.9188, 0.9041); the 2 misses (0.994 and 0.702) both use the "Fidelity" file, which the release ships as a byte-copy of the Euclidean file (free reading 4), so they are not reproducible from the release, not a failure of the code.
