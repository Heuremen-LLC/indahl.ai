### S6 AI-detector stance
- Senate [pdf] p.7: "GenAI detection tools are increasingly less accurate; there are no validated GenAI detection tools."
- RTL news, 1 May 2025 [html]: "Based on the findings from the pilot, along with numerous publications and studies, UC Berkeley has opted out of the use of Turnitin's AI writing detection tool."
- RTL Transparency [html]: "We do not recommend that instructors rely upon AI detection tools to identify usage for writing" ; third-party detectors "could lead to FERPA, privacy, and copyright violations".
- Turnitin similarity checking remains available, opt-in via bCourses (https://rtl.berkeley.edu/services-programs/academic-integrity).
