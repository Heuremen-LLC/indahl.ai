## S0 self-evaluation: MEASURED at course-pilot level (CFE/Lenovo-funded pilots, reports posted by CFE); institution-wide policy/guidance: USAGE-OR-ATTITUDE ONLY
- Pilot, comparison, no effect found - RELI 121 (https://cfe.unc.edu/ochoa_reli-121-cfe-lenovo-gen-ai-study-summary/, file is a .docx): "The study did not detect a statistically significant difference in either reading comprehension or speaking confidence because of AI-Driven Reading Engagement Assignments." Design: staggered rollout across 12 recitations, 208 enrolled; study n not stated ("small participant numbers"). Outcome = self-report survey.
- Pilot, randomized - PHCY 732 (https://cfe.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/1381/2025/06/Noel_Manuscript_GenAI-CFE-Grant_FINAL-DRAFT.docx): "An experimental design was employed involving 129 third-year Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) students. Participants were randomized into two groups: self-generated (SG) feedback and AI-assisted (AI) feedback." Outcome = peer-feedback comment quality (353 comments coded).
- Pilot, pre/post - EDUC 685 (https://cfe.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/1381/2026/05/Cross-Francis_Nguyen-Project-Report.docx): "Fifteen PSTs participated in five rounds of interactions, with the first and fifth rounds serving as the pre- and post-assessments".
- Assessment validity, AI vs human scorer - PHCY OSCE (https://cfe.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/1381/2026/05/Morbitzer-Project-Report.docx): "The final dataset included 389 unique OSCE recordings and 136 rubric items, resulting in 17,592 scored rubric items." Result: "all models tended to score students more harshly than human evaluators."
- Pilot, pre/post self-report - MEJO 379 (https://cfe.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/1381/2025/06/GenAI379_summary_report_final.docx): "40 students reported significantly higher confidence in using GenAI for research from pre-semester (M = 3.25, SD = 1.17) to post-semester (M = 4.08, SD = 0.97)" (confidence = attitude measure).
- Tool pilot, ratings only, no comparison - COMP423 TA Forklift (https://cfe.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/1381/2026/05/Jordan-Project-Summary.pdf): "The analysis drew on 50 grading-response records, including both Likert-scale ratings and open-ended comments."
- Institution level (Provost's AI Committee surveys) = usage/attitude, goals stated as (https://ai.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/1362/2025/09/AI_surveys_exec_summary.pdf p.3): "establish baseline data around campus use of AI tools, assess AI support needs, sample stakeholder attitudes about AI and related issues". ## Bones hand check (25 Oct): Noel et al. draft (CFE-hosted .docx, read)
- 129 PharmD students randomized SG vs AI-assisted peer feedback; outcome = coded feedback quality (TGAP) and perceptions (FPQ), not a
  learning outcome; affect difference "did not reach statistical significance (chi2(1) = 2.97, p = 0.085)".
- Funding verbatim: "This study was funded as part of a Center for Faculty Excellence Lenovo grant at the University of North Carolina at
  Chapel Hill." (company money via a university grant program - noted on the row; pilot stays in the pilot column, flagged).
- Uptake: use required ("All students were asked to complete the survey as a required part of the course"; AI group "utilized generative AI").
- Noel quality result (referee, from the draft text): Kruskal-Wallis H(1) = 27.3, 89.3, 86.8, all p < 0.001 on the three TGAP criteria;
  129 randomized, 112 consented (55 SG / 57 AI), 353 comments analysed (162 / 191).
