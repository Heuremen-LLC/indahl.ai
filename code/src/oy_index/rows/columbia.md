## S6 AI-detector stance
- Quick-start FAQ: "Generative AI detectors are notoriously unreliable, easily tricked, and they evaluate student work inequitably (Liang, et al, 2023). Faculty are discouraged from using AI detectors for the purpose of detecting academic integrity violations."
- Provost policy (Wayback): "a report from an AI detection tool confirming such use can not be used as the sole piece of proof in a Dean's Discipline case to find a student responsible for a violation of policy." and "AI detection should be treated as a guideline and not a grading metric."
- Understanding AI: "AI detection tools have significant limitations and should not be the primary mechanism for enforcing integrity (Elkhatat et al., 2023)."
- Promoting Academic Integrity: "AI-detection tools are not foolproof, and it is not recommended to rely exclusively on these resources."
