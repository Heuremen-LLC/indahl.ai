## S6 AI-detector stance
- Guidelines (spring 2026), https://ovpe.tufts.edu/initiatives/ai/ai-guidelines/ : "Because automated AI detection is unreliable and can produce harmful false positives that undermine trust, it is strongly discouraged as a primary basis for academic integrity determinations."
- Guidelines item 4: "instructors using AI to generate feedback, to grade, or who upload student work to AI tools or detectors should disclose these uses to students"
- Report p.16: "There were also reports of faculty interested in AI detectors, despite their unreliability." Report p.16 (faculty survey): "16% have adopted monitoring software."
- CELT Fall 2026: "If you use an AI detector, treat its result as one additional source of information alongside the student's explanation and other evidence of their learning, not as sufficient evidence on its own."
- Operational (news pointer only, Tufts Daily 2025-02, https://www.tuftsdaily.com/article/2025/02/could-an-ai-generated-essay-get-an-a ): "they do not have access to the version that has AI detection." No tufts.edu primary page found stating the Turnitin AI setting.
