## S6 AI-detector stance
- FAQ (Provost): "The Office of the Provost and IT's Academic Technology group do not consider any current AI detectors that work well enough to recommend their use or license them on behalf of the university. In 2023, NYU formally disabled Turnitin's AI-writing detection feature"
- Adapting page: "NYU does not license or endorse AI-writing detectors for academic integrity decisions."
- A&S page: "NYU does not license AI detection tools because of their proven lack of efficacy." and "Instructors who use AI detection tools on their own do so at the risk of violating policies on student data and privacy."
- NYUAD CTL: "Note that there are currently no software sources able to reliably detect the use of generative AI."
- Faculty record (S7, NYUAD 2023): "OpenAI's Text Classifier misclassifies 5% of student submissions as AI-generated, and 49% of ChatGPT's submissions as human-generated. GPTZero has a higher false positive rate (18%), but a lower false-negative rate (32%)." (the guidance pages do not cite this study)
