# THAT SCHOOL PASS - small private schools, by hand (Bones, 25 Oct, his word "take the pass now ... no agents ... small guys")
Procedure per school (same for all): (1) one standard web search "<school> generative AI students faculty study OR survey OR results OR assessment"; (2) open any hit from the school's own domain or a named faculty paper; (3) log: finding / evidence level / status (LEAD, CARD, WALL ROW, NOTHING FOUND) ; (4) nothing is a finding until the source is read. Rounds of 4 searches (leapfrog); a school with a lead is read before the next round starts.
