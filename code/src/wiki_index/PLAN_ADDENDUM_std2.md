# WIKI: plan addendum for Faults and Guards 2.0, 12 November 2026

This addendum is written before any reading under the new outcome rule. PLAN.md and its addenda are not edited. The frame, the sources, the matching rule, the bins, the known-good checks and the hand checks are unchanged. Only the outcome rule, the way a cannot tell is named, the counts beside the headline and the edge convention change. Readings recorded on 10 November stand under the rules they named.

## Section 1, the size reading
- Quantity: the gap of a cited figure, (cited minus current) over current, with the interval from the written precision that PLAN.md fixes.
- Size that matters: 1 percent of the source's current figure, as in PLAN.md. The null is a gap of zero. The band is minus 1 percent to plus 1 percent. The plan's one number is both the size that matters and the band edge, and this addendum says so.
- Edge convention: closed at both ends, as the plan reads it. Above means the whole interval is over plus 1 percent, below means it is under minus 1 percent, and within 1 percent means the whole interval lies inside the closed band.
- Method and level: the interval comes from the written precision of the cited figure, not from sampling, so there is no level, no draw and no seed. The Wilson and country-resampled intervals on shares (95 percent, 2,000 draws, seed 7) are descriptive and do not decide an outcome, as before.
- The row is read with heuremen_kit read(lo, hi, band, matters, null, direction both). The test of both alternatives applies, because the plan names the size. An interval that holds zero and also holds a gap of 1 percent in either direction reads CANNOT_TELL with reason TOO_WIDE_TO_RESOLVE. An interval that crosses an edge of the band without holding zero reads STRADDLES_EDGE. No source figure, a source figure of zero, and a failed gate read NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE.
- The plain cause the plan gave stays beside the code.

## Section 2, a stated figure against delivered data
- The stated figure is a band from the word rules of PLAN.md, the delivered value is an interval from the lowest to the highest month in the window or a single count read today. The outcome words keep the plan's direction, the stated figure relative to the delivered value. Above means the stated band lies wholly above the delivered interval. The kit reads the delivered interval against the band, so the kit's ABOVE and BELOW are swapped to the plan's direction. This is stated so that no recorded outcome changes its meaning.
- A two-sided band, a plain figure or nearly X, is read with read(band). Inside is CONSISTENT. An overlap without containment is CANNOT_TELL with reason STRADDLES_EDGE. No delivered data is CANNOT_TELL with reason NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE.
- A floor, over X, X plus, and X and counting, is a one-sided bound. It is read with read(bound), which gives only above, below or CANNOT_TELL and never CONSISTENT. Over X is open at X and the others are closed. A delivered figure above the floor reads below in the plan's direction, since the stated floor lies below the delivered figure. A delivered figure exactly on a closed floor reads CANNOT_TELL with reason STRADDLES_EDGE. The claim holds in the first case and the page says so in words.
- A delivered single count is an exact read with no sampling, an interval of zero width under owner decision O1.
- A figure across several definitions or moments reads the outcome they share. When they differ the figure is CANNOT_TELL with reason STRADDLES_EDGE. When every definition is CANNOT_TELL the figure carries their shared reason. Volunteers, which no public count can deduplicate, and the DSA figures themselves, which cannot be rebuilt from public data, are CANNOT_TELL with reason NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE, because the planned quantity cannot be formed.

## The DSA checks
Check 1, rounding, counts the cells that are not whole thousands against zero with no tolerance, so it prints the count and no outcome word. Check 2, sums, is an exact read of the difference between the EU total and the sum of the member states inside the stated band of 13,500 either way, with closed edges. Check 3, cross-filing, compares published values for equality and prints no outcome word. Check 4, the bound, is a one-sided bound, read with read_exact(bound). The stated EU devices above the global devices read ABOVE and below read BELOW, and equality prints the difference only. A family-window with no global series is CANNOT_TELL with reason NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE.

## Failed reads and the counts beside the headline
SOURCE_UNREADABLE is the status NOTHING_RETURNED of a failed read of a source. It is listed apart and the page prints its count. The headline share keeps the bins as PLAN.md defines them, so its denominator still holds those rows, and the page says so in the line beside it. Beside the headline the page prints the REQ-13 line: the NOTHING_RETURNED count, then the CANNOT_TELL share of the size reading split by reason. A failed known-good check gives CANNOT_TELL with reason NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE for every outcome and keeps the original beside it.

## What flips
No bin, no count of a bin and no Section 1 outcome count changes unless a size reading touches an edge exactly, and the re-run reports it. The floors, over 67 million articles, 300 or more languages and 114 million files and counting, move from CONSISTENT to below in the plan's direction, which is the kit's one-sided reading of a delivered figure above a floor. The DSA bound check moves from CONSISTENT to below in the same way. The rounding check keeps its count and loses the word CONSISTENT. The reasons and the REQ-13 line are new.
