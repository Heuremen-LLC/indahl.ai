\section{The card's number, rerun}
Model cards print benchmark numbers, often without the harness, prompts or extraction rule that produced them. For three open-weight models
(SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct, Phi-4-mini-instruct, Granite 4.1 3B) we reran on our Mac, with the original weights, every card number that has a matching
task in EleutherAI's lm-evaluation-harness, with the claim list and the outcome rule committed before any run. Of 17 claims read, 5 are consistent
with our interval and 12 cannot tell, because the card's harness differs from ours or is not named. The gaps behind those twelve are not small:
Phi-4-mini's card gives ARC-Challenge 83.7 and OpenBookQA 79.2 from Microsoft's internal platform, and our log-likelihood reading gives 58.0 and
46.8. A gap that size can come from the question format alone, which is why it is printed as cannot tell with its direction beside it, not as
below. Our first pass on one card did print a below and an above. The plan's own harness clause, applied at the hand recount, moved both to cannot
tell. The extraction rule also moves the number on the same replies: on Phi-4-mini's chat-format GSM8K replies, strict matching gives 56.3 and
flexible extraction 81.8, and hand-reading ten replies, strict matching missed two correct answers that did not end in the expected phrase. Granite
4.1's chat-format BBH replies were sometimes cut by the task's own blank-line stop before the answer.
