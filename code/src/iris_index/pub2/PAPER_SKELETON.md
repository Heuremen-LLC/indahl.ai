## Abstract (write last, from the tables only)

## 1 Introduction
Published LLM evaluations release per-item outputs more often than not. Read back through their own files, their numbers mostly rebuild;
what moves is the apparatus around the model and the ruler around the number. We also turn the same checks on our own earlier record.

## 2 Method
2.1 Selection: arXiv cs.CL, 11-22 Oct 2026; repos with per-item outputs; acknowledgements read by hand; exclusions as stated (no new work
    for defence or government-agency funded studies, companies indexed only). Every candidate, its status and reason in a table.
2.2 Recount: our own code on their released files, never theirs; outcome per number (rebuilds / above / below / cannot tell).
2.3 Pricing: a question committed before the data is read; a known-good copy wherever the release has one; four outcomes; post-hoc
    readings labelled in place.
