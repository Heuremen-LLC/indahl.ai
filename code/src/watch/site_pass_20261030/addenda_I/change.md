
## Addendum 3 (30 Oct 2026, after publication, from the site pass)
The reader skipped dates written with a three-letter month (Dec 11, 2026) and dates written with a non-breaking hyphen, so 41 OpenAI rows were missing from the first reading on, among them 12 shutdowns inside the next 60 days. It also read the rows of OpenAI's two-column Date and Update tables as shutdown dates, although most describe an announcement or another event. The reader now reads both month forms and the hyphen. In a Date and Update table only a row whose text says it will shut down carries a shutdown date, a row that says announced carries a deprecation date, and any other row keeps no date.
The rows the old reader missed or misread were set from the pages saved at the first reading on 29 Oct (fixtures/), which contain them, so they enter as part of the first reading and no NEW or date-change line was written for them. The store went from 281 to 322 rows and the saved OpenAI page now parses to 154 rows. fixtures/EXPECTED.json was updated to match and gained two OpenAI rows, one with an abbreviated month.
The lifespan table now also shows the Google rows with preview in the name apart from the rows without it. Every Google row that ran under 180 days at this reading is a preview name. The rows in the upcoming table now carry the address of the vendor page they were read from. No rule of the test changed.
