## Terms and Privacy versions: what the gate does when they change

- `/agree` POST (gate.js `agree()`) refuses unless the NDA, Privacy and Terms boxes are all checked and both legal boxes were read to the end. It signs the `ig_a` cookie (2 hours) with `nda: NDA.version` (from MUTUAL_NDA.md "DRAFT v"), `privacy: PRIVACY.version` (PRIVACY_VERSION in build_site.py, exported through pages.js) and `terms: '<literal in gate.js>'`.
- Nothing compares versions afterwards. `agreement()` checks signature, expiry and `k === 'agree'` only. inquire.js copies `ag.nda` and `ag.privacy` into the stored record and the notification mail. `ag.terms` is never read, so the Terms version is not in the inquiry record at all (needs Bones: add `terms: ag.terms` to `rec.agreements` in inquire.js if the record should show it).
- So a bump forces no re-acceptance and invalidates no cookie. A browser holding a live cookie keeps the old version numbers for up to 2 hours and its inquiry record shows the version it really accepted. New acceptances carry the new numbers.
- Bumped: `PRIVACY_VERSION` 2026-10-29 to 2026-10-30 (build_site.py), Terms `'2026-10-26'` to `'2026-10-30'` (gate.js). The old Terms value was already behind the page ("Effective 28 October"). Both pages now say "Updated 30 October 2026 ..." under the date, which the Privacy page promises for material changes.
