\section{Methods}

\subsection{Devices, roles and the look}
Two IBM Heron processors, ibm\_kingston and ibm\_marrakesh (156 qubits each). On each we reuse the roles of our earlier control run
\cite{readout}: a pack $E$ of 39 qubits whose mid-circuit readout is the \emph{look}, and the 117 qubits $S$ that are never measured or
gated during a look, which we call untouched. A look step reads out all of $E$ with the default \texttt{measure}. Its idle counterpart holds
every qubit of $E$ idle for exactly the scheduled readout length $L$ (545\,dt on ibm\_kingston, 671\,dt on ibm\_marrakesh). Barriers on all qubits
surround every step, and the untouched qubits receive no gates during a step.

\subsection{The correction}
After every look step each untouched target $q$ receives a virtual $R_z(-k_q)$, where $k_q$ is its kick in degrees per readout. A virtual
$R_z$ is a change of frame and takes no time. Every untouched qubit is corrected with its own point value and none is selected or dropped
by the size of its kick. Two tables are used. The committed table comes from our control run of 21 October, ten days before this run, read with
the committed estimator (phase $\phi=\mathrm{atan2}(\langle Y\rangle,\langle X\rangle)$, kick $=(\phi_M-\phi_D)/N$) and unwrapped per qubit
across $N=2,4,8$ readouts of that run so that a total phase near $180^\circ$ is not read on the wrong branch. The fresh table is measured in a
calibration job on the same chip minutes before the main jobs (idle and look, $N=4$ and $8$, $X$ and $Y$, 4{,}000 shots). Each table is applied
at the dose it was read at ($N=4$ or $N=8$).

\subsection{Arms}
Six arms run interleaved in the same job: idle (D), idle with a symmetric echo (DE), look (M), look with the committed correction (MC), look with
the fresh correction (MF), and look with the echo (ME). The echo is an $X$ on every untouched target after step $N/2$ and after step $N$, a net
identity. It also refocuses each qubit's own precession during the idle, so the echo arm is compared with the idle-echo arm and every other
look arm with the plain idle. Scheduled durations on the untouched qubits are identical for D, M, MC and MF, and for DE and ME (the two $X$
gates add 16\,dt on ibm\_kingston and 18\,dt on ibm\_marrakesh).

\subsection{Setups}
\emph{Spectators.} All 117 untouched qubits are put on the equator ($\sqrt{X}$), see $N=8$ steps, and are read in $X$, in $Y$, and after
$\sqrt{X}^\dagger$ in $Z$, where a qubit that kept its state reads 0 (2{,}000 shots). \emph{Pairs.} 48 coupled untouched pairs are prepared in
$|\Phi^+\rangle$ (the remaining 21 untouched qubits on the equator), see $N=4$ steps and are read in $ZZ$, $XX$, $YY$, $XY$, $YX$ (2{,}500 shots).
\emph{Clock.} A Page--Wootters history state of two clock qubits $c_0,c_1$ and a system qubit $s$,
$|\Psi\rangle=\tfrac12\sum_{t=0}^{3}|t\rangle\otimes R_y(t\theta)|0\rangle$ with $t=c_0+2c_1$ and $\theta=\pi/4$, is built as $H$ on both clock
qubits, a controlled $R_y(\theta)$ from $c_0$ to $s$, the look block ($N=4$), and a controlled $R_y(2\theta)$ from $c_1$ to $s$. The look sits in
the middle of the build, so a phase it leaves on $s$ is acted on by the second controlled rotation and cannot be removed afterwards. All 27
Pauli settings are read (1{,}000 shots). The clock qubits are $(c_0,s,c_1)=(3,16,23)$ on ibm\_kingston, as in our run of 24 October, and
$(104,105,106)$ on ibm\_marrakesh, chosen by a rule fixed before any job from the committed table and the day's calibration record.

\subsection{Jobs, readings and rulers}
Per chip: the calibration job, then the main job with the 210 circuits in order and again reversed, giving two copies. Dynamical decoupling and
twirling are off; circuits are transpiled once with the trivial layout. Every classical register is saved per shot, including the pack's own
readouts. The plan, code, synthetic test and their hashes were fixed and timestamped before the first job. Each reading has four outcomes
(above, below, consistent, cannot tell), is read on each copy and pooled, and is reported as the pooled outcome only when the two copies agree.
Spectator and pair readings use the median over qubits (pairs) with a 2{,}000-draw bootstrap over qubits and a 99\,\% band; clock readings use a
400-draw shot bootstrap and a 99\,\% band. The share of the kick that a correction gives back is read with a per-qubit regression of the residual
phase on the kick measured in the other copy (Theil--Sen slope): a slope of 0 means all of it is given back, 1 means none.
