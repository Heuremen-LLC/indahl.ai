Stated against delivered: Google Cloud regions

Google states a grid carbon intensity for each Cloud region, defined as the average operational gross emissions per unit of energy from the grid. EPA records CO2 per megawatthour of electricity generated in each state. Both are in grams of CO2 per kilowatthour here. The ratio is Google's figure divided by EPA's. The rule was committed before the comparison: consistent from 0.75 to 1.25, above over 1.25, below under 0.75. The band is wide because Google's figure is CO2 equivalent, EPA's is CO2 only, and a data center buys power across state lines.
