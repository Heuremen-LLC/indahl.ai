T1/T2 idle errors at opt 0) and the delivered <Z> is extrapolated linearly to score 0. Their 50-qubit QIC r=1 on ibm_torino: raw 0.9329 on the best
layout (their 'unmitigated', run with TREX + twirling + DD), FF-ZNE 0.96687; paper prints mitigated deviations of '~6%' (notebook: 7.1%, r=3 omitted).
