                 How to read precipitation forecasts

File Names:

prec01_forecast_above.bi - Archive of lead 01 seasonal above normal
precipitation probabilities
prec01_forecast_below.bi - Archive of lead 01 seasonal below normal
precipitation probabilities
prec01_forecast.bi - Archive of lead 01 seasonal precipitation
categories

Beginning of Record: Seasonal forecast issued in December 1994

Type of File: Gridded Binary File

Grid Resolution: 2x2

Grid Domain: 130W to 60W longitude, 20N to 56N latitude

Grid Values: 1-below normal;2-near normal;3-above normal;0-equal
chances  (categorical files)

Note: Probability files are expressed in total probabilities... not
probability anomalies.
