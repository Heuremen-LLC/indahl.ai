Estimator (onset.py, own code from the method description; used on his raw IQ for the known-good and on ours): per qubit and job, symbols 0/1/2 from the job's own R0/R1 clouds, 2-state HMM (enter a, leave b<=0.5, EM, 3 symbols), present if Delta-BIC>10 and the state reads other than 0 at least 25 % of the time; onset = a*present per cycle; lifetime = -T/ln(1-b).
Readings (abramson.py, fixed now; units: the TEN qubits; % onset per CZ per cycle, his unit; four outcomes ABOVE / BELOW / CONSISTENT / CANNOT_TELL):
  R1  B minus A: median over qubits of ((on_B1+on_B2)-(on_A1+on_A2))/2/cz_i. ABBA cancels LINEAR drift; a single step between jobs leaves up to half its size in R1, so R1's outcome is read net of guard = (|R2|+h2)/2.
  R2  DRIFT, A1 vs A2: median over qubits of (on_A2-on_A1)/cz_i (same units; positive = onset rose over the run).
  Shot-noise null (both): pool the four jobs' symbol records per qubit, shuffle whole shots, split in four, refit, recompute; 40 draws; h = t(39,.975)*sd. ABOVE: s-guard>h; BELOW: s+guard<-h; CONSISTENT: inside and h+guard<=S; CANNOT_TELL: inside and wider than S, or fewer than 8 of 10 qubits 'present' in any job (censored near the floor).
  Resolvability scale S = 0.09 % per CZ, fixed in advance from his published per-CZ figure (a bound narrower than S can exclude his effect). Not from our data.
  Descriptive, not scored: per-qubit signs (how many of ten qubits agree), B1 vs B2, lifetimes per arm, onset per job. No mechanism is named: a resolved B-A says the CZ-containing circuit differs from the reference by that onset under ABBA, not why.
