# PLAN -- free calibration run for arXiv:2605.30676 (Diaz Agreda et al., Universidad del Cauca; ibm_kingston). Written and committed BEFORE any job. Heuremen LLC / QIA, one-for-one side.
Why: the authors write that their digital twin (built from job.properties()) "does not reproduce non-local effects such as crosstalk or temporal calibration drift" (Sec. 2.3) and that
"the discrepancies with hardware are systematic rather than random" (Sec. 5.2); all four of their jobs "were carried out in a single experimental session" (drift controlled by assumption, not measured).
This run measures the drift term, with a size, for THEIR circuits on the same chip, and builds a twin from the record saved right before each job.
ECR vs CZ (checked, zero QPU, TWIN_RESCALE.md in the audit folder): the paper says the native two-qubit gate is ECR and queries gate_error("ecr",...); ibm_kingston's target is CZ (basis cz,id,rz,sx,x; no ecr in their
properties.json or in the target today; their submitted circuits hold cz, 2+6+...+30 = Table 1). Their released twin code attaches the two-qubit error only to "cz". So the twin's two-qubit input is the CZ stated gate_error, the figure our CZ
delivered/stated factors (1.30, 1.31) are measured against. fire_cauca.py asserts cz present and ecr absent in the live target and aborts otherwise.
Two twins exist: the released notebook (ZZ residual, readout matrix swapped) reproduces their released figures_data twin columns to 0.0 but is NOT Table A1; the paper's twin (ZZ probability = stated gate_error, thermal relaxation,
IBM-convention readout) reproduces Table A1 (L2) to <= 0.0035. This run uses the paper's twin. Their L2 record had qubit 1 prob_meas0_prep1 0.103 / prob_meas1_prep0 0.219 although their hardware N=2 target reads 0.9647; with that entry replaced the
small-N twin gap is ~0 (TWIN_RESCALE.md s.6). Our rescale of the twin by the delivered factors removes ~17 % of the N=9 gap (13-22 %), not half. Those are projections; this run measures.
Design: their SUBMITTED transpiled circuits (job_metadata run_03 = optimization level 2, identical to level 3; run_01 = level 0), unchanged, N=2..9 on physical qubits 0..N-1, first 12 of 20 identical repetitions per N (96 circuits),
2048 shots (theirs), default SamplerV2 options (theirs were {}). Four jobs in the order A B B A (A = L2 circuits, B = L0 circuits), each submitted after the previous finishes. The backend record is saved before every job and after the last.
Readings (cauca.py, fixed now; four outcomes each, 95 %, shot-noise null by binomial resampling, 2000 draws; P_j(N) = fraction reading all ones):
  R1 DRIFT  dA(N) = P_4(N) - P_1(N), same circuits, different time. R2 LEVEL  g(N) = mean(P_1,P_4) - mean(P_2,P_3) = L2 minus L0 (ABBA cancels drift linear in job number and a step at the midpoint).
  R4 TWIN   G(N) = mean over jobs 1,4 of [twin(record saved before that job) - P_job(N)] against their published gap g0(N) = Table A1 twin - raw (-0.093 ... +0.160).  R5 SHARE |dA|/S_gap printed.
  Resolvability scale fixed in advance from THEIR published numbers: S_gap(N) = |g0(N)| (R1, R4), S_lvl(N) = |published raw L2 - raw L0| (R2); CANNOT_TELL where the null half-width exceeds that N's scale.
  Every reading is the size of the term on this chip today for their circuits; none is a finding about their 29 Apr 2026 jobs. Multiplicity: 8 N per reading, ~5 % per cell nominal.
KNOWN-GOOD: their released raw counts (measurement_results L2 and L0) must reproduce their published raw means (figures_data / Table A1: 0.9647 ... 0.5075 for L2, 0.9641 ... 0.4615 for L0) to 1e-4 at every N through cauca.py -- PASSED (max diff 0.0, known_good_cauca.text); and the twin code must reproduce their Table A1 twin 0.8721 ... 0.6676 to <= 0.0045 from their released properties -- PASSED (max 0.0035, audit src/known_good.out).
Synthetic: synth_cauca.py plants STABLE, LINEAR DRIFT, STEP DRIFT, NEITHER, TWIN GAP GONE and Q1-READOUT-FIXED worlds from their own published means; expected outcomes are derived from the planted truth against the fixed scales; all six pass every scored cell (synth_results_cauca.text); 300-seed NEITHER floor: 0 of 4200 resolvable R1/R2 cells non-CONSISTENT.
Known limits written before the run: R1 compares job 1 with job 4 (~4 job-lengths apart), not their 4-5 min spacing; one interval, n = 1; the nominal jobs are small shots (24,576 per N per job) so N<=3 level readings are CANNOT_TELL by the scale rule; the twin is a model of the record, not of the chip.
Cost: ~52 s per job est (0.264 ms/shot from the Kyiv audit rate, not measured on kingston), 4 jobs ~208 s est, cap 300 s; remaining at plan time 1719 s on Darkwave Disco (shared with other runs). Dry run only so far; nothing fired.
