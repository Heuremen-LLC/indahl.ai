# PLAN -- free calibration run for QFIM-bench (Thinakaran P., Parthiban K., School of Computer Science and Engineering, Vellore Institute of Technology, India; ibm_marrakesh). Written and committed BEFORE any job. Heuremen LLC / QIA, one-for-one side.
Source read: github.com/Thina45/QFIM-bench tag v1.1.0 (commit c8eadce0c20af586d9196849a47e3688aec4f3a2, Apache-2.0) and Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23396363 (zip tree identical to the checkout); copies and sha256 in theirs/. There is no paper; the words below are from their review/ files (saved in theirs/). Their code was never executed.
Why, verbatim: review/OPERATING_POINT_ADDENDUM.md: "the simulator being a single calibration snapshot of a device that drifts." review/CLAIMS_MAP.md do-not-claim list: "Anything about day-to-day drift (all runs in one sitting, deviation D1), other devices, other layouts, or scaling beyond n=5." review/OPEN_ISSUES.md item 10: "The 95% intervals in the feasibility sweep and the fidelity fit cover shot noise only".
All five of their jobs share one calibration stamp (2026-10-08 17:49:07+05:30; files named day1..day5, jobs db3p2kklf4us73c1mp30 ... db3p6do4qg6s73c14veg), so the spread they publish is a between-JOB spread under one stamp, not a day-to-day one. Their logged job time is 20.0 s.
Our record: on fez, 15.4 % of qubits show between-job variance beyond shot noise within 4.5 min (a presence rate, no size); T2 on marrakesh moved ~3.5 sigma beyond shot noise between jobs minutes apart. Neither is a size for these circuits, which is what this run measures.
Design: their Tier A job = 8 circuits (ladder r0..r4, echo, readout zeros, readout ones; n=3, M=1, mcz oracle, uniform start, matched diffusion, marked state '011'; echo and readout keys '000', '000'/'111' as theirs), rebuilt from their published construction (theirs/src/circuit.py, ladder_predictions.py; ideal probabilities of my rebuild equal their published ideals) and transpiled ONCE
on ibm_marrakesh (optimization_level 3, seed_transpiler 42, physical qubits [5,6,7] for ladder/echo and [6,98,153] for r0 and the readouts, as in their manifest), 8192 shots, dynamical decoupling XpXm, sent unchanged in four jobs. Order ABBA over circuit order: A1 (their day-1 order) | B1 (reversed) | B2 (reversed) | A2 (their day-1 order).
Each job is submitted after the previous finishes; backend properties, remaining seconds and the planned job are saved before every job. Layout saved. Not a copy of their transpile: today's compiler is qiskit 2.5.2 (theirs 2.1.2); depth and 2Q count both equal their published ones for 3 of 8 circuits (the three without a two-qubit gate), the 2Q count is equal for 7 of 8 (r1 +1), depth is 4-6 higher on the five deep circuits (dry_run_qfim.text). One compile, identical in all four jobs, so the between-job reading is unaffected; their absolute means are not directly comparable (R4 says so).
ABBA: contrasts STEP (second half minus first half) and SLOT (A minus B) are orthogonal to each other's confound by construction; the job-sd mixes everything.
Readings (qfim.py, fixed now; four outcomes each, 95 %, shot-noise null = same statistic on 2000 binomial redraws at the pooled probability; rule = value outside the null range):
  R1 JOB-SD pooled RMS of the 4-job sd over 8 circuits (R1c per circuit, reported, not scored)   R2 STEP   R3 SLOT   R4 SINCE (today vs their 8 Oct means: different calibration stamp, not a drift reading)   SHARE = R1 / their RMS published sd 0.00509, next to its shot floor.
  Resolvability scale fixed in advance from THEIR published sds (analysis.json): a pooled reading is CANNOT_TELL if its null half-width exceeds 0.00509 (RMS of their eight published sds); R1c per circuit against that circuit's published sd. Our measured values are never used for it.
  None of this is a finding about their 8 Oct jobs: it is the size of the between-job term on this chip, today, for their circuits. Their published sd/binomial ratios (post-hoc look, not a result) are a reason to run, not a finding, and are not used.
KNOWN-GOOD: their published raw counts (theirs/raw, day1..day5.json from Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23396363, repo tag v1.1.0) must reproduce their eight published means and sds (analysis.json: e.g. echo 0.670972 sd 0.009316, r2 0.795923 sd 0.005497) to the printed 6 decimals, through qfim.py -- PASSED 8/8 means, 8/8 sds, max difference 0.0 (known_good_qfim.text).
Synthetic: synth_qfim.py plants NEITHER / EFFECT only (slot) / DRIFT step / DRIFT linear / BOTH / JITTER (job-level offsets) / SMALL linear / LOW SHOTS worlds from their own published means, 40 runs each, expected outcomes scored by a rule fixed in the file; all eight worlds pass (synth_results_qfim.text). Floor, said plainly: in NEITHER, R1 made 6 false calls in 40 (85 % as expected, the pass line); R2 4 in 40; R3 2; R4 2. One synth defect was fixed before any job: R4's first version treated their sample means as the truth (8 false BELOW in 40); their five jobs are now redrawn from the truth in each run.
Lessons applied: scale from their published numbers, ABBA arm order, every world planted incl. linear and step drift and job-level jitter, same transpiled circuits and layout in every job, record saved before each job, dry run first.
Cost: dry run estimate 80 QPU s on Darkwave Disco (4 jobs x 20.0 s, their logged time), hard cap 300 s inside fire_qfim.py (per-job max_execution_time 40 s), remaining checked before every job. Remaining at plan time: 1697 s.
Results go to the book (FILL or CLEAN entry) and, when the portfolio launches, to the authors as a free calibration. Nothing is published or sent before then. The run is BUILT, NOT FIRED.
