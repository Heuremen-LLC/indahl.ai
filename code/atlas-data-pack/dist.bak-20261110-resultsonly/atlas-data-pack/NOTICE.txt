  Epoch values are as of the pull date, 2026-10-28. Epoch has updated some sites since.
  Changes by Heuremen LLC: the pack keeps only the rows with Country = United States (77 of the 95 rows in Epoch's file at the pull) and only these fields: name, operator, mw, h100e, capex_usd_b and address. Epoch's Name became name and Epoch's Owner became operator. Empty or zero values in mw, h100e and capex_usd_b are blank. The owner text was cut at " #", which removes the tag Epoch puts after the owner (#confident, #likely or #speculative) from 69 rows. 7 rows have an empty owner in Epoch's file and stay blank, as for QTS Cedar Rapids. No owner in the file was only a tag. Trailing spaces were stripped from the address. The columns lat, lon, placed (the text the geocoder matched) and pulled were added. In the CSV and GeoJSON files the placed field is split into place_method and place_match. The JSON keeps placed. Coordinates were added by geocoding the address, with the US Census geocoder and then OpenStreetMap Nominatim. One geocoded point (Google Papillion) was replaced by a Nominatim point after the geocoder placed it in the wrong state. The scale files add yearly energy and cost figures computed from Epoch's stated power and EIA prices.
  Where a file holds both Epoch fields and OpenStreetMap coordinates, both licences apply.

California Data Exchange Center (CDEC), California Department of Water Resources

endoflife.date
  Release, support and end-of-life dates in json/support.json and the csv/support_ files. https://endoflife.date/ . MIT License, Copyright 2020 endoflife.date contributors. The full licence text is in LICENSE.txt.

Vendor pages
  The CHANGE files carry names, dates and links from the deprecation pages of Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and Cloudflare. The UPTIME files carry status indicators and incident titles from vendor status pages. These keep their own terms.

Tranco
  The SECRECORDS files name domains from the Tranco list. https://tranco-list.eu/ . Le Pochat et al., NDSS 2019.
