Why: the author writes (Sec. 7.4) that his 95% interval "comes from a basic (pivotal) multinomial bootstrap of the counts, so it reflects shot noise within each job rather than drift between jobs."
Our record: on fez, 15.4 % of qubits show between-job variance beyond shot noise within 4.5 min (a presence rate, no size); T2 on marrakesh moved ~3.5 sigma beyond shot noise between jobs minutes apart.
This run measures the size of the between-job term for HIS circuits: his protocol P (both ZNE scale factors in one job) against the same 20 circuits split across two jobs S.
Design: his 10 held-out QAOA MaxCut graphs (seeds 606..1506, n=5, p=1) at noise scales 1 and 3, rebuilt from his published construction without running his code (theirs/src), transpiled ONCE
on ibm_marrakesh (optimization_level=0, routing seed per circuit chosen to reproduce his published gate counts: 20 of 20 match, physical qubits 0-4), sent unchanged in six jobs, 4000 shots per circuit (his count),
order ABBA over arms: P1 | S1 (l=1 then l=3) | S2 (l=3 then l=1) | P2. Each job is submitted after the previous finishes; backend properties and the planned job are saved before every job. Layout saved.
Readings (lima.py, fixed now; four outcomes each, 95 %, shot-noise null = same statistic on resamples of the pooled counts, 2000 draws; rule = value outside the null range):
  R1 DRIFT  MAE(l=1, P2) - MAE(l=1, P1).   R2 SPLIT  A, B = S1, S2 ZNE estimate minus the mean of P1, P2, mean over graphs; R2C = (A+B)/2 (cancels drift linear in job number and a midpoint step).
  R3 GAIN   gain(P2) - gain(P1), gain = MAE(l=1) - MAE(ZNE).   R4 SHARE  D_k = mean per-graph |ZNE(S_k) - ZNE(P mean)| as a share of HIS PUBLISHED GAIN 0.06445 - 0.02810 = 0.03635, next to its shot-noise floor.
  Resolvability scale fixed in advance from the author's PUBLISHED number: a reading is CANNOT_TELL if its null half-width exceeds 0.03635. The measured gain is never used for that.
