SamplerV2, twirling OFF (gates and measure), dynamical decoupling OFF, max_execution_time 30 s, shots per PUB, no post-selection. Backend record (properties refresh) saved before the job and after. ## Readings (src/readings.py, fixed now; units = the 10 instances; errors log10 |E - E0|, E0 by exact diagonalisation, censored at 1e-10)
Solvers (src/assqd.py, mine): K = 50, B = 20, T = 10 (paper); tau = 1e-5, eps = 1e-6 (main.py); Random SQD candidates from all of S, AS-SQD from the dominant support (Alg. 1). Random SQD averaged over 5 expansion seeds per instance (the triage's "5-seed spread").
D1 = mean_i [AS(trot_i) - mean_k RND_k(trot_i)]  -- their hardware claim: AS-SQD beats random expansion on hardware samples (negative = AS better).
D2 = mean_i [AS(trot_i) - AS(ctrl_i)]           -- what the paper did not measure: do the Trotter circuit's samples help AS-SQD more than a no-gate control?
Interval: two-sided t(9) at 97.5 % over the 10 instances (two scored readings, ~5 % family-wise). Per reading: BELOW if hi < 0; ABOVE if lo > 0; CONSISTENT if the interval sits inside +-M, M = 0.5 decade (a factor 3.2 in energy error), print the bound; else CANNOT_TELL.
Four outcomes (the triage's, headline): FILL = D1 BELOW and D2 BELOW (AS-SQD beats random on hardware samples AND the samples beat a no-gate control); GAP = D1 ABOVE (AS-SQD worse than random expansion on hardware samples); CLEAN = D1 CONSISTENT (equal to random expansion within the bound); CANNOT_TELL = D1 CANNOT_TELL, or D1 BELOW while D2 is not BELOW (the no-gate control alone does as well; D2 is then printed as its own reading with its bound -- a D2 CONSISTENT says the hardware circuit's samples add nothing beyond +-0.5 decade over a no-gate control at n=12).
Descriptive, not scored: the paper's 5 seeds alone (t(4)); Standard SQD; AS vs RND on the control arm; AS from a classical uniform draw and from the noiseless rebuilt circuit (zero QPU); top-50 ground-state weight per arm vs 50/4096; Jaccard overlap of the two arms' top-50; weight in the 6-ones (ground) sector vs binomial 0.226; distinct strings; median absolute errors.
KNOWN-GOOD: none possible for their hardware numbers -- no counts, job ids or tabulated values are released (Fig. 3 is a plot; the repo holds code only). Substitute seats, both through my committed code: (a) zero QPU, src/known_good.py -> known_good_2603.13536.text: my H equals Qiskit's SparsePauliOp matrix (max diff 3.6e-15 at n=12), and the paper's PRINTED statements of its exact-contamination protocol (main.py seeds, 2000 and 3000 shots) reproduce: n=8 Random and AS-SQD near machine precision (median 3e-14 / 1e-14), AS < Random < Standard at n=10 and 12 (n=12, 3000 shots: 0.38 / 2.0 / 2.7), Standard growing with n -- PASS (rule fixed in the file before it ran); (b) real hardware, in this job: readout seats |0..0>/|1..1> on the 12 ring qubits; median over qubits of measured/stated readout_error (record pulled before the job) must sit in [0.5, 2.0] (Part 0: pittsburgh readout CONSISTENT with stated). If (b) fails, the chip is "not decided" and every reading is CANNOT_TELL (rule not relaxed after the result, /q 18).
Zero-QPU facts found while building (known_good (3), (4); defined after I looked, labelled, descriptive): H conserves total Z, so AS-SQD is confined to the Z-sector of its restricted ground vector; from 3000 uniform strings at n=8 it reaches E0 in 2 of 5 seeds (others 1.6-3.4), so their n=8 hardware "~1e-14" is reachable by dimension (250 of 256) but not guaranteed by it. The noiseless rebuilt circuit carries no ground-state information at n=12: its overlap sum_b p(b)|psi0(b)|^2 is 0.00020-0.00036 against uniform 0.00024, and its ground-sector weight is exactly binomial 0.2256 (the circuit conserves Z from |+>^12).
Synthetic (src/synth.py -> synth_results_2603.13536.text; 900 runs, Pool(4)): worlds NEITHER (D1 planted null = AS replaced by 5 other random-expansion seeds; both arms uniform + 1 %/2 % readout), H1 (D1 real; D2 null), H2 (D1 null; trot informative = 50 % paper's contaminated ground 0.8 p0 + 0.2 p1, 50 % uniform), BOTH, ASYM (apparatus artefact: both arms with 2 %/10 % readout asymmetry), ANTI (trot on high-diagonal-energy strings), IDEAL (noiseless rebuilt circuits). Results: null false-resolution D1 NEITHER 2.0 %, H2 2.0 %, ASYM 1.5 %; D2 NEITHER 2.0 %, H1 3.0 %, ASYM 1.5 %; worst 3.0 % (target <= ~5 %). Power: D1 resolves BELOW 100 % in H1/BOTH/IDEAL (truth -0.72 to -0.82 decade); D2 BELOW 100 % in H2/BOTH (truth -0.51); D2 ABOVE 100 % in ANTI; wrong-direction calls <= 1 %. Headline false calls: NEITHER GAP 1.5 %, H1 FILL 2 %. ALL WORLDS PASS. What it cannot do: D2's median half-width is 0.38-0.42 decade, so a D2 CONSISTENT comes out in 41-54 % of null runs (CANNOT_TELL the rest); an effect under ~0.3 decade is not resolvable. The analysis script itself ran on planted hardware-format output (synth_analyze_dryrun_2603.13536.text).
