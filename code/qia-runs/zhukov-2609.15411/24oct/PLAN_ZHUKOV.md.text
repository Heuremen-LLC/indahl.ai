Readings (zhukov.py, fixed now; four outcomes each: ABOVE / BELOW / CONSISTENT / CANNOT_TELL; 99 % intervals; the verdict never uses a value of ours for its scale):
 (a) DELIVERED vs STATED on qubit 16 in his gateless SPAM controls: A q16 packed / solo, prep 0 (P(read 1)) and prep 1 (P(read 0)), against prob_meas1_prep0 / prob_meas0_prep1, range over the five backend records saved (before each job, after the last). ABOVE / BELOW: our interval is above / below the stated range; CONSISTENT: it overlaps (a bound, printed); CANNOT_TELL: half-width > SCALE_A = 0.041 = 1 - 0.959, the worst control minimum he publishes. Qubits 3 and 2 of the same register: same rule, reported.
     PREP vs READOUT stays CANNOT_TELL unless an arm separates them. Arms (ours): S0 / S1 = Delta = P(r1 != r2) - 2 p (1 - p) of two back-to-back reads of qubit 16 after prep 0 / prep 1; independent per-read assignment noise on a settled state predicts Delta = 0, a state already wrong before the read predicts Delta < 0. BELOW rules OUT independent per-read noise; CONSISTENT rules out a persisting state; neither names a mechanism. SC = the same on qubit 3 (control). D0 = P(read 1 | prep 0, 100 us idle) - P(read 1 | prep 0, no idle), outcome against the band +- SCALE_A (a wrong state on qubit 16 relaxes with T1 of 367 us in the record: 0.73 -> about 0.56; an assignment offset does not).
 (b) DRIFT between jobs on his identical circuits: science lanes 1,2,3,5,6 of the packed circuits (60 circuits, about 300 rows); T = chi2/dof of the 8-outcome histograms (cells with pooled count >= 10, his rule). B_WITHIN = median T over the pairs (A1,A2) and (B1,B2); B_SLOT = median T of (A1+A2) against (B1+B2) (linear drift cancels; what is left is circuit position / order). Null: shots permuted jointly across lanes between the two jobs, 300 draws, 0.5-99.5 percent band. CANNOT_TELL if the null half-width of the median exceeds SCALE_B = 0.41, half of the excess median T (1.72 vs null 0.91) between his raw/003 and raw/004 on identical circuits 17 h apart (known_good_zhukov.text KG6).
 (c) HIS CHAIN-C PHASE ERRORS: signed phase error of C1 = <X1> + i<Y1> against his stored exact-Hamiltonian probabilities, Jt = 4/3, chain C (lane 3), Strang r=8 and exact; ours minus his raw/005 value (46.32 deg, 2.95 deg), both sides' counting errors (his S49 propagation) plus the job-to-job term (t, 3 dof, 95 %); CANNOT_TELL if the half-width exceeds SCALE_C = 10 deg, one fifth of his published r=8 / r=16 contrast (46 vs 96). This is a repeat on a new date, not a drift reading (his circuits ran 17 Sep; ABBA cancels linear drift only). C_MIN: mean over the five chains of the minimum correct joint-outcome probability over the 24 raw/003 controls (SPAM standard 16 + axis 8) against his value on the same circuits in raw/003, parametric bootstrap on both sides; CANNOT_TELL above SCALE_A. His published 0.959-0.971 is for his raw/005 controls and is printed beside, not used as the comparator.
