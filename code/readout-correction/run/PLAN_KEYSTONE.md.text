PLAN -- KEYSTONE: what a mid-circuit readout takes from the untouched qubits next to it, and how much a per-qubit virtual-Z gives back
Written, hashed and timestamped (OpenTimestamps) BEFORE any job. Heuremen LLC, 31 October 2026. Chips ibm_kingston and ibm_marrakesh (<our IBM
instance>). Code: keystone.py (circuits, corrections, readers), analyze_keystone.py (every reading below), synth_keystone.py (planted worlds),
fire_keystone.py (build, checks, submit), committed_kicks.py + unwrap_kicks.py (the correction table from the committed 21 Oct run), sim_check.py
(exact-statevector sign and placement check), pooters.py (byte copy of the 24 Oct analysis). Hashes in CODE_KEYSTONE.sha256.text.

WHOSE QUESTIONS (each setup is a question already in print, the paper cites each one; the literature pass is a separate file):
- Spectators during repeated mid-circuit readouts: an equal delay has been proposed to protect them (Santos & Uzdin, arXiv 2506.11270); a learned
  measurement instrument has assumed them negligible or absorbed (Chu et al., arXiv 2606.00433); a spectator probe was built for this (Kumar,
  arXiv 2610.28705); measurement modelled as its waiting time plus assignment error (Caselli et al., arXiv 2610.09062).
- A clock that is looked at: the Page-Wootters history state (Page & Wootters 1983; Moreva et al. 2014, photonic; Diaz et al. 2025, numerical
  only; Coppo et al. PRA 109, 052212, 2024) has no term for the clock being read. Our 24 Oct run (earlier_runs/2026-10-24-page-wootters) put four readouts of a neighbouring pack AFTER the build and the history
  fidelity fell from 0.665 to 0.158.
- Our own earlier preprint (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23169755): the kick is about 2 deg per readout on untouched qubits, and Bell-pair X-part
  concurrence falls slightly, a cost that a fixed local rotation can also produce.

PRIOR ART (literature pass 31 Oct, papers read in full; arxiv_keystone/lit_sources): Krinner et al., Nature 605, 669 (2022) measured the coherent
phase that a readout leaves on untargeted qubits (mean 0.6 deg, max 13.4 deg) and proposed correcting it with virtual-Z rotations of equal
magnitude and opposite sign, not done there. Seif et al. (ISCA 2024, arXiv 2403.06852) appended Z corrections for coherent idle ZZ and Z errors
during measurement and feedforward windows, calibrated by sweeping the window. Kan et al. (arXiv 2610.23840, ibm_phoenix) compared a readout with
a delay of equal length on neighbours in |+> read in X and Y, and used an echo. Govia et al. (NJP 2023) and Shirizly et al. (PRA 2025) found a
measurement-induced Z phase on spectators and reduced it with dynamical decoupling. This run does not claim the phase or the correction as new:
it applies the proposed per-qubit frame correction on IBM Heron from a table measured ten days earlier and one measured minutes earlier, beside the
echo, inside protocols already in print, and splits what comes back from what is lost.

DESIGN (fixed now; one chip at a time is not required, the two chips run independently)
- Roles from the committed 21 Oct control run of each chip (job_ibm_<chip>.json): the 39-qubit pack E is the look; the 117 untouched qubits S;
  48 coupled Bell pairs + 21 singles among S. Look step = measure E (default measure). Idle step = every qubit of E idles exactly L
  (kingston 545 dt, marrakesh 671 dt). Barriers on all qubits around every step. Untouched qubits get no gates during a step.
- Correction tables (degrees per step, applied as rz(-kick) on EVERY untouched target after EVERY look step; no qubit is selected or dropped by
  its kick): MC = the committed 21 Oct table (committed_kicks_<chip>.json), unwrapped per qubit across N = 2, 4, 8 of the same run (rule in
  unwrap_kicks.py: N=4 total on the branch nearest 2 x N=2, N=8 on the branch nearest 2 x N=4); the dose used picks the field (N=4 -> the
  N=4 kick, N=8 -> the N=8 kick). MF = a FRESH table measured minutes before in calibration job C on the same chip (D and M at N = 4 and 8,
  X and Y, 4,000 shots; branch rule: N=4 total nearest 4 x the committed N=4 kick, N=8 nearest 2 x the fresh N=4 total).
- Arms (all six in every setup, interleaved, same job): D idle | DE idle + echo | M look | MC look + committed correction | MF look + fresh
  correction | ME look + echo. Echo = X on the untouched targets after step N/2 and after step N (net identity). The none of M, MC, MF is D;
  the none of ME is DE (the echo also refocuses each qubit's own idle precession, so ME against D would not be like for like).
- Setups: S spectators: sx on all 117, N = 8, settings X, Y (phase = atan2(<Y>, <X>), as the control run) and Z0 (sxdg then Z: a qubit that
  kept its state reads 0), 2,000 shots. P pairs: |Phi+> on the 48 pairs + sx singles, N = 4, settings ZZ XX YY XY YX, 2,500 shots.
  C clock: H c0, H c1, CRY(pi/4) c0 -> s, [look block, N = 4], CRY(pi/2) c1 -> s, 27 tomography settings, 1,000 shots.
  Clock qubits: kingston (c0, s, c1) = (3, 16, 23) as 24 Oct; marrakesh by the rule in keystone.choose_clock (untouched path c0 - s - c1, every
  kick resolved and N=2/4/8 within 2 deg, |kick| <= 20, readout_error < 0.05 and CZ error < 0.01 in today's record, c0 the smaller |kick|,
  largest |kick(c1)| - |kick(c0)|; HOLD if the best contrast is under 5 deg): dry run gave (104, 105, 106), committed kicks -6.4, -4.6,
  -15.2 deg per readout. Both clocks are chosen FOR a large kick contrast (unlike S and P, which take every untouched qubit); the clock
  readings describe those qubits, not a chip average.
- Jobs per chip: C (8 circuits) -> fresh table -> A (210 circuits in order) -> B (the same 210 reversed: ABBA). Copies A and B. DD and twirling
  off. Transpile once (level 1, trivial layout, seed 31). Dry-run checks (both chips PASS): layout unmoved; CZ count 0 / 48 / 4; scheduled
  duration on the untouched targets identical for D, M, MC, MF and for DE, ME (echo adds 16 dt kingston, 18 dt marrakesh); rz count higher in
  MC than M. Every classical register is saved per shot, including the pack's own readouts in the look arms.
- Budget: estimate 184 s per chip at 0.30 ms per shot (cap 240 each); at the control run's 0.33 ms, about 202 s per chip, 404 s for both,
  leaving about 279 s of the 683 s remaining at 14:24 EDT. Kumar's marrakesh fill (its own plan, built
  24 Oct, cancelled only for a maintenance window) fires after this run if the remaining seconds cover its estimate + 30.

KNOWN-GOOD: the S-setup kick of arm M against D on each chip must reproduce our committed 21 Oct control-run median kick on the same 117
untouched qubits with the same estimator (wrapped phase difference at N = 8, divided by 8): kingston -2.864, marrakesh -2.393 deg per readout.
(read from arxiv_one_readout/control_run/results_prereg.txt, lines 57 and 106: -2.8638 [-3.5519, -2.0896] and -2.3931 [-2.9963, -1.7160]).
Rule: pooled median with its 99 % band upper end below 0 AND the median within 0.5x to 2x of the committed value. A chip that fails is
"not decided" and none of its readings is read as a finding.

READINGS (only these; four outcomes ABOVE / BELOW / CONSISTENT / CANNOT_TELL; per copy A, B and pooled; FINAL = pooled if A and B give the
same outcome, else CANNOT_TELL; CONSISTENT is printed as a bound). S and P: median over qubits (pairs), 2,000-draw bootstrap over qubits
(pairs), 99 % band. C: shot bootstrap, 400 draws, 99 % band.
 S1 signed residual per readout of MC, MF, ME against its none (median over the 117): vs 0. The uncorrected M median is printed beside it
    (both alternatives: all given back = 0, nothing given back = the M value).
 S2 THE SPLIT: per-qubit residual of MC, MF, ME regressed on the M kick read in the OTHER copy (Theil-Sen slope; copy A = y from A, x from B;
    pooled = mean of both): vs 0 (everything given back) and vs 1 (nothing given back). The M-on-M slope is printed as the ruler's ceiling
    (regression dilution makes it about 0.99), not scored.
 S3 back to zero: P(1) in setting Z0, arm minus its none (median over the 117), for M, MC, MF, ME: vs 0.
 S4 coherence |<X> + i<Y>|, arm minus none, for M, MC, MF, ME: vs 0 (the part that does not come back). S4b coherence MC minus M: vs 0 (a
    frame change cannot change coherence: a non-zero S4b means the corrected circuits differ in more than the frame).
 S5 the time ladder: SPLIT slope of MC minus SPLIT slope of MF (same resampled qubits): vs 0 (a ten-day-old table against a minutes-old one).
 S6 given back per qubit: |M kick| minus |MC residual| (median over the 117): vs 0.
 P1 Bell fidelity <Phi+|rho|Phi+> = (p00 + p11)/2 + (<XX> - <YY>)/4, arm minus none, for M, MC, MF, ME: vs 0 (phase-sensitive).
 P2 X-part concurrence (the control run's estimator), arm minus none, pairs whose none concurrence is 0 excluded and counted: vs 0
    (phase-blind: a Z rotation of either qubit leaves it unchanged, so this is the part a correction cannot give back).
 P3 Bell fidelity MC minus M: vs 0 (given back). P3b X-part concurrence MC minus M: vs 0 (frame only).
 C1 history fidelity F = (1/8) sum_P <P><P>_ideal (pooters.py), arm minus none, for M, MC, MF, ME; and M with the committed TOTAL phase put back
    at the END (tomography frame) minus D.
 C2 F(MC) minus F(M): vs 0 (given back); the recovered share (F(MC) - F(M)) / (F(D) - F(M)) printed.
 C3 F(MC) minus F(M put back at the end): vs 0 (in-circuit correction against the same numbers applied after the fact).

ADDED AFTER THE DESIGN REFEREE (31 Oct, before any job; referee report in REFEREE_DESIGN.md.text):
 S2c SPLIT slope of MC, MF, ME vs 0.5 (BELOW = more than half of the kick given back).
 S2d SPLIT slope of M minus the slope of MC, MF, ME (same resampled qubits): ABOVE = the corrected arm clears the ruler's own ceiling.
 S7 share given back = 1 - median|residual of the arm| / median|kick of M| (same resampled qubits), vs 0.5.
 P4a gate: median over pairs of |pair phase per step of M| minus the copy floor; P4 pair-phase share given back (MC, MF, ME) vs 0.5;
 P5 signed pair-phase residual (MC, MF, ME) vs 0. The pair phase is atan2(<XY> + <YX>, <XX> - <YY>), read against its own none, so it
    does not depend on the pairs' own idle phase (Bell fidelity P1/P3 does: secondary).
 C4a gate: |E_M - E_D|^2 over the 63 expectations minus the copy floor; C4 distance share given back = 1 - |E_X - E_none|^2 / |E_M - E_D|^2
    for MC, MF, ME, each squared distance less the shot-noise floor (once for the point, twice inside a bootstrap draw), vs 0.5. This is the
    clock's headline reading: it does not depend on the idle state's own offset from the ideal. C1-C3 and C2b (history fidelity) are
    SECONDARY: F measures distance to the ideal state, and the measured idle state sits off it (24 Oct: rotated by -25.7, +25.8, +36.0 deg it
    would read 0.834), so the sign of an F difference can depend on that offset.
 GATES: C2, C2b, C3 read only when C1(M) is BELOW; C4 only when C4a is ABOVE; P4 only when P4a is ABOVE; P3 only when P1(M) is BELOW;
    otherwise CANNOT_TELL with the gate named. The spectator readings are gated by the chip's known-good.

WHAT WOULD COUNT AGAINST "MORE THAN HALF OF WHAT A READOUT TAKES FROM THE UNTOUCHED QUBITS IS A PHASE THAT A VIRTUAL-Z GIVES BACK" (per chip,
stated now; "mostly" means more than half, read on the two baseline-free rulers):
 - spectators: S7 of MC not ABOVE 0.5, or S2c of MC not BELOW 0.5, or S2d of MC not ABOVE 0.
 - clock: C4 of MC not ABOVE 0.5 when C4a is ABOVE. pairs: P4 of MC not ABOVE 0.5 when P4a is ABOVE.
 - The claim holds only for a fresh table if MC fails these and MF passes (said that way, never folded).
 - A corrected arm whose signed residual (S1, P5) lands on the far side of zero from M is OVER-CORRECTION and is reported as such.
 - S1 at its +-0.1 deg resolution will flag a ten-day drift in MC; that is the S5 reading, not a failure of the claim.
 - The N = 4 tables (pairs, clock) and the N = 8 table (spectators) disagreeing on the same qubits is reported (dose dependence).
 - What is LOST is reported with the same weight: S4, P2 and the residual distance |E_MC - E_D|^2 left above the copy floor.
 - A chip whose known-good fails, or a reading whose copies disagree, is CANNOT_TELL, not a result.
