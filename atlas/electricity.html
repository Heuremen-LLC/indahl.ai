What electricity costs by state, and where it comes from

Average prices paid for electricity in 2024, by state, with the data centers from the ATLAS map drawn over them. In the United States as a whole, the average was 8.13 cents per kilowatthour for industrial customers, 12.75 for commercial customers and 16.48 for residential customers. These are averages of what customers paid, which is revenue divided by sales. They are not tariff rates, and a data center may buy under a negotiated contract that differs from its state average.
