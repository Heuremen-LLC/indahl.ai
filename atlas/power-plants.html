Operating power plants in the United States

Every operating plant in the U.S. Energy Information Administration’s monthly generator inventory for September 2026, 14,280 plants with 1,390.6 gigawatts of nameplate capacity. The map draws the 4,634 plants of 25 megawatts or more in the contiguous states, colored by the fuel with the most capacity at the plant and sized by capacity. Rings are the AI data centers from the ATLAS map. Nameplate capacity is what a plant is rated for, not what it produces. For generation and prices by state see Electricity.

Operating plants can be switched on the one ATLAS map with the data centers, planned plants, prices and carbon intensity.

Plants below the size cut-off and plants in Alaska and Hawaii are in the table and the data file and are not drawn.
