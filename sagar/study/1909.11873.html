What the Paper Shows Elsewhere

“For SEOBNRv4PHM,the analysistimecanbereducedto11.5dayson640cores,vs2.9years on 16 cores.” F5, paper text

“GW150914 3.9 d 23.3 hr 2.8 hr 83.7 d 21.2d 2.5 d” F5, paper text

F5. Table 1, SEOBNRv4PHM on GW190412: 2.9 yr on 16 cores vs 11.53 d on 640 is a 92x speed-up on 40x the cores (16 to 64: 3.8x; 64 to 640: 23.9x on 10x). Even ideal scaling from the 640-core time puts 16 cores at <= 461 d, so the 2.9-yr estimate is inconsistent with the measured scaling (same waveform on GW150914: 33.5x).
