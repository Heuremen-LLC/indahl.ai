8 of 10 printed Mantel statistics reproduce from their files. Our run on ibm_kingston, 24 Oct 2026. All 20 circuits sat on one layout, the one the transpiler chose. On that layout identical pairs read 0.09–0.18 where the exact value is 1 (deviation -0.8682 [-0.9422, -0.7943], 99% t-band over 4 pairs) and their pairs deviate by -0.6318 [-0.8163, -0.4473] (99% t-band over 12 pairs). Rank order is kept (Spearman +0.755). On that layout one physical pair, (q1, q0), reads -0.844 against exact and the other 8 pairs -0.0005 to -0.019 (defined after the data). On a second layout that moves that pair (defined after the data) identical pairs read 0.855 (deviation -0.1455 [-0.2311, -0.0599], 99% t-band), their pairs deviate by -0.1003 [-0.1388, -0.0619] and Spearman is +0.923. So the collapse is not chip-wide
