Qubit Data

Published quantum-device datasets beyond IBM

Published datasets from qubit and quantum-device experiments beyond IBM: superconducting qubits from other labs and vendors, trapped ions, neutral atoms, NV centers and spin qubits, quantum dots, photonics, sensing, and the raw output of quantum key distribution and random number generators. A dataset is listed when it releases measurement files. Records with code or figures only, simulations only, or no open files are left out. Studies funded by defence, military or intelligence agencies of any country are left out. Civilian science funders are kept. The classification is for indexing, not an assessment. IBM device studies are on The List. Removal on request, any time.
