Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on the date Counterparty accepts it between Heuremen LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company ("Heuremen"), and the organisation named by the person accepting it ("Counterparty"). Each is a "Party"; a Party disclosing information is the "Discloser" and a Party receiving it is the "Recipient".

1. Purpose

The Parties wish to discuss a possible engagement in which Heuremen would measure, review or advise on systems, data or results of interest to Counterparty (the "Purpose"). This Agreement governs information exchanged for the Purpose. It does not oblige either Party to enter any engagement.

2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by the Discloser to the Recipient for the Purpose, in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. It includes business plans, technical specifications, unpublished data, credentials and access details, and the existence and terms of the discussions between the Parties.

3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show by written record:
(a) is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement by the Recipient;
(b) was known to or possessed by the Recipient before receipt from the Discloser;
(c) is received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality to the Discloser; or
(d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

4. Independent work (no restriction on Heuremen's own research)

Counterparty acknowledges that Heuremen conducts and publishes independent research, including measurements of commercially available systems made on Heuremen's own accounts, from public records and from information described in §3. Nothing in this Agreement restricts Heuremen from conducting, publishing or commercialising such work, or any results, methods, code or findings that do not use Counterparty's Confidential Information, whether made before, during or after the term of this Agreement, and whether or not they concern systems that Counterparty makes, sells, uses or competes with. The burden of showing that a disclosure by Heuremen used Counterparty's Confidential Information rests on Counterparty.

5. Obligations

The Recipient shall: use Confidential Information only for the Purpose; protect it with at least the care it uses for its own information of similar sensitivity, and no less than reasonable care; and disclose it only to its employees, members, contractors and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than these.

5A. Contact and premises

(a) "Heuremen Personnel" means Heuremen's members, managers, employees, contractors and agents. "Related Person" means any person with a personal, professional, social or family relationship with any Heuremen Personnel, including a spouse, partner, parent, child, sibling or other direct relative. "Heuremen Premises" means any property, office, facility or holding owned, leased, used or operated by Heuremen or its divisions, including their systems and accounts.
(b) Counterparty shall not, and shall cause its employees, contractors, agents and advisers not to, initiate or conduct any contact or engagement with any Heuremen Personnel or Related Person concerning the Purpose, Heuremen's work or the Confidential Information, or enter any Heuremen Premises, unless and until this Agreement has been accepted by Counterparty and confirmed by Heuremen under §12. After that, all such contact goes through the contact Heuremen names in writing.
(c) Information obtained through contact or entry in breach of (b) is Heuremen's Confidential Information under this Agreement, and is not covered by the exclusions in §3(b) or §3(c).

6. Compelled disclosure

If the Recipient is required by law, regulation or court order to disclose Confidential Information, it may do so after giving the Discloser prompt notice (where lawful) so the Discloser may seek protection, and shall disclose only what is legally required.

7. Return or destruction

On the Discloser's written request, or at the end of the discussions, the Recipient shall return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information within thirty (30) days, except copies retained in automatic backups or as required by law, which remain subject to this Agreement.

8. No licence, no warranty

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. No licence under any patent, copyright or other right is granted except the limited right to use it for the Purpose. Confidential Information is provided "as is".

9. Term

This Agreement covers disclosures made within two (2) years of its date. Obligations for each item of Confidential Information last three (3) years from its disclosure, and for trade secrets and credentials for as long as they remain such.

10. Remedies

Unauthorised use or disclosure may cause irreparable harm, and the Discloser may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy, without posting bond where permitted.

11. General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules; the Parties submit to the state and federal courts located in New Jersey. It is the entire agreement on its subject, may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties, and may be signed electronically and in counterparts. Neither Party may assign it without the other's consent, except to a successor of its business. Nothing in it creates an agency, partnership or obligation to purchase.

11A. Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted and the remaining provisions remain in full force.

12. Electronic acceptance

Counterparty may accept this Agreement electronically through Heuremen's inquiry process. Acceptance is made by a person who reads this Agreement, confirms their authority to bind Counterparty, and gives their full name, title and organisation from an identity verified by that process. Heuremen records the version of this Agreement accepted, the time, and the verified identity. The Agreement takes effect when Heuremen confirms acceptance in writing. Each Party agrees that this electronic acceptance and record have the same effect as a handwritten signature.

Privacy Notice

Effective 23 October 2026. Updated 29 October 2026 to describe the installable app.

This notice explains how Heuremen LLC (“Heuremen”, “we”, “us”), including all of its divisions, collects, uses, shares, protects and retains personal information through indahl.ai, heuremen.org, our inquiry form, our correspondence and our engagements. We hold the information of everyone who contacts us, and of any client, in confidence.

Information we collect

When you visit our websites

Our websites set no cookies and run no analytics, advertising, tracking or session-recording scripts. indahl.ai is delivered through Cloudflare and heuremen.org is hosted on GitHub Pages; each processes standard request data (IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time) to deliver and protect the site. Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives the requesting IP address and browser information.

If you install indahl.ai as an app on your phone or computer, your browser keeps a copy of the public pages you open on your own device so that they open without a connection. We receive nothing from that copy and it holds no personal information from you. Removing the app or clearing the site’s data deletes it. Live data feeds, the inquiry form and sign-in are never kept in it.

When you submit an inquiry

The inquiry form collects what you enter: your name, organisation email address, organisation, role, a description of what you need (including the systems or results involved and your organisation’s relationships with relevant vendors), an approximate budget range and timeline, who referred you, and your confirmations regarding non-disclosure, authority and this notice. For an engagement inquiry we also record the identity you verified (your work email address, the name on that account, the sign-in method and the identifier our sign-in provider assigns), and your acceptance of our Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement and this notice (which versions, the time, that you read both to the end, and your name, title and organisation). Nothing is recorded until you submit the inquiry. We also record the time of the inquiry and the country the request came from. We do not store your IP address; a truncated one-way hash of it, which may still be personal data, is held for up to one hour only to limit repeat submissions. Each accepted inquiry is also emailed to us.

When you add a PDF link under a Recount Matrix

The one-field box under each Recount Matrix stores the link you paste, the table it was added under and the time. It stores no name, email address or IP address. A truncated one-way hash of your IP address is held for up to one hour only to limit repeat submissions. We read each link before anything changes in a table.

When you add a response

The response form stores the reference of the page you answered, the link to your PDF, the host name of that link and the time. It stores no name, email address or IP address. A truncated one-way hash of your IP address is held for up to one hour only to limit repeat submissions. We read each file before anything is posted. A posted response shows its date and the host it came from.

When we correspond or work together

If we reply to an inquiry or enter an engagement, we keep our correspondence, the agreements we sign (including non-disclosure agreements and statements of work), invoicing records, and the data you provide for the engagement.

Information we do not seek

We do not ask for, and ask you not to send through the form, payment card details, government identification numbers, health information or other special-category data. Engagement data containing such information is handled only under a written agreement that provides for it.

How we use it

  • To review inquiries for fit, independence (including conflicts with vendors we measure) and authority, and to reply.
  • To negotiate, perform and invoice engagements.
  • To keep our websites secure, and to detect and prevent abuse of the inquiry form.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, and to establish or defend legal claims.

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, use it for automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects, or use it to train models.

Legal bases

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interests in reviewing inquiries and running our business securely; on steps taken at your request before entering a contract, and the performance of that contract; on compliance with legal obligations; and, where we ask for it, on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that process it on our behalf and under their contractual obligations: Cloudflare (delivery of indahl.ai, the inquiry form’s storage and email delivery), WorkOS (verifying the work identity of people who send an engagement inquiry), GitHub (hosting of heuremen.org), Google Workspace (our email), and Google Fonts (typefaces). We disclose information when required by law or valid legal process, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this notice continues to apply to the information transferred. We never name a client, partner or engagement without written consent.

International transfers

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in other countries. Where the law requires it, transfers are made under appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses relied on by those providers.

How long we keep it

  • Inquiries that do not lead to an engagement, and the copy emailed to us: up to 24 months from receipt, then deleted.
  • Engagement records, agreements and invoices: for the life of the engagement and as long afterwards as contract, tax and legal obligations require.
  • Engagement data you provide: returned or deleted at the end of the engagement on request, except where we must keep it by law.
  • The one-way hash used to limit repeat submissions: one hour.
  • Inquiry cookies: a signed cookie recording that you accepted the agreements, up to two hours; a cookie that ties a sign-in to your browser, up to ten minutes; a signed cookie holding your verified identity while you complete an engagement inquiry, up to 45 minutes. We set no other cookies and no tracking cookies.

How we protect it

Inquiries are stored in a private storage area accessible only to us. We use the least access an engagement needs, prefer credentials that clients own and can revoke, never write credentials into code or reports, and keep client data separate from our published research. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we will notify affected people and authorities of a breach where the law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to receive a copy, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent. California residents have the rights to know, delete and correct, and to non-discrimination for exercising them; we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined. We honour Global Privacy Control signals, although we have no sale or sharing to opt out of. To make a request, use the inquiry form and choose “Privacy request” as the reason. We reply to every privacy request, verify your request against the information we hold and respond within the time the law allows. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our websites and services are intended for organisations and are not directed to anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this notice

We will post any change here with a new effective date. Material changes will be noted at the top of this page.

Contact

Heuremen LLC. For any question about this notice, use the inquiry form and choose “Privacy request” as the reason.
