Cost to scale

One year of electricity for the AI data centers in Epoch AI’s dataset, at each site’s full stated power and each state’s average 2024 industrial price, comes to 7.4 billion dollars. That is 7,419.0 million dollars for 11,560.33 megawatts and 101.3 terawatthours, across 57 sites that state a power and sit in a state we could place.

It is arithmetic on two published figures and not a forecast. Epoch AI states each site’s power, in a dataset we pulled on 28 Oct 2026. The U.S. Energy Information Administration’s EIA-861 data for 2024 gives each state’s average industrial price. It is full stated power for a full year at the state average, and not any site’s contract price or actual bill.

How the cost is worked

For each site, stated megawatts times 8,760 hours gives megawatthours. Megawatthours times the state’s industrial price in cents per kilowatthour, with 1,000 kilowatthours to the megawatthour and 100 cents to the dollar, gives dollars. Example, Anthropic-Amazon New Carlisle: 1,037 MW × 8,760 hours = 9,084,120 MWh, and 9,084,120 MWh × 8.15 cents per kilowatthour = 740.4 million dollars.

Epoch AI’s dataset lists 77 data centers in the United States. 60 state a power and 17 do not. 57 of the 60 that state a power are in a state we could place, and the total covers those 57. The other 3, Google Mesa, Google Kansas City East and Google Storey County, state 449 megawatts between them and give no address, so they are not in the total. 8 of the 77 sites could not be placed in a state at all. Where the geocoders found no point, the state is the one Epoch’s own address text names. That applies to Amazon Madison Mega Site (Mississippi) and OpenAI Stargate Shackelford (Texas, no stated power).

The state comes from the point where the site was placed on the map, with one correction. The geocoders placed Google Papillion in Kansas from a city and ZIP match. Its Epoch address names Papillion, Nebraska, and the replacement point is on Gold Coast Road in ZIP 68138 (OpenStreetMap Nominatim, looked up 30 Oct 2026). The map and the table use Nebraska.

Epoch describes its power figures as estimates of IT power, the power of the computing equipment, so the power a whole site draws with cooling and other loads is not in them. Two pairs of records with a stated power land on the same map point: QTS Richmond 2 with QTS Richmond 3, and Core42 Lake Mariner with Anthropic Lake Mariner. Each record is counted once, as Epoch lists it.

At each state’s commercial price instead of its industrial price, the same arithmetic gives 10,807.1 million dollars.
