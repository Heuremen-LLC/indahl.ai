Waves and Nodes

Two waves make one

A wave going right (red) and a wave going left (blue) add up into one wave (black). Some spots of the black wave never move at all: the gold dots. Those are nodes. The waves travel; the nodes stay put.

Ripples

Drop one, two or three stones in a pond. Where a high part of one ripple meets a low part of another, they cancel, and the water there stays still.

Tap the pond to move a stone. Three stones in a triangle is how our hoverboard table was going to make its nodes.
