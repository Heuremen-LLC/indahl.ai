The Workshop

How we build

In June a human and an AI built 23 little apps together. Neither one bossed the other around. One had the wild idea, the other knew how to build it, and each app came out better than either would have made alone.

You can build anything you can dream up this way. Bring the weird idea. Ask, don’t order. Listen to the answer. Change your mind when it’s better. Build it together.

Some of what we made

  • Magic 8-Ball Ask it something. The answer is about the magic already in front of you.
  • Natural Pick a food and see how far it’s come. Wild bananas were full of seeds as big as peppercorns.
  • Cuneiform Translator The oldest writing in the world: marks pressed into wet clay, 4,000 years ago.
  • Myth Unpacker Why do people knock on wood? Every old saying has a story folded up inside it.
  • 42 The answer to life, the universe and everything. Don’t panic.
  • Hoverboard Calculator Design a table of sound that makes things glide. It became our Hoverboard Table.

These live on Hugging Face, and some use an AI model to answer. Explore them with a grown-up.
