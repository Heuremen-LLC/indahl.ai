The data behind the OY page at https://indahl.ai/oy and its school pages at https://indahl.ai/oy/school/, as files you can open in a spreadsheet or Python. OY lists what colleges and universities have published about AI in teaching and learning and about measuring their own AI policies, with a source link for every row. The OY pages publish these tables as web pages and not as data files. The files here are the tables of the pages as published on 2026-11-10, read out of the pages without changes to their text, as JSON and as CSV with one row per record. The list is as of 25 October 2026. The school pages were read on 28 October 2026. Pages that a school has moved or rewritten since can differ from what we quote.

What is in each file

Chatbot citations (json/oy_chatbot_citations.json, csv/oy_chatbot_citations.csv): 8 rows. Each has the institution, the document, the quoted text and source_url, the address of the document.

Findings (json/oy_findings.json): the table of findings of the page, with 16 findings. csv/oy_findings.csv has one row per finding with the finding, the rows text as printed, such as 8 of 148, the count, count_high when the page prints a range because a row cannot be settled, the denominator of and a note when the finding has no institutions to list. csv/oy_finding_institutions.csv has one row per institution or study named under a finding: name, url, kind when the page marks the link as a journal article, a preprint or another kind of document, and what_we_found with what_we_found_source_url where the page gives them. A blank url is a row without a link, such as our own row.

The list (json/oy_list.json, csv/oy_list.csv): 148 rows, one of them our own. The columns are institution and institution_url (the document the page links from the name), institution_url_kind, first_document_cited with first_document_url (the first link in our notes on the institution's AI documents, not a judgement that the document is a policy), measures_own_policy, released_own_data, pilot_results and pilot_source_url. A blank means the page shows no link or a dash.

Private baccalaureate colleges (json/oy_schools.json): 389 schools, one page each. csv/oy_schools.csv has one row per school with the federal ID in unitid, the Carnegie class, the school page address, the address of the school's site, the text about what the script read, sentences_total and course_titles_total as the table prints them, read_in_full_label for the schools whose pages we also read in full, and the notes printed on the school page. A blank in a total means the page shows a dash. The columns sentences_json and course_titles_json hold the lists of the school page as text. The same lists come as csv/oy_school_sentences.csv (61 rows) and csv/oy_school_course_titles.csv (291 rows), one row per item with its url. A sentence ends in an ellipsis where the script cut it, as the page shows. The page shows at most the number it says in its note, and sentences_total gives the number found.

Page text (json/oy_text.json, csv/oy_text_meta.csv): the headline, the explanation of how the rows were read and the other text of the page that describes the tables, as key and value rows.

Sources and dates

SOURCES.md lists the sources and their terms. NOTICE.txt carries the attribution lines. Every sentence, course title and quoted text has the address of its page in the row. The list rows carry the address of the document each cell comes from. The other cells are our own classification and carry no address of their own.

How to open the files

Spreadsheet: open any file in csv/. The files are UTF-8 with a byte order mark so Excel reads names correctly. Empty text and missing values are both shown as blank cells.

Python, standard library only:

`` import json, csv rows = json.load(open("json/oy_list.json", encoding="utf-8"))["rows"] schools = list(csv.DictReader(open("csv/oy_schools.csv", encoding="utf-8-sig"))) ``

Check the pack with python verify.py. It recounts the rows of every file, compares every CSV cell with the JSON, and recomputes the counts the files state about each other, such as the number of institutions under each finding and the sentences and course titles of every school.

Notes on the data

The list is a classification for indexing. It is not an assessment of any school or its people. None found means we looked in the places the page names on that date and found nothing. That does not show that none exists. We chose the institutions ourselves, in batches, so the rows are not a random sample.

The sentences and course titles on the school pages were found by script and were not read in full, except for the schools marked in read_in_full_label. Many sentences are not about teaching and learning. The page of each school says how many rows our filters removed.

Not in this pack are the pages of OY that are not data tables, which are the pages for children, and the CAMPUS page, which watches the change of the cited documents over time.

Terms

Each part of this pack keeps the terms of its source, and LICENSE.txt gives them by file. The sentences, titles and quotations belong to the schools and authors of the pages named in each row. The federal ID and class come from the federal IPEDS directory. To correct or remove a row, use https://indahl.ai/correction. It is open to anyone, and we acknowledge every request within three business days.

Heuremen LLC permits anyone to use, copy and change its own parts with attribution to Heuremen LLC and https://indahl.ai/oy. For its own text (the README, SOURCES.md, NOTICE.txt, LICENSE.txt, TESTED.md, START_HERE.html and the labels, counts and notes it wrote in the tables, which LICENSE.txt lists by column) that permission does not include use as AI training data. Its reservation against text and data mining and AI training covers that text only and does not apply to the quoted sentences, titles and quotations.

How it was tested

On 2026-11-10 the zip was unzipped into an empty folder and every CSV was opened with pandas 2.3.3 on Python 3.9 at default settings. The list, findings and text were compared with the OY page as built for release that day, and the school pages and their files are unchanged from the test of 2026-11-01, when all 389 live school pages were fetched and read with lxml 6.1 and Beautiful Soup 4. Every count and number below matched.

  • Headline: 10 of 148 listed colleges and universities with a published, measured grade or learning outcome of their own AI policy, guidance, tool or pilot under the rule written before any school was read, and 3 to 4 of 148 under the rule set after the first 41 schools were read, 148 rows in oy_list.csv and 8 chatbot citations in oy_chatbot_citations.csv
  • Table of findings: 16 findings with the Rows text as printed, and the 311 institutions or studies named under them with their links and their what we found text
  • Chatbot cited as a source: 8 of 148, with institution, document, text and source address of each row
  • Measured its own policy, guidance or a centrally provided tool: 6 of 148
  • Outcome reported without counts: 3 of 148
  • Plans to measure its own policy: 47 of 148
  • Surveys of use or attitudes, no measure of its own policy: 71 of 148
  • None found: 17 of 148, one of them our own row
  • Not reached, because the pages refused our reader or need a login: 4 of 148
  • Released nothing: 76 of 114, and summary tables or an instrument only: 38 of 114, with 114 read and 34 not read
  • Pilots run or funded by the institution, results read: 10 of 148, and the list shows 12 pilot cells, of which 2 say the results were not read
  • The list: institution, institution link, first document cited and its link, measures, released data, pilot text and pilot link of all 148 rows equal the page table
  • Private baccalaureate colleges: 389 rows with school, state, site text, sentences, course titles and read in full equal to the page table, and 41 schools marked read in full
  • School pages: all 389 live pages have the same name and federal ID as the CSV, and every sentence and course title with its address equals oy_school_sentences.csv (61 rows) and oy_school_course_titles.csv (291 rows)
  • Sentences found are 62 and sentences shown are 61, because one school page says it shows 1 of 2. Course titles found and shown are both 291

Where the page shows a dash, the CSV cell is blank. At default settings pandas reads a blank cell as missing. No cell in any file is text that pandas reads as missing, such as NA or None.

The file verify.py compares every CSV cell with the JSON, and recomputes the counts the files state about each other, such as the institutions under each finding and the sentences and course titles of every school.

Not tested: Excel or LibreOffice. The outside links in the rows were compared with the page and were not opened again, so a document that has moved since is not detected here.
