Quantum Coins

Flip a real one

On 7 April we put 8 qubits on IBM’s quantum computers into superposition and measured them, thousands of times, on three different chips. Each answer was a 0 or a 1. Here, 1 is heads.

Coins from ibm_kingston. The answers were saved sorted, so we hand them out in a shuffled order. Every one is a real measurement.

A spinning coin

A coin spinning on the table already has a side it will land on. You just can’t see it yet. A qubit is different: before it’s measured, it has no side yet. Both feel the same from outside. Telling them apart is one of the great experiments of science.
