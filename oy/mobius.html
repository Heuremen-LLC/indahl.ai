The Möbius Strip

Make one

  • Cut a long strip of paper. Give it half a twist and tape the ends together.
  • Draw a line down the middle without lifting your pencil. Where did you end up?
  • Cut along your line. Count the pieces. Then make another and cut a third of the way in.
