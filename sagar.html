SAGAR

SAGAR indexes gravitational-wave papers and lists checkable errata. An erratum here is a place where a paper disagrees with itself. The text disagrees with its own table, or a printed number does not follow from the paper’s own printed numbers, or a headline is a single best value, or a claim carries no significance or interval.

Each listed item was read by two independent readers who found the same place. Where the paper released data that could settle it, we recomputed from that data and say so in the last column. Every quote is verbatim from the paper’s PDF, with its location. A listing is a classification for indexing, not an assessment of the study or its authors. An erratum can be a typo, and many are.

Papers are indexed by arXiv id and DOI. Items are listed newest arXiv id first. If a paper’s authors have a correction, or want an item changed or removed, use the correction or removal form, open to anyone.

Codes and Tiers

F2 a single best value is the headline and the spread is not beside it. F3 a claim with no significance or interval. F5 a printed number does not follow from the paper’s own printed numbers. F6 the text disagrees with the paper’s own table, caption or file.

T1 arithmetic or typo, with no claim built on it. T2 the text and the paper’s own table, caption, appendix or file disagree. T3 the item sits in, or sets, a number the abstract or conclusion relies on. A row takes its highest tier. Tiers describe where in the paper the item sits.

Eccentricity Constraints Disfavor Single-Single Capture in Nuclear Star Clusters as the Origin of all LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA Binary Black Holes
arXiv 2603.29019, September 2026
doi:10.3847/1538-4357/ae9479
Study page		arXiv PDF as served 12 Nov 2026. The version number was not recorded.

“the thick green curve shows the globalσmodel posterior with median 3.9 +19.7−3.3 km/s (90% HDI).” Figure 5 caption

“we constrain the velocity dispersion of the clusters hosting the observed systems toσ=3.9 +19.7−3.3 km/s (90% HDI).” Conclusion

“hierarchical inference on the velocity dispersion of the host environment of the BBHs and findσ <19.7 km/s (95% credible upper bound).” Abstract

“We report the marginal posterior overσ global (Fig. 5, left panel) and findσ global <19.7 km/s (95% credible upper bound), with a median of 3.9 km/s.” Body

The caption interval 3.9 +19.7 −3.3 km/s (90% HDI) has edges 0.6 and 23.6 km/s. From the released samples (6,560 posterior samples) the 95th percentile is 19.67 km/s, the equal-tailed 90% interval is 0.45 to 19.67 km/s, and the 90% HDI is 0.00 to 15.08 km/s. None of 2,000 bootstrap resamples (95th percentile 19.67, bootstrap 95% interval 19.10 to 20.42) puts the upper edge of either 90% interval at 23.6. The median (3.85) and the 95% bound (19.67) match the abstract.

Scope: Recomputed from sigma_posterior.h5 only, treating the samples as equally weighted. Other files in the released record were not checked.
