What it shows about the model

Under our planned scoring rule, which scores a judge by the share of its 16 picks per item, the GPT-5.6-Sol judge scores 72.9 against 74.5 printed. The printed 74.5 rebuilds exactly only under a rule found after the data, which scores the judge by its more-picked answer.
