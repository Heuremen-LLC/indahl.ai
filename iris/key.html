IRIS codes: what moves a language-model result besides the model

CodeNameWhat happensMeasuredIndexed studies where it touches the data
L1Option orderThe answer changes when only the order of the options changes.falcon3:3b 42.5%, mistral:7b 27.3%, phi4-mini 27.8%, smollm2:1.7b 50.0% of answers change with the options reversed (our setup). GPT-4.1, Claude Sonnet 4.5 and Gemini 2.5 Flash flip the verdict on 31.0%, 31.3% and 30.9% of prompts when the two answers swap position (from the judge calls released with arXiv 2610.30512).arXiv 2610.30512
L2Prompt wordingThe answer changes when only the wording or format of the prompt changes.With the prompt template changed: falcon3:3b 22.2%, gemma3:4b 11.3%, granite3.3:2b 29.4%, llama3.1:8b 12.0%, mistral:7b 13.1%, olmo2:7b 20.8%, phi4-mini 10.6%, smollm2:1.7b 30.0% of answers change (our setup).arXiv 2610.30512
L3Repeat spreadThe same setup run again, with another seed, or on another GPU or host gives different answers.Our setup: exact repeats agree on all but 0% to 0.5% of answers. Mamba-2.8B with the same seed on a different GPU and host: 0.95% of 8,000 items change (from the files released with arXiv 2610.31135).arXiv 2610.30512, arXiv 2610.31135, arXiv 2610.25626
L4Length limitReplies are cut off by the length setting before an answer is given.deepseek-r1:1.5b 108 of 200 and qwen3:4b 191 of 200 replies hit our length limit, so their cells are not read.—
L5Serving backendResults change when the provider’s serving backend changes behind the same model name.GPT-4.1 as judge: outcome change with the reported serving backend changed against unchanged, +9.8 points [+2.1, +17.6], one run pair (from the files released with arXiv 2610.30512).arXiv 2610.30512
L6Answer reading ruleThe score depends on the rule used to read the answer out of the reply.F1 moves 6.13 points [4.43, 7.83] on the adversarial split, 5.37 on popular and 3.15 on random depending on which yes or no tokens are read (from the files released with arXiv 2610.25964). Under the planned scoring rule the GPT-5.6-Sol judge scores 72.9 against 74.5 printed (arXiv 2610.17203).arXiv 2610.25964, arXiv 2610.17203
L7Model as referenceA model’s output is the reference or the judge the results are scored against.The GPT labeller's gold labels, once reviewed, give 0.960 accuracy and put it ahead of every trained classifier by 0.115 to 0.145 (arXiv 2610.25159). On G1 against G2 the Gemini 3.1 Pro judge agrees with the reader majority 0.657 [0.486, 0.800] of the time, consistent with chance (arXiv 2610.21468). Our own pipeline: a judge model gave different verdicts on 22 of 24 items it saw more than once, and held (no decision) on 57.8% [55.5, 60.1] of its 1,779 verdicts.arXiv 2610.25159, arXiv 2610.21468, arXiv 2610.17203
L8BatchingSeveral items go into one call, and the answers differ from one item per call.Labelling 4 or 8 records per call agrees with single-record labels 0.02 to 0.03 less than two single-record runs agree with each other (from the files released with arXiv 2610.24999).arXiv 2610.24999, arXiv 2610.15732
L9Answer options offeredAdding or removing a “none” or abstain option changes the score.Allowing a “none” answer raises macro-F1 by +0.21 for GPT, +0.27 for Gemini and +0.20 for Claude (from the files released with arXiv 2610.15732). Our setup, with a none option offered: gemma3:4b gives a false “none” on 0.755 [0.696, 0.815] of persona items.arXiv 2610.15732
