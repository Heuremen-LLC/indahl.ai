A Falcon Trained on the Faults and Guards

Falcon3 3B Instruct, tuned with LoRA on new questions in the same form, then answered on two fresh evaluation sets. Three models answer the same items: the untouched model, the model trained on the Faults and Guards, and a control trained on the same items with mixed labels. Settings were fixed and committed before any training. Results follow.

The Two Finalists, Head to Head

After eleven rounds, the two models with the lowest Fault rate under the Guard wording were smollm2:1.7b and qwen3:4b. The selection rule and the reading rule were committed before any round was read. Both finalists then answered the same two fresh question sets, rounds 12 and 13, in the same arms at temperature 0.
