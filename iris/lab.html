A Falcon Trained on the Faults and Guards

Falcon3 3B Instruct, tuned with LoRA on new questions in the same form, then answered on two fresh evaluation sets. Three models answer the same items: the untouched model, the model trained on the Faults and Guards, and a control trained on the same items with mixed labels. Settings were fixed and committed before any training. This is one training run per model, with one training seed. A second seed and two further evaluation sets have been run and are not on this page yet. Results follow.

The Two Finalists, Head to Head

After eleven rounds, the two models with the lowest mean Fault rate among readable replies under the Guard wording were smollm2:1.7b and qwen3:4b. That mean included families in which fewer than 10 replies could be read, which the written rule leaves out. With every cannot tell family left out, the two lowest are smollm2:1.7b and olmo2:7b, and qwen3:4b has no readable family. The head to head below uses the two models as first chosen and was not re-run with olmo2:7b. The selection rule and the reading rule were committed on 27 October, before any round was read. The scoring rules that read replies were amended afterwards in dated addenda, as the note under the table says. Both finalists then answered the same two fresh question sets, rounds 12 and 13, in the same arms at temperature 0.
