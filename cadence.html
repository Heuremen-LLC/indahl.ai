Stated Update Cadence Against Delivered

Government data portals publish, for each dataset, how often it is updated and when it last changed, in the standard data.json. This page holds the stated cadence against the last modified date. A series is on schedule if it changed within twice its stated interval and behind if not. The interval used is at least two days, so a daily series is behind after four days, and a date with no time of day is read as the end of that day. Records that share a landing page are one series, because archived releases cannot change. The rules were committed before any portal was read, except that the series rule was decided after the first portal read and before any outcome was shown (plan, addendum 1).

The modified date is what the publisher reports. On a sample of each portal's download links, the file's own date is compared with it and shown. Series with a cadence that is not a regular interval, or with no readable date, are counted as cannot tell. No ranking of agencies.

Plan, code and trend history for this page are public: cadence. Every plan was committed before the data it describes.

Data read from: CMS data.json · CDC data.json · HealthData.gov data.json · Veterans Affairs data.json · Energy data.json · Labor data.json · HealthCare.gov data.json · BLS data.json. Each portal is read once in 24 hours. FEMA’s data.json could not be read on the first pass, so FEMA is not in the table.
