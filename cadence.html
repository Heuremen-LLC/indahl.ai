Stated Update Cadence Against Delivered

Government data portals publish, for each dataset, how often it is updated and when it last changed, in the standard data.json. This page holds the stated cadence against the last modified date. A series is on schedule if it changed within twice its stated interval and behind if not. Records that share a landing page are one series, because archived releases cannot change.
