Division: HERMES

The version we read against the version that stands now

QIA and IRIS record the arXiv version of every study they index. Authors revise. For every indexed arXiv study this page shows whether a later version exists and whether the revision changed what we read: the numbers in the abstract, the title or wording, or the standing of the paper. Each study is read again every seven days. The plan was committed before the first comparison.

Nothing here says a revision is better or worse. A study whose abstract numbers changed gets a recount, read in full, before any row of ours changes. No claim is tested, so there is no verdict column.

Named here and want to answer a row? Respond

Unchanged: the version we read is the newest. Revised, nothing changed: a later version exists with the same title, wording and abstract numbers. Revised wording: same numbers, different title or abstract. Revised numbers: the numbers in the abstract differ. Withdrawn: the paper itself. Source: the arXiv API.

Plan, code and trend history for this page are public: arxivdrift. Every plan was committed before the data it describes.
