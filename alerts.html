Email Updates

One form for every email from indahl.ai. The same form sits at the foot of the site’s pages.

The weekly newsletter comes every Tuesday and starts at once. New desks and new data sends one email when a new desk goes live or a desk publishes its first value under its change rule. Outcome changes sends one email when an outcome changes on a desk you follow. A change is what the desk’s ledger rule counts as one: the outcome moves, for example from consistent to above, or a new value passes the rule the desk states. Moves inside the printed interval stay on the page and send nothing. Several changes found in one pass come in one email.

For every box but the newsletter, we email a confirmation link first and send nothing else until you open it. Every email has a link to unsubscribe, and every alert has a link to change what you get. Each desk’s changes are also on its own feed, for example /airq/changes.xml, and each brand page offers the desks with rows about that organisation.
