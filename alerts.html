Email Updates

One form for every email from indahl.ai. The same form sits at the foot of the site’s pages.

The weekly newsletter comes every Tuesday and starts at once. New desks and new data sends one email when a new desk goes live, when a desk publishes its first value under its change rule, and when a release adds an entry to the changelog.

Desks marked outcome changes send one email when an outcome changes. A change is what the desk’s ledger rule counts as one: the outcome moves, for example from consistent to above, or a new value passes the rule the desk states. Moves inside the printed interval stay on the page and send nothing. Desks and pages marked updates send one email when the data the page publishes changes: a row added, a count or an outcome that moved, or a new release that changes what the page says. A re-read that finds nothing new sends nothing, and neither does a change to the site’s layout. Each line says how often we check it, and so how soon an email can follow a change, or that it is sent when we publish a new version. A division box covers the division page and every desk in it.

You can have alerts and updates as they happen, in one daily digest or in one weekly digest on Tuesdays. Several changes found together come in one email.

For every box but the newsletter, we email a confirmation link first and send nothing else until you open it. Every email has a link to unsubscribe, and every alert has a link to change what you get. Each outcome desk’s changes are also on its own feed, for example /airq/changes.xml, and each brand page offers the rows about that organisation.
