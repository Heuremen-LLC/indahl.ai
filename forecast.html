Stated Chance of Precipitation Against What Happened

The National Weather Service states a chance of precipitation for each 12 hour period. For 30 major airports, each forecast is stored when it is fetched, and each period is scored after it ends against that airport's hourly observations: did precipitation occur or not. If a stated 30 percent holds, about 30 percent of such periods should have it. Rules were committed before the first forecast was stored.
