SUPPORT · Stated Support Against Releases

Division: ATLAS

Is a cycle that is stated as supported still being released?

Vendors state how long each release cycle of a product is supported. This page takes those stated dates from endoflife.date, which collects them from the vendors, and holds them against the date of each cycle's latest release. A supported cycle counts as silent when it was released more than 400 days ago and its latest release is more than a year old. Silent is not abandoned: a vendor can patch through a channel the release date does not capture. The page counts what the record shows.

The first row is the test whose rules were committed before the first read. Its interval treats whole products as the independent units, because the cycles of one product are not independent. The two product-level rows were defined after the first read and are labelled as such. The plan and its two dated addenda are in the public repository.

Named here and want to answer a row? Respond

Supported: the stated end of life is in the future or not stated, and the cycle is more than 400 days old. Cycles with no latest release date, an end of life stated only as passed, or a support end that has passed with no end of life are counted apart and not tested. The dates are as endoflife.date gives them and are not checked against the vendors.

Plan, code and trend history for this page are public: support. Every plan was committed before the data it describes.
