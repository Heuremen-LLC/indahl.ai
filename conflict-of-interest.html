Conflict of Interest Certification

Version 26 October 2026

Every employee of Heuremen LLC signs this certification when they join and again each year. Signed copies are kept with the company records.

I certify that

  1. I have no business interest in quantum computing or in large language models that could pose a conflict of interest with my work at Heuremen LLC. This includes shares, options or other securities in a company in either field, paid advisory or consulting roles, and any agreement to receive payment from such a company.
  2. I have no business interest in any other area of Heuremen LLC’s work that could pose a conflict of interest. This includes any company, institution or study listed on Heuremen LLC’s websites.
  3. I will not buy or sell securities of any company named in Heuremen LLC’s published work.
  4. If any of this changes, I will tell Heuremen LLC in writing within 10 days, before doing further work on the affected matter.

Interests disclosed

Each signer lists any interest they disclose, or writes “None”.
