Security

Our data

Our internal records are confidential client information and a private book of work we have not published. Every index entry and calibration we do publish appears on this website exactly as we store it.

Reporting an issue

If you believe you have found a security issue in this site or in materials we publish, use the inquiry form and choose “Security report” as the reason. Please give us reasonable time to fix it before disclosing it, and do not access data that is not yours. We reply to every security report, acknowledge it and keep you informed.

Client access and data

  • We use the minimum access an engagement needs, preferably accounts and credentials that you own and can revoke.
  • Credentials are never written into code, reports or published materials.
  • Client data is kept separate from our published research and is returned or deleted on request when the engagement ends.
