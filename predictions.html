Predictions against their deadline

NOW keeps a log of public predictions that name an event and a deadline. Each entry holds the exact words, a link to where they were said, an archived copy where one could be made, the date said, the deadline and a criterion we wrote in plain words. At the deadline we read the record and the entry reads right, wrong or cannot tell. A stated probability is kept as stated and read with others from the same source, by bin. The log only grows. A correction is a new row, and the old row stays. Every row carries the hash of the row before it, and each version of the log is stamped with OpenTimestamps. The rules were committed before any entry was collected.

The log holds 668 entries. 484 have a reading. 178 wait for their deadline. 6 are statements of a probability of catastrophe from AI, which read cannot tell by design. The entries are those we found. They are not a sample of predictions in general, and no share on this page describes predictions in general.

We read every yes-or-no market in its AI topic with at least 50 traders that closed or resolved from 1 January 2025 to 31 October 2026 and resolved yes or no. The stated chance is the market probability 30 days before it closed. The market's own resolution is the record. 441 markets were read. Each bin reads above when the share that resolved yes is higher than the mean stated chance by more than its 95 percent Wilson interval, below when lower, consistent when the stated chance sits inside the interval, and cannot tell under 30 markets.
